# Payments Guidelines Query Expert

Current submission narrative, revised 4 October 2026. This supersedes earlier midterm product framing and roadmap descriptions.

**Purpose:** Help bank and fintech product, operations and compliance teams ask payments questions, read cited answers and inspect the source guideline.

**Initial corpus:** NPCI circulars across multiple payment products. Preliminary evaluation is UPI-focused. Broader payments coverage is an intended extension, not an implemented claim.

**Three capabilities:** cited questions and answers; guideline search and inspection; summaries and comparisons of selected documents.

**Boundary:** a local research assistant. Compliance decisions, automatic determination of effective requirements and cross-functional workflow orchestration are outside this submission.

## One roadmap

The local prototype is implemented. Indicative windows below begin after the midterm and depend on access to reviewers.

| When | Work | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Independently review the 50 cases and freeze unseen questions | Reviewed test set and answer-quality scorecard |
| Weeks 3–5 | Repair source and answer failures, shorten long summaries, compare retrieval settings | Tested revision and quality/latency comparison |
| Weeks 6–8 | Observe payments users completing research tasks; package setup and final demo | User-task results and repeatable local demonstration |

Assess answer correctness, citation support, omissions and appropriate abstention separately from retrieval coverage. A second payments source is an optional later extension after validation, not a condition of completing the core capstone. Cloud hosting, extra models and fine-tuning are not commitments.

## Evidence boundaries

The saved four-way comparison used 50 AI-assisted UPI cases, awaiting independent review. All methods found 55/55 expected anchors. BM25 returned no evidence in 6/6 negatives; semantic methods did so in 5/6. Retrieval coverage does not establish answer correctness. No reranking coverage gain was demonstrated on this set. The 15 summary/comparison routes bypass reranking; timings are preliminary observations.

The revised slides, report, narration and frontend use this scope. The registered title and actual team contributions must be confirmed by the team.
