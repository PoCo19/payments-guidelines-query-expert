# RBI payments source map

Research checked: **9 October 2026**. Purpose: select an RBI expansion corpus for a payments research assistant alongside NPCI documents. This is a source-selection map, not a complete catalogue or a legal-status opinion. Only RBI-hosted sources support the findings below. The eight sources in **Direct payments core** have now been acquired as official HTML and converted to Markdown for the 9 October intake. PDF downloads were unavailable. Other sections remain a research map, not implemented coverage. See [the integration record](RBI_CORE_INTEGRATION_20261009.md).

## Selection and status rules

- **P0**: foundational or directly applicable to common payments questions; ingest first.
- **P1**: add for the relevant regulated entity, product or research scope.
- **P2**: specialist expansion or historical provenance.
- “Body checked” means the official document text was accessible in this research pass. “Header checked” means title/version metadata was accessible, but substantive PDF/body review remains necessary.
- Keep `document_type`, `issuing_department`, `regulated_entities`, `product_scope`, `issue_date`, `updated_as_of`, `effective_from`, `transition_deadline`, `source_url`, `retrieved_at`, and paragraph-level relationships separate. A website footer date is not a document amendment date.
- Use the [RBI Master Directions index](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasterDirections.aspx) as the discovery starting point. RBI explains that Master Directions consolidate subject instructions and changes are communicated through circulars/press releases and reflected in updated directions. Preserve both consolidated versions and their amendment/repeal provenance.

## Boundaries and next acquisition pass

1. Prioritise the P0 consolidated documents, their explicit dependencies, and relevant P1 entity variants. Do not ingest the entire RBI catalogue just because an entity also makes payments.
2. Keep general lending, capital adequacy, provisioning, treasury, monetary policy, currency management, securities settlement and unrelated FEMA material outside the initial retail-payments scope. Add cross-border FEMA/MTSS/RDA material only for questions that require it.
3. Treat Acts/Regulations, operative directions, amendment circulars, FAQs, drafts, speeches and annual reports as distinct authority classes. Drafts and speeches are not operative requirements. This pass did not acquire or validate the underlying Acts/Regulations.
4. Create explicit `amends`, `supersedes`, `repeals`, `savings_exception`, `incorporated_in`, and `references` edges with paragraph evidence. A newer date alone is insufficient to infer repeal; applicability can differ by entity and provision.
5. A current PPI page still links some older authorisation circulars that the June 2026 authorisation MD explicitly repeals. Preserve both pieces of evidence and resolve that relationship before answering; simple hyperlink-following is not enough.
6. Acquisition should additionally check the full payment-notification archive for changes after each consolidation and unlisted operational topics (NEFT/RTGS procedure, DMT, accessibility, offline payments, AePS, cross-border links, card-network arrangements). Those topics are discovery work outstanding, not an asserted exhaustive gap list.

## Access limitations observed

- Most core MD pages exposed readable official HTML. Several newer card/KYC/conduct pages returned only title/version metadata to the web reader. Treat these as discovered records with **body acquisition pending**; do not let empty extraction pass ingestion QA.
- Some `NotificationUser.aspx`/FAQ/index requests produced tool `Internal Error`. Following the current MD's embedded link recovered the official PSO outsourcing text at `FS_Notification.aspx?Id=12136&Mode=0&fn=9`. This is a retrieval failure, not proof of withdrawal.
- Search results included old official `systemhealth.rbi.org.in` snapshots and non-official mirrors. Canonical live `www.rbi.org.in` pages were preferred for status. Third-party mirrors were not used as authority in this map.
- This pass verified source pages and selected text, not downloaded PDF bytes, completeness of annexures, hashes, OCR quality, link persistence, or exhaustive amendment status. Record these separately when the acquisition pipeline is built.
