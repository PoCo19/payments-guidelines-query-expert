This is in reference to our Operating Circular (0C) No. 328 dated 6th March, 2019, (copy attached),
wherein we have communicated to NFs members on the Network to Network connectivity
arrangement with Royal Monetary Authority (RMA) of Bhutan. The scope of the arrangement
included, is as follows:
Phase 1:Acceptance of RuPay cards issued in India at merchant locations and ATMs of RMA
memberBanksin Bhutan.
Phase 2:Issuance of RuPay cards by member banks of RMA and acceptance of these cards at
merchant locations and ATMs in India.
The above proposal has already been approved in the NFS Steering Committee meeting held on
11thApril, 2018.
Phase 1i.e.acceptance of Rupay cards on RMA's ATM Network in Bhutan, is already made live in
NFSATMnetworkfrom12thMarch,2019.
We are nowpleased to inform that Phase 2i.e. Issuance of RuPay cards by member banks of RMA
and acceptance of these cards at ATMs in India, will be made live from 3rd November, 2o20.
