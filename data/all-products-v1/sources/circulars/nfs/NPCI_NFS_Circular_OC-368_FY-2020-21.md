Sub: NFS ATM - Discontinuation of Spool (STL) reports & changes in adiustment report nomenclature.
NPCl provides members as part of daily settlement various reports for reconciliation & settlement at their end
such as Raw data, DSR, Spool (sTL), verif & veraf files and VerifReversalTrans. From the reports mentioned
above, Raw data contains the complete list of transactions for a particular settlement both as an Issuer &
Acquirer. Members uses Raw data for reconciliation purpose at their end. Similarly Spool files contains the
summary files for a particular settlement processed at NpCl. Since, Raw data contains all the details of a
particular settlement and members uses it for reconciliation purpose, providing Spool files may not be required.
In view of the above spool files (sTL reports) both as an Acquirer & Issuer which was provided to members as
part of daily settlement shall be discontinued eff 1st Jul, 2020. This shall be applicable to all ATM products
domestic/international such as NFS, ICD, JCBUPI & DFS. The product wise detailed list of files with the file format
and location is given in Table A below for reference:

We would also like to highlight that, Adjustment report format will get changed as mentioned in Oc-366.
Annexure B was given with Oc-366 specifying the new format of Adjustment report. In addition to the file
format of the Adjustment report, we have also tried to modify the nomenclature of the adjustments. This shall
help in giving more clarity in understanding the type of adjustment raised/received by the members. We would
also like to inform that earlier in DMs for Deemed acceptance cases there were no identifier in the adjustment
report. Once we migrate to Bcs, the deemed cases shall also reflect in the adjustment report. The product wise
list adjustment nomenclature is given in separate annexure below. The list contains the nomenclature for
