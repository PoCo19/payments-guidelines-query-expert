among members by posting MNSB settlement files in RBI RTGS application for various online
productssuchasNFS,IMPS,UPl,AePS,RuPayPoS,etc.

customers andbanksfrom7:OOamonwardsi.e.SOD (start of day)ofNG-RTGSapplication shall

banksto dotheneedful attheirendtoaccommodatethis change.
