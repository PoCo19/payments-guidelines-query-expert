Subject: ATMs - Dispensing of Old High Denomination notes - closure of ATMs 
 
Madam/Dear Sir, 
 
As per Gazette Notification issued by Government of India on Nov 08, 2016, the legal tender 
character of the existing and any older series banknotes in the denominations of Rs 500 and Rs 
1000 stands withdrawn with effect from November 08, 2016. 
 
In view thereof it has been decided that all ATM / Cash Recyclers / Cash Deposit machines 
services shall remain closed and the National Financial Switch will be switched off from the mid 
night of Nov 08, 2016 so that no ATM will dispense the withdrawn old higher denomination 
banknotes (OHD). We hereby also advise banks and WLAO's to switch off their respective 
switch. The ATM / Cash Recyclers / Cash Deposit machines may be allowed to resume 
functioning from Nov 11th,2016 upon their recalibration to dispense valid notes. 
 
You are, therefore, requested to issue necessary directions for the implementation of the 
above. All payment systems other than ATMs shall continue business as usual.
