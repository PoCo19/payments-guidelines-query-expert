Subject:WhiteLabelATMOperatorsonNFSNetwork
NFS,as the largest ATM network in the country, has on-boarded four (4)White Label ATM
Operators (WLAO) on the network.WhiteLabelATMs (WLA)are the ATMs operated and
managed by non-banking entities. Customer of any member bank can transact on ATMs of
WLAO. As of date, the following WLAO are connected to NFS network:
Sr. No. WLAOName Brand Name
TataCommunicationsPaymentSolutionsLtd Indicash
PrizmPayments Money Spot
MuthootFinance MuthootATM
BTIPayments India1ATM
The above details have already been shared by the NFS Operations team with all the members.
It has been highlighted by WLAOs that customers are facing difficulty while lodging complaint
with their respective bank as front end teams are not aware of White Label ATMs. Hence, all
members are requested to circulate this information with their customerquery handling teams
including customer care, branch banking etc. so that customer complaints are duly registered
