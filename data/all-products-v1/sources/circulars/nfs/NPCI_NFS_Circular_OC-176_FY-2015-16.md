We refer RBI Circular No. RBI/2015-16/168 DPSS (CO) RTGS No. 492 / 04.04.002/ 2015-16 dated 1st
September, 2015 on changes in RTGS time window. As advised therein, RTGS will not be operated on
secondandfourthSaturdays from1stSeptember,2015.
We wish to inform you that with effect from September, 2015, NFS and AEPS settlement entries shall not
be posted in RTGS on second and fourth Saturdays (This is in addition to Sundays and RTGS holidays
published by RBl). For Sundays, RTGS holidays and for second and fourth Saturdays, the settlement shall
bedoneonthenext workingday.
All members of NFS and AEPS are requested to take note of the above mentioned settlement holidays
anddisseminatetheinformation tothe officials concerned.
