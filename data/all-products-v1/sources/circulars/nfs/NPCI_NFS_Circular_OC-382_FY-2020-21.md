Sub: Adherence to NFS Operating and Settlement Guidelines (NFS-OSG)
We refer to the NFS Operating and Settlement Guidelines (NFS-OSG) version 2.2 dated October 01,
2018, Section 2.6 - on conducting audit of NFS related systems. It also states that each member should
conduct annual internal audits pertaining to systems and processes of itself and its processing agents to
comply with NFS-OSG. Extract of Section 2.6 of NFS-OSG is given in Annexure A for reference.
During the last audit, RBl has advised NPCI to ensure compliance by member banks on conducting
annual internal audit as mentioned in the NFS-OSG.
