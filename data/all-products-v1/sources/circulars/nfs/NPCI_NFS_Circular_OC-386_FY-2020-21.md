We refertotheRBIcircularRBI/2020-21/70DPSS (CO)RTGSNo.750/04.016/2020-21dated
December 04,2020 wherein RBl informed that RTGS services shall be made available
24X7,365 days w.e.f.December 14,2020(Please referSection -VPayments and
Settlement Systempointnumber17fordetails)
In view of the above, we wish to submit that NPCl has communicated the aforesaid proposal
and modus operandi to member banks as part of the Working Group Meeting (WGM)
convened on the 16th of December, 2020. We shall hence be going live with settlement for
seven days aweek forAePS, NFS&RuPaywith effect from17th of January2021.
NPCl is also evaluating to increase thenumber of settlement cycles for each of these products
in due course, and the same shall becommunicated to member banks separately.As detailed
in the Working group Meeting, Banks may work on necessary provisions to accommodate the
changeinpipeline.
Settlement for AePS, RuPay &NFS shall be processed through RTGS on all 365 days except
specified holidays as and when decided by the RBl from time to time. Any changes tothe
memberbanksthroughemail&backoffice (BcS)notifications.
*Please note that existing settlement cycles and cut-offs remain unchanged.
