Sub: NFS - Migration to Dynamic Key Exchange (DKE) before 31st December 2020.
We refer to our Operating Circular (OC) No.264 dated 22nd september, 2017 and OC No. 369
dated 1gth May, 2020 advising all NFS members to migrate from Static Key Exchange (SKE) to
Dynamic Key Exchange (DKE).
DKE being a security enhancement measure for online NFS transactions, it is important for all
NFs mermbers to implement it at the earliest.
While most of the commercial banks and many co-op. banks have already migrated to DKE,
there are members who are yet to start the process for DKE implementation.
In view of the above, we reiterate to NFs members who are yet to implement DKE, to do the
required changes at the switch end, and submit the necessary documents for production
movement under cluster model approach OR start certification (as the case may be), at the
earliest but not later than 3oth September, 2020.
All NFS member banks need to mandatorily implement DKE by 31st December, 2020.
be forced to take steps necessary for ensuring compliance.
NPCl shall provide all the support necessary for members to implement DKE at the earliest.
