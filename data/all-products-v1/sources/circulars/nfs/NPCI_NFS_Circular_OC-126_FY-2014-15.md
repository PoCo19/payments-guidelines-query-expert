Cashretractionstatus:
NPCI collates the details from members and submit a monthly report to RBI on the progress of the cash retraction
disablementactivity.
Further to various operating circulars issued by NPCI on cash retraction, banks have reported that 99.93% ATMs
havebeendisabled oncashretraction.
For the balance ATMs cash retraction is pending due to hardware and software issues and member banks have
confirmed that such ATMs will be replaced in a phased manner.We would request the members to complete the
process of replacing such ATMs so that the banks are not exposed to the frauds related to cash retraction.
