Subject: NFS ATM Network-EMV migration of ATMs and Micro-ATMs
We refer to Reserve Bank of India (RBl) circular dated 26th May,2016 on enabling EMV chip cards
acceptance and processing at ATMs and Micro-ATMs by 3oth September,2017. We also refer to NFS
circular number OC 306 dated 10th September, 2018 and NFS circular number OC 317 dated 24th
December, 2018, whereby acquirers were advised to implement EMV at ail ATMs and micro-ATMs as
EMV liability shift would be made effective from 1st January,2019 onwards.
We observe that while most of the transactions routed through NFs network are now initiated from EMV
enabled terminais, a few member acquirers have not yet implemented EMV on all their terminals and are
routing all the transactions from such non-EMV terminals as magstripe transactions i.e. DE 22 value with
first two digits as '02' or g0' in online message.
Members would appreciate that it is important to secure the card based transactions routed through NFs
and that considerable time has been elapsed since the RBl mandate. Thus, members are advised to
expedite migration of all their ATMs and Micro-ATMs to EMV for enabling the acceptance of card plus
PIN transactions.
Please be informed that from 1st April, 2022, NPCl shall start declining all magstripe transactions from
non-EMV ATMs and non-EMV Micro-ATMs i.e. all magstripe transactions shall be declined at NFS,
expect for Non-EMV chip prepaid cards that are confirmed by member issuers (Reference NFs circular
number383).
