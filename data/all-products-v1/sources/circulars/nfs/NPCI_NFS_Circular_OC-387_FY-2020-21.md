Sub: NFS -Mandatory migration to Dynamic Key Exchange (DKE)
We refer to Operating Circular (OC) No.379 dated 16th October, 2020, advising all NFS members
to mandatorily migrate from Static Key to Dynamic Key Exchange (DKE) latest by 31 st December,
2o20. It also was informed that NPCl shall be forced to decline transactions of member bank who
compliance.
We would like to inform that most of the member banks have already migrated to DKE or have
initiated the process but are yet to complete the activity. These banks have requested NPCl to
consider giving some more time to implement DKE.
Considering the request of these members, we wouid like to inform that the timelines for NFs
members to migrate to DKE has been one time extended up to 31st January, 2021. Members are
now requested to complete the activity and mandatorily migrate to DKE within this time.
NPCl shall be forced to decline the transactions of the member bank who do not impiement DKE
by the specified timelines, due to non-compliance.
NPCl shail provide all the support necessary to implement DKE at the earliest. Members can
contact following officiais for any further information and support required for migrating to DKE.
