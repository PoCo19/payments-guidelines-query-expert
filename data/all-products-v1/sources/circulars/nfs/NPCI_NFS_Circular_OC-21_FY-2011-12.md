As per the Standard Operating practice, member banks are required to inform NFS whenever a DR
Drill is to be conducted for their switching operation. DR Drills are also required to be commenced
during odd hours (after 11.o0 p.m.) and restoration to PR site should also take place during the odd
hours.
But we observe that members have not been adhering to this instruction. On many occasions, DR
Drills are carried out without adequate preparation as a result of which large volume of transactions
are getting declined with the reason"lssuing processor not available",
We request the following :
(2) The Drll should commence preferably at night (after 11.00 p.m.) and restoration to the PR
site should take place well before O6.00 a.m. Ifa bank would like to use the DR site for full
24 hours, it is most welcome and restoration may take place only after 11.o0 p.m.
Restoration to PR during the busy period (12.00 noon to 09.00 p.m.) should be strictly
avoided.
(3) Scheduled maintenance activities should also be carried out only at night (after 11.00 p.m.)
We request you to kindly acknowledge receipt of the Circular and intimate to us as to whether you
observeDRDrill onaquarterly,half-yearlyoryearlybasis.
