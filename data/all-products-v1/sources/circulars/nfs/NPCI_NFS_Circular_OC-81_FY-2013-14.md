Dear Sir/Madam,
Ref: Compliance with respect to Disaster Recovery (DR) drills on NFS infrastructure
This is with reference to our communication vide Operating Circular (Oc) No: 21 dated January 05,
2O11 on the subject "BCP and DR drill for ATM switches under NFS Network". We would like to
inform that as per the standard guidelines, DR drill for the bank's complete ATM infrastructure
needs to be carried out at least once in every six months (half-yearly as per financial year).
In order to achieve compliance the DR drill needs to be mandatorily carried out as per set guidelines.
As part of compliance, NpCl would be tracking and submitting the list of compliant and non-
compliant banks with respect to adherence of DR drills to the Regulators in the months of April and
October forthepreceding sixmonths period.
We request Member Banks to adhere to the following:
1. All scheduled maintenance activities to be carried out only after Oo:oo Hrs. Banks should
avoid any maintenance activity during the high volume weeks of the month i.e. first and last
week. In other words, any scheduled maintenance activity should be carried out in 2nd/3rd
week of any month between O0:00 Hrs and 06:00 Hrs.
2. DR drill and the rollback to PR should be carried out during the night time between 0O:00
Hrs to 06:0o Hrs so that the customer impact is minimised.
3. Share the DR drill plan with NFS team at least a week in advance so that we can make
suitable arrangement at our end.
Please inform us the date of DR drill activity carried out during the period October - March, 2013. In
case the DR drill is yet to be carried out send us a tentative schedule. You may kindly contact Shri
Pankaj Samarth - NFS Operations (e-mail: Pankaj.samarth@npci.org.in / Tel: 8108122861) for
sharing the DR drill details or the scheduled plan.
We request you to acknowledge receipt of this Circular.
