# Circular 359 - Cyber Security controls for Third party ATM Switch Application Service Providers

Date: 31st March, 2020

AllMembersofRuPay&NFSATMNetwork
Madam/ DearSir,
Sub: Cyber Security controls for Third party ATM Switch Application Service Providers
Werefer toRBI CircularNo.DoS.CO/CSITE/BC.4084/31.01.015/2019-20 dated
31 st December, 2019 (copy attached for reference).
RBl vide the said circular has advised RBi Regulated Entities (RREs) who manage their ATM
Providers (ASPs) to ensure that the contract agreement signed between RREs and the third
party ATM Switch ASP shall necessarily mandate the AsPs to comply with the cyber security
controls given in the Annexure of the said RBl circular on an ongoing basis and to provide
access to the RBl for on-siteloff-site supervision. The contract agreements are to be amended
at the earliest or at the time of renewal, in any case not later than 31st March, 2020.
We reguest you to make note of the above and disseminate the instructions contained in the
said RBl circular to the officials concerned of your Bank and to your respective Application
Service Providers (ASPs), wherever applicable.
NPCl will add the cyber security controls mentioned in the Annexure of the said RBI circular
to the scope of annual AsP audits performed.
