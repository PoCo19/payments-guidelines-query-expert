Dss standards. Of late, we have observed that members are sending clear card numbers while communicating
with NpCi and also with other members. We request members to'ensure that card numbers are masked at all
times while communicating with NPCt and with other members. Example of card masking is given below:
1. For 16 digit card -<6 digit BIN><XXXXXX><Last 4 digits of card> (e.g. 123456XXXXXX7890)
2. For 19 digit card -<6 digit BIN><XXXXXXXXX><Last 4 digits of card> (e.g. 123456XXXXXXXXX7890)
In case of business requirement, if full (clear) card numbers are required to be shared for addressing
disputes/queries, adequate care needs to be taken by members to encrypt the card details. While sharing the
information through e-mail, the data containing full card number should be sent in a compressed password
protected format (zip) and the password should be communicated through separate e-mail or any other
channel.
Members are requested to take a note of the above and ensure compliance in all future correspondences with
NPCIand with other members in the NFS network.
