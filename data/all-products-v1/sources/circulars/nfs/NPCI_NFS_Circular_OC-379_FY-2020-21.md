Sub: NFS - Mandatory migration to Dynamic Key Exchange (DKE) before 31st Dec'20.
We refer to our Operating Circular (OC) No.375 dated 31st August, 2020, advising all NFS
members to mandatorily implement DKE by 31st December, 2020 (copy attached).
DKE being a security enhancement measure for online NFs transactions, it is important for all
NFs members to impiement it at the earliest.
Most of the commercial banks and many co-op. banks have already migrated to DKE. However,
we observe that there are members who are yet to start the process for DKE migration. Some
members have started the process but are yet to complete the activity.
In view of the above, we again request NFS members who have not implemented DKE, to initiate
the process and implement it before 31st Decemeber'2020.
In case, any NFS member bank does not implement DKE by the specified timelines, NPCl shall
be forced to decline transactions of that member bank in NFS ATM network from 1st January 2o21
onwards, due to non-compliance.
NPCI shall provide all the support necessary for members to implement DKE at the earliest.
