Capacity Building of NFS Member
Banks
As you are aware, the volume
of NFS transactions
of all the member
banks has increased considerably
during the last few months.
NFS has touched
all time high of 3.1 million
transactions
in April 2010. Daily
average volume
of transactions
has gone up by 140% since April 2009. On an average presently
NFS is
handling
2.4 million
transactions
per day. With
installation
of a new i550 IBM Server, the transaction
handling capacity has gone up to 10 million a day. Flow of transactions
through
NFS network
is expected
to increase manifold
with breaking of existing bilateral
arrangements
between
banks. Besides, joining
of
new member
banks will also add considerable
volume
of transactions
in the network.
However, with all
this accolades, which worry
us, are the unabated
Technical declines. Ideally technical
declines should be
less than 1%, however,
our monthly
average figure hovers between
3 and 4%.
The member
banks are requested
to take
stock
of their
infrastructure
capabilities
to
support
the
additional
load of transaction
volume.
We
request
our
members
to
check their
network
capacity,
readiness
of the
core
banking
solution
to take
on the
additional
load,
interface
with
Core banking
solution,
switch capacity to process and interface
with
network
and plan your activity
accordingly.
Our
discussions with
few service providers
indicate
that
some of them
are not in a position
to handle the
increased volume.
We would
like t emphasize that banks should also discuss with the Service Providers
in this regard. If banks connect to NFS directly
instead of routing
through
intermediary,
it would
help us
to pinpoint
the problem
areas and check the technical
declines more effectively.
!
Member
banks are urged to review their
position
and share the same with
us. We are in readiness to
extend all technical
support
in case of need.
Assuring you best in class services from NPC!.
Yours faithfully,
Chief Executive Officer
