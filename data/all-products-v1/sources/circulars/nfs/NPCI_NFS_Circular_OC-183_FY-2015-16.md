MigrationfromNFSSpecificationstoRuPaySpecifications
This is in continuation to our earlier Operating Circular numbers 149 and 159, wherein NFS
members wererequested tomigratefrom NFS specificationsto RuPay specifications to bring
standardization in the system.
Of the 43 banks which were originally on NFS specification, 7banks have completed the
banks have started work towards migration to RuPay specifications and have requested to
extend the timelines for migration.The matter was placed in the Steering Committee meeting
held on1gth November2015 and the committee agreed to extend the completion deadline
till 31st March 2016.The list of banks with their status is annexed to this circular.
Therefore, we request and adviseall member banks to initiate the process and complete the
migration toRuPay specification by31st March 2016.Forany queriesorclarifications,please
