Dear Sir/Madam,
Sub: National Payments Excellence Awards - 2018
We are pleased to announce about the National Payments Excellence Awards, 2018 to recognize the
exceptional performance of Members on the various NPCI products, such as, NFS, RuPay, Digital Payments
(IMPS+UPI+BHIM+USSD), CTS, NACH & AePS.
We would like to mention that NPCl has assigned the task of building the framework for the Awards to a
reputed professional consultancy firm under the guidance of independent Jury. The entire process of
finalization of various business/operational parameters, grouping of banks, etc. has been finalized by the
Jury.
The Winners / Runners Up shall be recognized and felicitated with Awards in the User Group Meeting to
be held in the month of March 2019.
Given below are some important points with respect to the 'National Payments Excellence Awards - 2018'
for all the six NPCI products viz. NFS; RuPay, Digital Payments (IMPS+UPI+BHIM+USSD), CTS, NACH & Ae PS.
Banks have been classified under the same group for all the six products. Banks have been grouped based
on the business mix of Deposits and Loans & Advances as of March 2017.
Grouping of Banks:
a. Commercial banks - Public Sector, Private Sector and Foreign Banks have been considered in this
category. Banks have been classified into three categories based on the size of the domestic
business mix of deposit and loans & advances, as follows:
Category A - ( Deposits + Loans & Advances > 4 lakh crores )
Category B - ( Deposits + Loans & Advances => 1.50 lakh crores and <= 4 lakh crores)
Category C - ( Deposits + Loans & Advances < 1.50 lakh crores )
b. Co-operative banks
c. Regional Rural banks
d. Payment Banks
e. Small Finance Banks
f. NBES
g. White Label ATM Operators (WLAOs) -- only for NFS
h. Prepaid Payment Instruments issuers (PPIs) - only for IMPS
i. Merchants
The grouping of commercial banks is given in Annexure "A",

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
The participant must be a member (direct or sub-member) and should be live on the respective product
as on 1st January 2018.
ASSESSMENTPERIOD:
The assessment period data collation for 2018 awards will be from January 2018 to December 2018.
PARAMETERS:
The parameters for determining the Winner / Runner Up in each of above-mentioned categories are given
in the Annexures attached herewith, as follows:
Annexure“B"-NFs
Annexure"c"-RUPAY
Annexure "D" - Digital Payments (IMPS+UPI+BHIM+USSD)
Annexure“E"- CTs
Annexure"F"-- NACH
Annexure"G"-AePs
The sum total of the score in various parameters in each of the groups will determine the Winner and
Runner up in the respective groups and sub-group (wherever applicable). Product-wise-group-wise list of
Awards is given in Annexure "H".
Please note that the Jury may not consider recognizing Winners/Runners Up for any one or more of'the
groups if, in the opinion of the Jury, the volume and/or efficiency ratio is too low and/or negligible to be
recognized for an Award in such groups. The Jury may also recommend a minimum threshold volume for
any Product/s so as to be eligible to be considered for Award/s for such Product/s. If the volumes of banks
in a particular group does not meet the threshold level, in such case, the Jury may recommend
consolidation of points for more than one Product so as to determine Winners for such Products.
Please note importantly that the decision of NPCl and of the Jury will be final and binding. No
correspondence whatsoever will be entertained in this regard.
Thanking you and looking forward to your active participation in the National Payments Exceilence
Awards - 2018.
