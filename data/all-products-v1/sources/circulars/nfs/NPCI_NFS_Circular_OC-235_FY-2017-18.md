DearMadam/Sir,
Aadhaar-based Authentication forCard PresentTransactions
A reference is invited to our circular DPSS.CO.PD.No.892/02.14.003/2016-17 dated September
29, 2016 wherein banks were advised to ensure that all new card present acceptance
infrastructure deployed with effect from January 1, 2017 are enabled for processing payment
transactions using Aadhaar-based biometric authentication also.
2. It has been brought to our notice that the rate of deployment of acceptance infrastructure has
slowed down owing to the mismatch between demand and supply of such Aadhaar-enabled
devices. Therefore, on a review, it has been decided to extend the time for deployment of
Aadhaar-enabled devices till June 30, 2017.However, banks may continue to make necessary
to ensure adherenceto aboveinstructions.
3. Further, it is also clarified that the instructions contained in our circular dated September 29,
2016 are for deployment of new card acceptance infrastructure. As regards enablement of
existing card acceptance infrastructure for processing payment transactions using Aadhaar-
based biometric authentication, the timeline will be advised in due course.
4. This directive is issued under Section 10(2)read with Section 18 of Payment and Settlement
Systems Act 2007 (Act 51 of 2007).
