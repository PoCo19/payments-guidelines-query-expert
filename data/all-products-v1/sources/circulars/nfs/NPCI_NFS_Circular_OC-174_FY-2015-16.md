Mobile Banking Registration and AADHAAR Seeding in National Financial Switch (NFS)
This
is
to
inform
you
that
as
per
RBI's
communication
to
NPCI
vide
letter
number
DPSS.CO.PD.No.921/02.23.001/2014-2015
dated
November
26, 2014, NPCI has been advised to introduce
'Mobile
Banking Registration'
as a new value added service in NFSwhich would enable customers to register
their
mobile
phone
numbers
for
mobile
banking. Also, as per the
latest
RBI circular
DPSS.CO.PD.Mobile
Banking.No.1/02.23.001/2015-16
dated
July 1, 2015, all banks are required
to
provide
options
for
easy
registration for mobile banking services to their customers, through
multiple channels (like ATM, USSD,MPIN
mailers, etc.).
Since this is a regulatory
direction,
all NFS members are required to enable 'Mobile
Banking Registration'
at
their ATMs as well as for their customers at other banks ATMs i.e. member banks are expected to offer this
service both as an issuer and acquirer on NFS. Please note that the aforementioned
service will be applicable
only for cases where the mobile number of the customer is already available with the bank but is not registered
for mobile banking. Customer will not be allowed to update his mobile number through this particular service.
NFSmembers are requested to enable mobile banking registration on or before rs" September, 2015.
It may also please be noted that, NFSSteering Committee
in its meeting held on 1ih
December 2014, besides
approving
mobile
banking registration,
has also accorded its approval
for
introducing
AADHAAR number
seeding on NFSas a new value added service. Hence, it is advisable that along with mobile banking registration,
NFS members also enable AADHAAR number seeding as well. NPCI Relationship
Manager shall be happy to
assist you with technical specification document for this purpose.

regarding the services, kindly contact the following
