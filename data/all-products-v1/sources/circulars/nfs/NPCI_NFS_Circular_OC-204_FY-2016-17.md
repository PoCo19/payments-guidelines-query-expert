Sub:RevisionofServiceTaxratefrom14.50%to15%
Government of India has notified to levy Krishi Kalyan cess of 0.50% with effect from 1st June, 2016 on all the
services where service tax is chargeable. Hence, from 1st June, 2016 onwards the service tax will be levied at
the revised rate of 15% (including cess) instead of 14.50%, wherever applicable. The details of fees and
service tax would be available in the daily settlement report pertaining to the respective verticals i.e. NFs,
IMPS, UPI, RuPay (RGCS / IRGCS), AePS and DFS (ATM Acquiring).
Please note that for chargeback, representment, etc. (except for debit adjustment) for transactions done on
or before 31st May, 2016, the old service tax rate of 14.50% will be applicable.
