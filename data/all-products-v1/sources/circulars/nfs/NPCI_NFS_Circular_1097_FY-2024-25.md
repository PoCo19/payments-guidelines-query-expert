Sub: Initiation of Self-Attestation for Q4 FY 2023-24
Dear Sir/Madam,
We refer to the circular no. NPCI/UPIl/OC-99/2020-21 dated 12th January 2021 on adherence to
NPCl guidelines by members/participants and Self-Attestation.
Self-Attestation is a process where members/participants declare themselves as compliant with
NPCl guidelines. The Self-Attestation checklist is based on documents - Procedural Guidelines,
Operating Circulars, Technical Specification Document, etc. Members/Participants shall submit
Self-Attestation through PCOMP portal confirming Compliance. The Self-Attestation activity shall
be on a periodic basis such as annuallylquarterly or as and when required.
We also refer to the Circular no. NPCl/2023-24/PD/005 dated 28th December 2023 or
implementation of New PCOMP (Product Compliance) portal for Self-Attestation for Q1 & Q2 F.Y.
2023-24 for Members/Participants and Circular no. NPCl/2023-24/OC/PD/006 for Q3 F.Y. 2023-
24.
This is to inform you that directives for Q4 of F.Y. 2023-24 is updated on the New PCOMP portal
for Self-Attestation by Members/Participants. Members/Participants must submit the self-
attestation by 30th June 2024 or as per the completion date mentioned for specific directives.
Action for Members/Participants:
1. All Members / Participants to complete the Self-Attestation for F.Y. 23-24 including Q4 by
30th June 2024.
2. Self-Attestation for general compliance for F.Y. 2023-24.
3. For some of directives/circulars Members/Participants need to complete the Self -
Attestation as per specific date mentioned for respective directives.
Please note any non-adherence to completion of Self-Attestation process and non-compliance
observed, will be liable for proper remedial actions and may further lead to penal actions by
Surveillance and Enforcement Team (S&E) as per Circular no NPCl/UPl/OC-99/2020-21
(Annexure B) and Circular no NPCI/UPl/OC-103/2020-21 (Annexure C),
Please make a note of the contents herein and disseminate the information contained herein to
the official concerned.
