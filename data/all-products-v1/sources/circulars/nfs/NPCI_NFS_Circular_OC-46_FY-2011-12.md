DearSir,
OurCurrentA/cNo. andSettlementA/cNo. withtheReserveBankof India

1.The National Payments Corporation of India (herein referred to as the NPCl),has admitted us as member
of the National Financial Switch (NFS),which is an arrangement through which switching and processing of
electronic payments transactions over their network would be allowed and settlements executed under
NPCI'sSettlementGuidelines.
2. Accordingly,we hereby authorize and request you that as and when a settlement instruction is received
by you from the NPCl relating to our transactions in the said National Financial Switch Network, you may,
without reference to us, debit/credit our above Current Account/s with such sums as may be specified by
theNPCl.
3. The settlement instruction for debiting/crediting our Current Account/s with you shall be conclusive
proof of debit/credit of our Current Account/s relating to our transaction in the said National Financial
Switch Network as referred to in paragraph 2 above and it shall not be necessary for us to admit and /or
confirm to the fact of such debit/credit by means of separate advice toyou and/or NPCl.
4.We hereby further unconditionally and irrevocably undertake to arrange for the requisite funds in our
Current Account with Deposit Account Department, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbaito meet the demand of
the NpCi. We shall be bound by this undertaking and shall be liable therefore under all circumstances.
5. The mandate and undertaking shall not be revoked by us except with the prior concurrence of both the
NPCl and the Reserve Bank of India (the RBi) and you may act upon this mandate till such time this authority
is revoked in writing and all actions taken by RBl in pursuance of this mandate shall be absolutely binding on
us,withoutanyriskorresponsibilitytotheRBl.
Thankingyou,
