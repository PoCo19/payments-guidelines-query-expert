Subject: Scheduling of maintenance and DR-drill activities
NPCl products (viz. NFS, IMPS, RUPAY (PoS and e-Com), AEPS, UPI) have seen significant growth in number
of transactions in the recent months. While the surge in the number of digital transactions is gratifying, the
benefit of the surge in volumes will be realised only when the transaction is successful. It has been
inconvenience is caused to the customers.
In order to ensure highest standards of customer service, member banks are therefore advised as under:
1) Maintenance activities - Software patches released by the switch vendor/s must be rigorously
tested in the test environment before applying to the production system. Bank should provide at
least 2-3 days prior intimation for all scheduled activities related to switch, CBS, middleware etc.
which require downtime. Further, these activities should be carried out only during the non-peak
hours i.e. between 00:00 Hrs. to 06:00 Hrs. preferably in the 2nd and 3rd week of the month and also
avoid such activities during festive season. This will help to avoid customer dissatisfaction and non-
availability of services during peak hours.
2) DR-drill activity - plan should be shared with NPCI helpdesk team at least a week in advance so that
suitable arrangements can be made to provide online support. Also, Bank should provide proper IP
and port details for carrying out such activities smoothly.
All members are requested to take a note of the above, and ensure to put in place proper processes as
stated above to ensure compliance to NPCl guidelines.
Kindly disseminate the information contained herein to the officials concerned.
