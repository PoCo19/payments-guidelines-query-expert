Subject: Implementing Daily Settlement for NFS & RuPay International Products on    
All Calendar Days. 
 
As per the existing process, settlements for international transactions in NFS and RuPay are not 
processed on Sundays and the 2nd & 4th Saturdays. In order to strengthen settlement risk 
management and ensure harmonization across domestic and international transactions 
settlements, it has been decided that international transactions shall be settled on all calendar 
days.
