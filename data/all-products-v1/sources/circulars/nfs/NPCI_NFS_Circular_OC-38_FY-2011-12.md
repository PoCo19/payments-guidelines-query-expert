reports offered bythe existing MiS application, including dailyupload/download of Settlement files have

Server:

Repeatedtransactionsreport 10 ServiceTaxreportMonthly
SuspectTransactionsreport 11 MonthlyATM-wiseChargebacksreceivedreport

etc.
We request Member Banks to avail the benefit of the new DMS-Mis System for accessing the settlement
files and other reports. However, with a view to provide sufficient time to Banks to adapt to the new
System, both the systems were allowed to run simultaneously.Memberbanks have now confirmed us
that they are able to download daily files from the new DMs Server.Therefore effective 31st October,
2o11,MiS server will be decommissioned and the related MiS services will be available through DMS
Server.
