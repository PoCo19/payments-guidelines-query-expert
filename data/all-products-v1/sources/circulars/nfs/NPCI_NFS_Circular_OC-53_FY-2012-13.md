SettlementHolidaysforNFS,IMPS,AEPSandAPBSettlementOperations
Please refer toour Operating Circular no.49 dated January19, 2011 regarding operationalization of
the NPCI RTGS facility effective from January24, 2012.The Service is functioning successfully and
daily settlement instructions of NFs(National Financial Switch),IMPS(Interbank Mobile Payment
Service),AEPS(Aadhaar EnabledPayment System)andAPBS(AadhaarPayment BasedSystem),
previously routed throughCCIL, are now processed through the NPCIRTGS gateway.
Settlement operations at NPCI would be performed as pertheNPCIor RTGS working days, subject to
the public holidays on which the settlement will not be performed. During weekends and holidays,
settlement shall be performed on the next"business day". The business day shall be the working day
forbothNPCIandRBIatMumbai.
In respect of NFS, IMPS,AEPS andAPBS SettlementOperations,thefollowing Settlement Holidays
willbeobservedduringtheyear2012:
