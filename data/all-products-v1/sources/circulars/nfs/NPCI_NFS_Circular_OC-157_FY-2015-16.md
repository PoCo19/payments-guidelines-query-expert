Settlement Holidays for NFS and AEPS.
We refer to our NFS operating circular (OC) No. 150 dated 14th January, 2015 pertaining to non-
performance of settlements for NFS, IMPS, DFS (ATM Acquiring) & AEPS for calendar year 2015 as per the
list provided therein.
We wish to inform you that with effect from 25th March 2015, NFS and AEPS settlement entries would be
posted in your RTGS settlement a/c with RBI on all days except on Sundays and RTGS holidays published
by RBl. For Sundays and RTGS holidays, the settlement shall be performed on the next working day.
The list of RTGS holidays on which NFS and AEPS settlement will not be effected for calendar year 2015 is
given below:
Sr. No. Holiday Date Day
Republic Day 26 Jan Monday
Annual Closingofbanks 01 Apr Wednesday
Ramzan Id 18 Jul Saturday
Independence Day 15 Aug Saturday
MahatmaGandhiJayanti 02 0ct Friday
Dassera 22 0ct Thursday
Christmas 25 Dec Friday
All member Banks are requested to take a note of the above Settlement Holidays and notify their
departments concerned accordingly.
