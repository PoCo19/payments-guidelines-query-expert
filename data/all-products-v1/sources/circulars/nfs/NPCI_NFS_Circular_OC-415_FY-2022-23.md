Members should ensure the following:
As Issuer:
To ensure that all 6/8 digit BiNs on which your Bank have issued cards are updated in
NFSBINMasterFile(availableon NFSBCS portal)forrouting ofATMtransactions.
In case, any BIN is not updated in NFS, the details of such BIN should be submitted to
NPCl using the'BINs on-boarding Form'(Refer Annexure A)as per the existing process.
In case, any bank surrenders the existing 6 digit BiN and issues cards on new 8 digit
BiN, request for BIN deletion for the surrendered 6 digit BIN (Refer Annexure B) and
BIN addition request for new 8 digit BIN should be submitted to NPCl. This shall also
apply for cases where Bank start issuing 8 digit BIN cards for the existing 6 digit BIN
and are surrendering part of the 6 digit BiN range to Network/Scheme.
Please note that if the BIN which is surrendered is the settlement BiN at NFS, though
it can be deleted for online transactions, however it shall remain enabled in NFs
backoffice for settlement purpose till the end of dispute life cycle.(Member may
contactNPCIfor informationon settlement BiNforthe Bank,if required)
