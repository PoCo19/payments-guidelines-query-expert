We refer to the discussions in the NFS Steering Committee (SC) meeting held on 18th
September, 2018 in respect of capturing information contained in EJ for ATM transactions.
Members informed that it is not feasible to standardise EJ format for all make of ATMs /banks
Hence it was agreed to capture minimum transaction details in the EJ to bring in clarity about
the status of transaction. NPCl was requested to issue an Operating Circular (OC)
recommending the minimum information to be captured in EJ for ATM cash withdrawal
transactions.
