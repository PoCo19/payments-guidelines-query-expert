Sub: NFS & RuPay - Mandatory implementation of MAcing for card transactions routed
throughNPcl
We refer to our circulars issued to members for implementing MACing for card transactions routed
advising all NFS & RuPay members to mandatorily implement MACing latest by 15th November
2019.
Many members have already implemented MACing for NFS and RuPay transactions. However,
in spite of many follow ups, we observe that there are members who are yet to start the process
for implementing MACing. Some members who started the process, have not yet completed the
activityof enabling MACingon all their ports/BINs.
The criticality of the implementing MACing is to enhance the security of card based transactions
and safeguard the ecosystem. Thus, we again advice all NFs and RuPay members to
mandatorily implement MACing before 3oth September, 2021, as Issuer and Acquirer both.
In case, any member does not implement MACing by the specified timelines, NPCl shall be forced
to take action as deemed necessary for ensuring compliance.
NPCl shall provide all the support necessary for members to implement MACing at the earliest.
Members can contact their respective Relationship Managers of NPCl for more details or any
supportrequired inthisregard.
