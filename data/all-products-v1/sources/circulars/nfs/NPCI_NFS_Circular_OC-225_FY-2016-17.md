Sub:ImplementationofDynamicKeyExchange(DKE)onNFSNetwork
We refer Operating Circular No.149 dated 1st January, 2015 on migration from Static Key Exchange (SKE)
toDynamicKeyExchange (DKE).
Inorderto enhancethe systemsecurity,we recommendtomigrateto DKEfrom the present SKE.
Please note that in the NFS Steering Committee meeting held on 22nd September, 2016, the committee
has accorded itsapprovalfor migration from SKEto DKEmechanism (relevant extracts of theMinutes of
the Meeting attached). It may be noted that it is mandatory for Banks to migrate from NFs Interface
specificationstoRuPayInterfacespecificationstoenableDKE.
PleaseensurethatDKEonNFSnetworkis implementedby31stMar'17.
Further, Banks are requested to ensure thattheycomplete DKEimplementationwell intime soas to avoid
last minute exigencies.
