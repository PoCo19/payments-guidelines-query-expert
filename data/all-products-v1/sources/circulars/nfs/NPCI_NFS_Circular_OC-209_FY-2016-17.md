RE:MigrationfromNFsSpecificationtoRuPaySpecification
We refer to our Operating Circular-183 dated 23 November 2015 on migration of the
respectiveATMinterfacesof BanksfromtheexistingNFS (Base24/NonBase24)specification
toRuPaySpecificationforstandardizationacrossall participants.
The status of migration of all Banks has been provided in the annexure for ready reference.
Out of 43 banks which wereon the NFS specification, 1o banks have completed the
migration to RuPay specification and another 14 banks are under various stages of
migration.*
Further, it maybenoted thatmigration to RuPayspecification is apre-requisite forBanks to
beEMVcompliant.
Werequest thepending Banks as given intheAnnexureto initiatetheprocess and complete
theprojectonpriority,inanycaseby3othSeptember2016.
