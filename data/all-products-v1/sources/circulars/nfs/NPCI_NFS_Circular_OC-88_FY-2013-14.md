Sub: Displaying of NPCI RuPay Stickers by NFS member banks on ATMs / ATM Premises
With reference to NPCI Circular No: NPCI/NFS/oC no.24/2011-12 dated 1oth May'11 on captioned
subject. We conducted a survey recently and found that some of the ATM sites of banks did not display
the RuPay stickers. Member banks are once again requested to complete the exercise of affixing RuPay
Stickers at prominent point in their respective ATMs / ATM Premise. RuPay Stickers should be displayed
at the ATM glass door or any other prominent place, wherever the same is easily visible and creates a
Brand image to customers as per banks existing practice. This exercise has to be completed latest by 31st
July2013.
Please also ensure to replace the torn / tampered / mutilated Stickers with fresh RuPay stickers.
Member Banks are requested to contact the below mentioned NPCI Officials for obtaining the RuPay
stickers,incaseofinsufficientstickersinstock.
