Sub:NFS ATM Network-Changes in dispute guidelines for Interoperable Cash
Deposit (ICD)
We refer to our Oc 220 dated 18th August, 2016 on Settlement &reconciliation procedure
and OC 241 dated 07th February, 2017 Dispute management guidelines for ICD
transactions.
Chargebacks are raised for cases where the cardholder has deposited the cash, however
the deposited amount is not credited to the beneficiary.Acquirers refer to Electronic Journal
(EJ),Switch logs,reconciliation reports,etc.while handling IcD chargebacks.In few
scenarios like media jam, power interruption, etc. it was informed that the denomination and
the number of bills (notes)may not get captured in the EJ if the bills are not passed through
thebill validator.
The process to handle such disputes were discussed during the Operations Working Group
Meeting held on 14th June, 2019 and in the NFS Steering Committee meeting held on 16th
July,2019.It was agreed that following documents should be additionally provided by
Acquirers for cases like media jam, powerinterruption, etc.where denomination and number
ofbillsdepositedarenotcapturedinEJ.
1.Mandatorily two custodian (dual) should visit the machine for clearance.A report
should be prepared & signed by both the custodians giving transaction details and
amountdeposited/found.
2.Bank official's declaration ontheamountof cashdeposited/found.
Additionally,images/CcTV footage of external camera and of hand camera may be
submitted by the acquireron best effort basis.It was also recommended that transaction
details be captured in hand camera images, similar to PiN-hole camera for ATM
transactions.
