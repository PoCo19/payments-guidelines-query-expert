Sub:NFSATM Network-Migration from existing switching solution toBharat switch
NPc's current NFS switching solution is operational for overa decade and operates on legacy platform.
Considering the increasing transaction volumes and future requirements, it has been decided to migrate
NFS members to Bharat switch which is capable of handling larger transaction volumes, has better
scalability to introduce new features & functionalities, and has better throughput & capacity (TPS).
As discussed in recent NFs Steering Committeemeeting held on 11th April, 2018, we are scheduling the
switchmigration for NFS members from May2018and have plan to complete it by end of September
2018.
Theapproachisgivenbelow:
Presently,in NFS ATM Network most of thebanks are live on RuPay specification and few are on
non-RuPayspecifications.
For migrating to Bharat switch, banks needs to beon RuPay specifications.Banks not live on RuPay
specificationsmust mandatorilymigratetoRuPayspecification immediately.
NPCl will identify few banks for sanity testing.Banks will be identified based on different switching
solutions/versions they use and will have mix of small,mid-sized and large banks with respect to
transactionvolumes.
NPCl will migrate the banks in the following order:
Banks having Issuerinterfacesonly.
Banks having Acquirer and Issuer on a single interface.
Banks having Issuer and Acquirer interface separately.
Actionatbank'send:
Member banks will be provided with IP and Port details for connecting to Bharat switch. Banks
needs to whitelist the IP and port details shared by NPCl.
NPCl team will co-ordinate with the Bank's SPoC to open the firewall, initiate connectivity and
migratethebank.
For banks identified forsanity testing-NPCI team will contactbank's SPOCto share IP&portdetails
(forestablishing connectivity), share the test cases and also support banks for completing the sanity
testingformigration.
