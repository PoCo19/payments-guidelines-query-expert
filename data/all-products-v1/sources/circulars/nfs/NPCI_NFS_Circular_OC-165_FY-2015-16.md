NFs Members are currently reporting every month to NPCl, number of ATMs deployed by them along with
thegeographicalclassificationasperOc160.
RBI has instructed NPCI to collate complete ATM location including geographical classifications of the ATMs
deployed by NFs members across the country.This information is required to understand the geographical
dispersion/deployment of ATMs across the country and to plan and strategise deployment of ATMs
throughout the country. NPCI is required to report the details of ATMs deployed by NFS members on a
monthly basis to RBl.
We, therefore, request you to submit complete location details of ATMs deployed as at the end of each
month. The details need to be submitted by the 1oth of the month following in the excel format (refer
Annexure A) by email.We will compile the information submitted by banks/WLAOs and arrange to forward
theconsolidatedreporttoRBl.
Please note that RBl needs the correct and complete information every month. Hence, we will require the
wholehearted support of all banks/WLAOs for timely submission of the information so as to meet RBI's
requirementanddeadline.
