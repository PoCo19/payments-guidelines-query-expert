Sub:NFS,IMpSandAePS-Postingof separatesettlemententriesforeachdayafterholiday/s.
Settlement entries are posted by NPCI for NFS, IMPS and AePS in member banks'RTGS settlement
account on RTGS working days through Multi-Lateral Net Settlement Batch (MNSB)file.A consolidated
settlement entry is passed for Second and Fourth Saturdays, Sundays and RTGS Holidays on the next
working day.
Passing separate settlement entry for each day will help in reducing the risk associated with debiting
consolidated settlement entry. The process of passing separate settlement entry for each day was
approved in NFS and IMPS Steering Committee meeting held in themonthof November,2o15.
Separate settlement entries will be passed for each day after holiday/s in bank's RTGS settlement
accountforNFS,IMPSandAePSfrom21stDecember,2015onwards.
Please make a note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein to the officials
concerned.
