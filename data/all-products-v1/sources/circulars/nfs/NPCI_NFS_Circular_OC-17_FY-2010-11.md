Sub:ValueAddedServicesinIndiaPayNational Financial Switch(NFS)
Currently, the IndianPay National Financial Switch is providing two basic services in the network, viz.
Cash Withdrawal and Balance Inquiry.We observe that most of the member banks have activated
services like PiN Change,mobile top up and Mini Statement etc.in their switchfor their own customers,
but not available across the banks in the NFS network. On a review, it was decided that IndiaPay NFS
should extend the services to its members in stages.The IndiaPay NFS Steering Committee decided that
NFS should offer two value added services to member banks to start with viz. PiN Change & Mini
Statement.
On 2oth August 2010, NPCl made soft launch of these two services with ICICI Bank and Punjab National
Bank. Initially selected branches of both the bank have gone live with the facility.Interchange for these
value added service has been fixed at Rs.8/-plus applicable tax similar to interchange for other non-
financial services. Both the banks have decided that the maximum charges they may recover from the
customers would be to the extent of the interchange i.e.Rs.8/-plus taxes. The switching fee for these
transactions payable to NPCl will be Re.0.5o per successful transaction which is similar to other
transactions.
We request the member banks to take note of these new services with a request to launch this service
