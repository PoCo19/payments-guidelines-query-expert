Dear Sir / Madam,
Best practices
codes-NFS
February 10,2010
As you are already
aware
that National
Payments
Corporation
of India
(NPCI)
has taken
over
the
operations
of National
Financial
Switch (NFS) from Institute for Development
and Research
in Banking
Technology
(IDRBT) w.e.f. 14th December 2009. With the concerted efforts of all concerned,
the technical
declines have been brought down significantly.
We endeavour
to bring down technical declines to the bare
minimum which necessitates collaborative
action from all the member banks. Following few best practices
codes may be adopted by our member banks with immediate effect to meet the objectives.
DownTime:
a)
Schedule downtime would be permissible
only between 1-00 am and 6-00 am. Banks are advised
to carry out all planned maintenance
activities only during this time. Please avoid any maintenance
activity during the high volume weeks of the month i.e. first and last week. In other words, any
scheduled maintenance
activity should be carried out in 2nd/3rd week only between 1-00 am and 6-
00 am.
b)
Banks carrying out Scheduled Maintenance
should inform NFS by way of mail at least 24 hours in
advance.
This will enable
NFS to inform
other member
banks or handle
other bank queries
efficiently.
Contact
Details:
Member banks should keep on updating NFS about the Contact details of it's IT and Operations
Wing.
Whenever
any addition
/ deletion
/ modification
in the contact
details
(from Levell
to Level 4) are
effected,
the
same
should
be
immediately
intimated
to
NFS
for
updating
the
records.
(mail
to
nfshelp@npci.org.in,
prsujana@npci.org.in)
ISDN/Backup
Link Testing:
Banks should
undertake
ISDN/Backup
Link testing
as frequently
as possible,
preferably
once a week
during
night time. This will help ensuring
minimum
downtime.
In case of inability
to do so, please
coordinate with NFS Help Desk team at Hyderabad
(nfshclp@npci.org.in,
avsanandCwnpci.org.in).
We would incorporate the above best practices in the next version of NFS Operating Manual.
