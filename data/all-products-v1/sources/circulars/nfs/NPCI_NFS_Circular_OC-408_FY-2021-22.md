We refer to RBI Circular No. RBI/2021-22/40 DPSS.CO.PD.No.S-99/02.14.006/2021-22 dated 19th
May'21, whereby RBI has permitted cash withdrawal from Full-KYC PPls of non-bank PPI issuers.
bank as mentioned in the RBl circular. The non-bank PPl Issuers shall be responsible for these
conditions:
a. Maximum limit of 2,000 per transaction with an overall limit of 10,000 per month per PPl;
b. All cash withdrawal transactions performed using a card/wallet shall be authenticated by an
Additional Factor of Authentication (AFA) / PIN;
c. Any PPl issuer offering this facility shall put in place proper customer redressal mechanisms.
Complaints in this regard shall fall under the ambit of the respective ombudsmen schemes and
instructions on limiting the liability of customers; and
d. PPl issuers shall put in place a suitable cooling period for cash withdrawal upon opening the PPls
or loading / re-loading of funds into PPls to mitigate the risk of fraudulent use of PPls.
Non-bank PPI Issuers shall be on-boarded in NFS as sub-member under the sponsorship model. The
inclusion of on-boarding non-bank PPI Issuers in the NFS network is being updated in the NFS Operating
& Settlement Guidelines (NFS - OSG) in the section - Membership criteria.
Please note existing NFs settlement & dispute management processes including fees, interchange, etc.
shall be applicable for transactions done by PPl holders of such Issuers.
Non-bank PPI Issuers can come for certification with NPCI for enabling ATM transactions in the NFS
