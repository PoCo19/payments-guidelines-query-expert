NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/NFS/OCNO.345/2019-20 Date: 1gthSeptember'19
To:
All Banksparticipatingin various NPCI Products
We are pleased to inform that we have rolled-out the training calendar for October 2019. The
day-wise training program, venue, etc. are given in Annexure I- attached herewith.
We request banks to nominate participants so that they can avail of the training conducted by
NPCl officials. We request banks to send us the nominations well in advance so that proper
arrangements can bemade forthetrainingprogram.
We submit that depending on the number of participants, the training will be scheduled at
various centres. To make the project viable, a minimum of 25 participants should be available
for a class-room training program. The nominations can be sent on bank.training@npci.org.in in
the attached format-marked Annexurell.
For nominations and all your training needs, you may please contact Ms. Nayan Bhandarkar at
nayan.bhandarkar@npci.org.in
Kindly arrange to disseminate the information contained herein to your team and officials
concerned.
Yoursfaithfully,
