Sub:RevisionofServiceTaxratefrom14%to14.50%
Government of India has notified to levy Swachh Bharat cess of o.50% with effect from 15th November, 2015
on all the services where service tax is chargeable. Hence, from 15th November, 2015 onwards the service tax
will be levied at the revised rate of 14.50% (including cess) instead of 14%, wherever applicable. The details of
fees and service tax would be available in the daily settlement report pertaining to the respective verticals i.e.
NFS, IMPS, RuPay (RGCS/IRGCS), AePS and DFS (ATM Acquiring).
Please note that for chargeback, representment, etc. (except for debit adjustment) for transactions done on
orbefore14th November,2015, the old service tax rateof 14%will beapplicable.
