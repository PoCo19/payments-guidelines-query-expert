Sub: NFS -Migration of“Magstripe Only' cards to EMV Chip cards
We refer to RBI Circular No. RBI/2015-16/163 DPSS.CO.PD.No.448/02.14.003/2015-16 dt. 27th Aug'15
whereby issuing banks were advised that the magnetic stripe debit and credit cards issued would have
to be replaced by 31 st Dec'18, irrespective of the validity period of the card.
We still observe 'magstripe only' card transactions are approved by Issuer banks in NFs ATM network.
The 'magstripe only' card transactions can be identified in the online NFs ATM transaction based on the
card service condition code value received in Data Element (DE) - 35 / 40. 'Magstripe only' cards shall
have the 1st digit of the card service condition code value as '1' OR '5'.
In the NFS Steering Committee (SC) meeting dated 7th Oct'20, it was decided that NPCl may start
declining ATM transactions done by 'magstripe only' cards due to risk associated with it. Members who
are yet to migrate all their 'magstripe only' cards to EMV Chip and Pin cards are requested to complete
the migration at the earliest, to avoid any inconvenience to the cardholders.
Please be informed that NPCl shall start declining ATM transactions done by 'magstripe only' card from
1 st April, 2o21, except for those prepaid card BlNs requested by member banks for exclusion.
In case of prepaid cards, which may be in force for some more time, banks can share the BlNs of those
prepaid cards with NPCI for excluding them from being declined.
