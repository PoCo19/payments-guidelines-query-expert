Sub: NFS - Mandatory implementation of MACing for card transactions routed through NPCl
We refer to our circular No. NPCl/NFS/OC No.401 issued on 2gth June, 2021 as a notice to all members to
mandatorily implement MACing latest by 3oth September, 2021. it was informed that in case, any mermber
does not implement MACing by the specified timelines, NPCl shall be forced to take action as deemed
necessaryforensuring compliance.
The criticality of implementing MACing is to enhance the security of card based transactions and safeguard
the ecosystem.
If any NFS member bank does not implement MACing by 3oth September, 2021, NPCI shall be forced to
decline transactions of that member bank in the NFS ATM network from 1st October, 2o21 onwards, due
to non-compliance.
NPCl shall provide all the support necessary for members to impiement MACing at the earliest.
Members can contact their respective Relationship Managers for more details or any support reguired in
this regard.
