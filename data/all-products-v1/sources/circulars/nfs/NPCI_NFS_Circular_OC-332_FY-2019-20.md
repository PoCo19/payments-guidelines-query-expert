Sub:NFS ATMNetwork-Enablement ofInteroperable CashDeposit (ICD)transactions byWhite
LabelATMOperators (WLAOs)
We refer to RBl circular No. DPSS.CO.OD.No.1916/06.07.011/2018-19 dated 7th Mar'19 allowing
WLAOs to offer ICD transactions, subject to technical feasibility and certification from NPCl.
We are pleased to inform that WLAOs can now enable ICD transaction in NFS ATM Network after
undergoing certificationwithNPCl.
WLAOs should refer to NFS Operating and Settlement Guidelines (NFS-OSG) for settlement and
reconciliation procedure, and for dispute management guidelines for IcD transactions. The change in
the process of handling counterfeit notes where Acquirer is a WLAO is given in Annexure A, which
needstobefollowed byWLAOsandtheirSponsorBanks.
WLAO and their Sponsor Bank need to submit request letter for enabling ICD transactions by the WLAO
as per the format given in Annexure B & C respectively
