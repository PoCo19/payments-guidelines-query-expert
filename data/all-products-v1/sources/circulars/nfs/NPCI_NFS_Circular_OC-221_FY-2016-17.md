Sub: Interoperable Cash Deposit (ICD) on NFS 
 
We are pleased to inform that Interoperable Cash Deposit (ICD) is now live on NFS. 
Interoperable Cash Deposit is the latest service being offered on NFS network. The service is critical 
as it will help bank to contain cash handling cost for ATMs and at the same time help migrating 
customer to electronic channel for depositing cash. The service enables card holder to deposit cash 
using Bulk Note Acceptor (BNA)/ Recyclers of one Bank, in his/ her own account maintained in 
another Bank or any third part account. Since many Banks have already deployed Bulk Note 
Acceptors (BNA)/ Recyclers, interoperability will further help Banks to enhance Return on 
Investment (RoI) as the interchange is higher for this service (refer operating circular no. 219) for 
Interchange fees details). 
Salient features of this service are given below: 
1. Instant verification of notes 
2. Cash deposit by use of debit card for customer identification 
3. Immediate credit to beneficiary’s account 
4. Customer convenience (No need to fill deposit slips/ stand in long queues) 
5. 24/7 availability 
6. Paperless transaction 
7. Better fund management 
8. No need to sort and arrange currency notes denomination wise 
Bank can participate in ICD as: 
a. Acquirer 
- 
Making available their BNAs so that other Bank customers can       
deposit cash 
b. Issuer 
- 
Making available the facility to their customers for depositing cash    
through other Bank BNAs 
c. Beneficiary - 
Making available the facility of receiving funds in their customer 
accounts through other Bank’s card and BNAs
