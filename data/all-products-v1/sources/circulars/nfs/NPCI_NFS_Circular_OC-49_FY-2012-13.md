OperationalizingofNPCIRTGSSystem
Please refer to ourOperating Circular no.46 dated December 8, 2011, informing NFS Member Banks on
NPCIbeing accorded Type-D membershipof the RTGS system.Wearepleasedto informthat all Member
Banks have submitted Letter of Authority to RBl, mandating NPCI to process settlement instructions
throughNPCI'sRTGSgateway.
NPCI's RTGS infrastructure is also ready.We are now in readiness to commission the RTGS facility for doing
thedailyNFSSettlements.RBIDADandCCILhavebeen informedaccordingly.
TheNPCIRTGS facilitywill bemadeoperational fromJanuary24,2012following which,the services of CCIL
will be discontinued and daily settlements will be facilitated through NPCI RTGS System.Further, it has
been decided not to charge any fee from Member Banks for this service. Each Member Bank would save
around Rs.60,o00 peryear by this arrangement apart from eliminating the reconciliation burden.
This arrangement will help NPCl in swift processing of daily settlement files as the steps involved in routing
the files via CCIL will be saved.No updation is required to be made by Member Banks in their settlement
systems and procedures since the current settlementfiles and Reports will continue.
All our Member Banks are requested to take note of the above change of Settlement Agency and inform
their internal departments accordingly.Should you have any clarification to seek, Mr.Shaktishwar Rao (e
