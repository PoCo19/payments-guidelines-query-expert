Sub:Goods and ServicesTax (GST)-Using NPClgenerated GST invoices thru GST
portal.
We refer to our Operating Circular (OC) 269 & 280, dated 05/10/2017 and 28/12/2017,
respectively, issued on generation and sharing of invoices for GsT on interchange & switching
fee through NPCl GsT portal. Receiver of interchange/switching fee (Payee) can generate and
share invoices with the payer of interchange/switching fee (Payer) through NPCI GST portal.
We have received feedback from members as Payer on the difficulty to sight and match the
information in invoices with data in GSTR2A in the GSTIN portal. The invoices transferred in the
various banks in their system have different formats which leads to operational challenges in
retrieving the details in the invoice in a format which can be used for reconciliation purpose.We
are trying to build a solution for capturing the details from the PDF file and providing the invoice
details like GSTN number of Payer & Payee, Taxable amount, CSGT & SGST breakup, etc., in
spread sheet. Since various banks use different formats, we are facing challenges in converting
the pdf to spread-sheet. Hence, we request that members use the invoices generated by NPCl
through GsT portal. (On a similar note, during the meeting between IBA, banks and networks, a
sub-committee was formed for finalising the modalities for implementation of accounting &
invoicing related issues. The sub-committee recommended that members should use GsT
invoicesgeneratedbynetworks.)
We list belowfew of the benefits of using NPCl generated GST invoice:
Invoices generated by NPCI GST portal is based on the monthly GST reports. The GST
monthly reports tallies with the day-wise report and hence it is convenient for
reconciliationwiththerespectiveproductsGLa/c.
Ready PDF invoices for the members with the nomenclature as required for sharing
through NPCI GST Portal and hence sharing of invoices becomes seamless.

<!-- Page 2 -->

NPCl is also in the process of providing the bulk digital signature option. This will enable
digital signing activity through the GsT portal itself. Also, the monthly reports are
provided through GsT portal in addition to the respective settlement system. Hence,
GST portal will provide integrated service for all the GST related activities.
Availabilityof GSTR1&GSTR2reportswilleasereconciliationwithGSTINdata
Reduce effort in follow-ups with members as GsT compliance is made easier.
Members should start using the invoices generated through the NPCI GST portal by Sep., 2019
onwards i.e. for Aug'19 transactions the invoices generated through the NPCl GST portal
should be used in the Sep., 2019.
We would also like to inform that Steering Committee of all the products has already approved
to make good the loss incurred (to the extent of inability to claim ITC amount) by the Payer due
to non-submission of invoices by Payee. The process shall be implemented for the transactions
processed of FY 2018-19. Hence, we request you to comply with the GsT guidelines. The most
convenient option would be to use the NPCl generated invoices through GsT portal, due to the
advantages enumerated above. Members are requested to coordinate with their respective
finance teams on the changes required for using the invoices generated through NPCl GsT
portal.
We would once again remind members who have still not taken access to the Invoice sharing
tool to obtain the access by submiting the duly signed Annexure D of oc 269 dated 5th
October,2017-copyattached.
