Sub: NFs ATM Network-Submission of documents for cases referred to Arbitration.
We refer to Operating Circular (0C) No. 151 dated 22nd January, 2015, detailing the list of documents to be
submittedbyNFS members atvarious stages of disputecycle.
We also refer to 0c No. 164 dated 07th May, 2015, wherein it was reiterated that acquirers need to upload the
documents in Dispute Management System (DMs)within 1o days from the next day of Arbitration raised by
Issuer.Thedocuments shouldbestamped and signedbytheAcquirers.
We observe that some members are not uploading complete set of documents and in few cases the documents
are not stamped and signed by the bank/WLAo officials. It is also observed that few members are uploading
Issuer/ AcquirerSubmissionForms inold format instead oftherevised format.
We once again request members to upload complete set of documents, stamped and signed alongwith the
revised Issuer/Acquirer Submission Form in DMS within the timelines stipulated in OC 151.Revised Issuer/
AcquirerSubmissionFormareattachedherewithforreference.
Non-submission of complete set of documents may result in PRD decision going against the Acquirer on
technical grounds.
Members aretherefore requested totake note ofthe above andensure compliance.
