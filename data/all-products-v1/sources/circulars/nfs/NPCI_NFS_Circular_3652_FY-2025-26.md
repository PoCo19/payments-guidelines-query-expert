Dear Sir/Madam,
We refer to circular no. NPCI/2022-23/OC/PD/001 dated 7th September 2022 and NPCl/2023-
24/PD/005 dated 28th December 2023 on adherence to NPCl guidelines by members/participants and
Self-Attestation. We also refer to the advisory no. NPCl/2025-26/PD/005 dated 16th April 2025 on
submission of Self-Attestation by Internal Audit or Compliance Function of the member/participant.
Self-Attestation is a process where members / participants submit status of compliance with respect
to NPCl guidelines. The Self-Attestation checklist is based on documents such as Procedural
Guidelines, Operating Circulars, Technical Specification Document, etc. Members / participants shall
submit Self-Attestation through NPCl's PCOMP portai. This activity shall be on a periodic basis, such
as annually or as and when reguired.
This is to inform you that directives for Quarter (Q1) F.Y. 2025-26 are updated on the PCOMP portal
for Self-Attestation by members / participants.
