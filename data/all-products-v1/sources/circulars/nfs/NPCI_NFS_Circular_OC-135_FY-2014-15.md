Sub:RuPay-Change infrequencyof Cards PortfolioReport submission bybanks
Banks have been submitting to us, every quarter, the data relating to their RuPay cards portfolio, ON
US transactions data for RuPay cards used on their ATM and POs,etc.in the prescribed format as
per Cards Portfolio Report (QCPR). The report submitted by banks are used by us to compile -
1.RuPaycardsincirculation
2. Usage pattern
3.Totalusage(count/amount)
4.Manageincentiveprogram (whereverapplicable)
In order to facilitate timely consolidation and analysis of the information submitted by banks,we
request you to submit the Cards Portfolio Report every month instead of every quarter. In view of
the change in frequency, the report shall hereafter be known as MonthlyCards Portfolio Report
(MCPR). The Report should be submitted to us on or before the 5th of the month following, The
instruction contained herein is effective from Oct 2o14 onwards. Thus, theMCPR for the month of
Oct2014shouldbesubmittedonorbefore5-11-2014.
In view of the criticality of the data compilation, we seek your support and co-operation for
submissionofMCPRby5thofeachmonth.
