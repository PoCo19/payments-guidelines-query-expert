DearSir/Madam,
Successful completionof NFSDR Drill
We arepleased tosharewith member Banks of NFSthat aDisaster RecoveryDrill of theNFS
Switch and related applications including the recently introduced Interbank Mobile Payment
Systems (IMPS) were carried out and the same has been successfully completed.The
processing of transactions was carried out at our DR centre at Mumbai for over 48 hours
from 3.28 a.m. on 24th June, 2010 up to 4.52 a.m. on 26th June, 2010. Transactions for these
two days were carried out smoothly and without any disruption to the activities of member
banks and the DR system was able to handle the transaction volumes comfortably.
Connection from member banks continued to terminate at PR Centre and the transactions
were routed to the switch at DR Centre instead of the primary switch to conduct this DR
exercise. The downtime was onlyone hour while switching from PR to DR Centre and less
thananhourwhileswitchingbacktoPRfromDRCentre.
This establishes the resiliencyof NFS switching system and related applications as well as
theconnectivitybetweenthePRsetupandDRsetup.
