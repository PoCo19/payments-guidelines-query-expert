Overview:
Since the takeover of National Financial Switch (NFS)from IDRBT, NPCI has taken significant
steps towards process enhancements and technology upgradation. This has contributed
substantially in enhancing the performance of NFS. It would be opportune to state that your
network has grown to 177 member Banks and 120,000+ ATMs as at end May 2013 from 37
Banks and 51,500+ ATMs as at end Dec 2009, when NFS took over the operations from IDRBT.
NPCl aims to be the best managed ATM network in the world in terms of transaction volume, system
uptime, transaction declined ratio, dispute management, compliance etc. In this regard, NPCl has been
operational efficiency in the ATM network and growth in volume. The Business decline, Technical
decline and chargeback ratio have an immense potential for further improvement and we are sure that
the benchmarks set can be achieved working in close coordination with our member banks.
You are aware that NPCl instituted the 'Operational Excellence Awardsin the year 2011 to identify and
reward member banks for their performance on the NFS ATM Network.Member banks were classified
into 3 categories as follows:
1. PublicSectorbanks
2. PrivateSector&MNCbanks
3. Co-Operative banks
NPCl empanelled independent panel of judges to adjudge the Winners & Runners up in each of the
categories.
For the Operational Excellence Awards- 2013, NPCl undertook a detailed study to arrive at the
framework for assessing various parameters of Operational Excellence. These parameters shall be
reckoned for determining the Winners and Runners Up in each of the three categories described above.
