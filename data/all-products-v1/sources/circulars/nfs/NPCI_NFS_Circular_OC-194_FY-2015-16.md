Obiective:
The objective of this circular is to provide NFS members with a list of BINs maintained at NFS
forrouting interbankATMtransactions throughNFSnetwork.
This will help NFS members to verify the following:
a. As an Issuer, whether memberbank's existing BiNs aremaintained at NFS, and
b. As an Acquirer, whether all the BINs as per the list are updated by NFS members at
theirswitch.
Proper updation of BINs by NFS members and at NFS shall prevent declines of ATM
transactions due to non updation of BiN at either end. This will help in avoiding customer
complaints.
WeseekconfirmationfromNFSmemberonthefollowing:
AsIssuer:
Request you to check and confirm that all existing BINs of your bank are updated in NFS
