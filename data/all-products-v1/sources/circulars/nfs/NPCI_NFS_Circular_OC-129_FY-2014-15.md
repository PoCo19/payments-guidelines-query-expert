Subject:SeparateDailySettlementReport(DSR)forsub-members
Daily settlement reports (DsR) of sub-members are provided in the sponsor Bank's settlement report as a
separate section for each of their sub-members. Reconciliation files such as RAw files, STL reports,
verification reports, etc. provided in DMS can be accessed through DMs logins given for sub-members.
We received requests from member banks to provide separate DSR to sub-members through DMs.
We are pleased to inform that separate DSR for sub-members has been made available in DMS effective 23rd
August, 2014. DSR for sub-members can now be downloaded from DMS using logins given for sub-members.
reconciliation purpose. Sponsor banks shall continue to get DsR of their sub-members in the settlement report for accounting and
We request sponsor banks to disseminate this information to their sub-members.
