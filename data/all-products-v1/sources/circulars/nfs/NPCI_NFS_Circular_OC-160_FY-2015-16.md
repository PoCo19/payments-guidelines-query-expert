Sub: Reporting of Geographical Deployment of ATMs in the country.
NFS members (Banks / WLAOs) are currently reporting every month to NPCI the number of ATMs
deployed by them. With the manifold increase in Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
accounts, RBI has sought information on Geographical Deployment of ATMs in the country by NFS
members in order to monitor the developments in deployment of ATM infrastructure so as to
ascertain their ability to meet the growing demand from the newly opened accounts holders. RBl
has sought the information every month as per the format attached.
2. We, therefore, request you to submit the information of total no. of ATMs deployed by you in
the enclosed format (provided by RBl) as on each month end by the 5th of the month following. We
will compile the information submitted by banks/WLAOs and arrange to forward the consolidated
information every month urgently. Hence, we will require the wholehearted support of all
banks/WLAOs for timely submission of the information so as to meet the RBI's requirement and
deadline.
3. RBI has also sought information on no. of ATMs deployed by banks/WLAOs in Rural location, as
follows:
a.TierVlocation-Rural (population 5,000to9,999)
b. Tier Vi location-Rural (population less than 5,ooo)
4.We request you to submit the information on ATMs deployed in Rural locations (paragraph3
above) by April 18, 2015 so that we can consolidated and submit the Report to RBl immediately.
