MaskingofCard Numbers inemail CommunicationsaddressedtoNPC!
With a view to ensure adherence to PCl-Dss (Payment Card Industry-Data Security Standard)
standards,NPCIhascommencedtheimplementedPCi-DSSguidelinesacrossNFSOperations.
PCI DsS requirement -clause 4.2requires strong controls to be in place whenever cardholder data is
sent via end-user messaging technologies (render cardholder data unreadable or secure with strong
cryptography).To meet this PCI DsS requirement, we request all Member Banks, not to transmit
electronically the Card Numbers in clear form to NPCl.Similarly,NPCl will also not share the card
numbersine-mailcorrespondencetoMemberBanks.
While corresponding with NPCl, Member Banks are advised to communicate theCard Numbers in
thefollowingformat (theexampleisgivenfora16digitcard number):
<6digitsBIN><xxxxxx><Last4digitsofthecard>
Forexample:123456xxxxxx3456
Digits of the card number between 7th and 12th digit should be masked in all communications
addressed to NPCl.
In case of business exigency,where transmission of the card numberin plain/clear text
indispensable, adequate care needs to be taken by Member Banks to encrypt the card details.
When using e-mail technologies, Member banks should zip and password protect the cardholder
data and send this password in separate email other than the email containing the zip password
protected file.
Member Banks are requested to follow the above guideline in all future correspondence to NPCl.
