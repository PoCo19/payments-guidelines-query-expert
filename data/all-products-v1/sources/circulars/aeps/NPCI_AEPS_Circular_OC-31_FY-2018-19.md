Dear Sir/Madam,
Subject:NFSMicroATMNetwork-EMVmigrationofMicroATM's
We refer to RBI circular dated 26th May'16 on enabling EMV chip cards acceptance and processing at
ATMs and MicroATMs by 30" Sep'17.
Wewould liketo informyouthat therepresentationmadebyIBAandNPCIforallowingbanks to
rollout EMV onATMs and MicroATMs in phased manner upto 31st Dec.2020 has not been acceded
to by RBl. Once EMV liability shift is made effective, the liability for misuse on non-EMV enabled
MicroATMs shall shift to the Acquirers.
In this regard, please note that:
Members should complete EMV Acquirer certification and ATM Terminal certification (for all
combinations of L1, L2 & MATM application)at the earliest. This shall help members to start EMV
roll out on MATMs. Please also note that EMV liability shift for MicroATM transactions has been
deferred only till 31st Dec'18.
All membersare requestedto take a noteof theabove.
Kindly disseminate the information contained herein to the officials concerned.
