ReleaseofAEPSInterfaceSpecificationversion4.7.1
In the 15th AEPS Steering Committee interim AEPS offus cash deposit transaction flow
wasproposedtomemberbanks,memberbanks agreedtoimplementthe sameas the
proposed solution is beneficial to the banking industry as banks would not be in out of
fund scenario with the implementationof interim cashdeposittransactionflow.
After making relevant changes as per the requirements of the interim AEPS offus cash
deposittransactionflow,NPCIherebyreleasesAEPsInterfaceSpecificationVersion
4.7.1. (Annexure 1).
The major change in interim AEPS offus cash deposit transaction flow from the
existing transaction flow will be the introduction of deemed acceptance instead of
reversal for AEPS offus cash deposit transactions.
Member banks are requested to kindly take a note of the same and develop
application as per the AEPs Interface Specification Version 4.7.1.Once the
application development is complete member bank is requested to notify NPCl its
readiness to start testing.
In case of any queries member banks are requested to send an email to
aeps@npci.org.in
