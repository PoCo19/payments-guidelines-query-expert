# AePS | OC 39| FY 18 19 | Tokenization transactions to be performed between 10pm and 6am

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2018-19/AePS/015
Date: 25th March, 2019

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2018-19/AePS/015 25th March, 2019
To,
Ail Member Banks
Tokenize transactions to be performed between 10:00 pm and 06:00 am
UIDAl has introduced Tokenization service whereby UiD Token of each Aadhaar linked
account will be provided by UiDAl. AUAs will be initiating tokenize transactions for all their
Aadhaar linked accounts.
These transactions will be customer not present transactions. As banks need to fetch UiD
token for each of the Aadhaar linked account, we are anticipating large number of
transactions. In order to avoid any impact on financial transactions during business hours,
we are requesting member banks to initiate these transactions during non-peak hours
i.e. 10:00 pm and 06:00 am.
The above mentioned timing to perform Tokenize transaction is applicable with immediate
effect.
Member entities are requested to kindly take a note of the same.
