Implementation of SHG Transactions
Self Help Group (SHG) is the primary building block under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana -
National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM) which is the flagship program of the Government
of India. The financial assistance for these SHGs is provided by DAY-NRLM in the form of a
revolving fund.
To ease the access of this fund and leverage the existing channel of Business Correspondent in
the rural segment of India, it was proposed in AePs SCM dated o7th January 2020 for inclusion
of the SHG services in the ONUS & OFFUS environment.
NPCl has implemented the dual authentication services for SHG in the AePs product off-us
transactions using the existing business correspondent's channel.
Action Required from Member banks:
Member banks to approach NPCI for SHG certification and go live by 30th April 2022.
Pre-requisites:
Member banks to undergo certification and go live on all existing AePS services in XML format,
as per Circular No. 60 dated 0gth August 2021.
