Members, Aadhaar Enabled Payment System
Circular: Charges against authentication requests from non-registered devices
As per UIDAl notification number K-11020/44/2012-UIDAl (Auth-l) dated 24.05.2017, AUAs were
notified to upgrade to authentication APl 2.0 and KUAs to eKYC APl 2.1 and ensure adherence to
registered device mandate. As per the provisions of the above stated circular, usage of non-
registered device would attract charges for AUAs at Rs. O.2O per authentication request and for
AsAs at Rs. 0.10 per authentication request w.e.f 1st August 2017.
As ASA, NPCl, was ready in production for Authentication 2.0 and eKYC 2.1 as per the guidelines
issued by UiDAl supporting registered devices. However, NPCl continued to extend support for
non-Registered Devices for the benefit of the member banks who had either not completed
certification or not yet completed the upgrade in the field for the registered devices, against the
provisions of the circular of such requests to be chargeable as mentioned above.
UiDAl, against the provisions of the above stated circular, has recently communicated the charges
for the months of August, September and October 2017 to NPCl. However, since NPCl has been
ready in production and kept the backward compatibility only for the benefit of the members, it
has been decided to recover the AsA charges from members as per the volumes processed by
them respectively. The same was also communicated in the 2oth AePS Steering Committee
Meeting on 3rd November 2017, and circulated in the minutes as well.
In view the above, kindly note NPCl shall recover cumulative charges for all authentication
requests from non-registered devices by each member bank, as part of the daily settlement
activity in a week. Your RM shall proactively share the details of the said recovery with you.
Thanking you and looking forward to your continued support.
