NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
Circular: NPCI/2017-18/AEPS/018 22nd March, 2018
To all AePS member Banks,
Temporarily discontinuation of AePS OFFUS Cash Deposit service
With the relentless vitality by Government of India, Aadhaar enabled Payment Systems
(hereinafter referred to as AePS) has been growing at an exponential rate. AePs provides
various financial and non-financial services which includes cash withdrawal, cash deposit,
fund transfer, etc.
It has been raised by many member Banks that AePs cash deposit solution has led few
member Banks to lose money because of frauds. Further, few Banks have temporarily
discontinued the service.
To avoid such fraudulent activities, NPCl has proactively taken the decision to temporarily
discontinue AePs' OFFus Cash Deposit service till new solution is in production
environment.
Specification document for the new cash deposit solution has already been shared on 21st
March, 2018. So, it is hereby advised to implement new cash deposit solution to offer the
service to customers.
