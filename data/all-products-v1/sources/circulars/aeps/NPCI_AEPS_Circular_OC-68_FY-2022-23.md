Subject: Extension in timelines for migration from IsO to XML messaging specification
This is with reference to the circular number NPCl/2021-22/AEPS/001 dated 05th August, 2021
advising ali AePs members to implement the changes for migration from 1sO-8583 platform
to XMl. platform before 30th April 2022. It was also informed to members that post the given
timeline, the ISO8583 piatform wouid be sunset.
Though some member banks have commenced the certification, we have also received
requests for seeking more time for development and for commencing certification. Given the
criticality of the ecosystem go live, the timeline for migrating from Iso to XMl.. messaging
specification has been extended till 30th August, 2022.
All Members are requested to take note of the above revision and disseminate the information
contained herein to the officials concerned.
We request members to commence certification at the earliest so as to complete the same
and go-live before 3oth August 2022 an XML messaging specification.
