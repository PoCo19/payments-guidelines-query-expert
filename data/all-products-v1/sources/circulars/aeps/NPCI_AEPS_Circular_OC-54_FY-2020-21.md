Certificationof CashDeposit and SHGDual Authenticationservices ofAePS
Cash Deposit being an integral part of basic banking services, NPCl along with steering
committee members have introduced Revised Cash Deposit transaction flow. This will further
strengthen AePs ecosystem by catering the need of customers. In multiple steering committee
meetings, member banks have deliberated the need of Cash Deposit and decided to expedite
the implementation process. In 2gth Steering Committee meeting it was decided that all the
member banks have to approach NPCl for certification and go live on Cash deposit by 3oth
September'20.
NPCl has also introduced OFFUS SHG dual authentication services in AePS. SHG dual
authentication will allow SHG to do Cash Deposit, Cash Withdrawal and Fund Transfer through
existing business correspondent channel. Member Banks are requested to approach NPCl for
October'20.
Please make a note of the above and disseminate the instructions contained herein to officials
concerned.
