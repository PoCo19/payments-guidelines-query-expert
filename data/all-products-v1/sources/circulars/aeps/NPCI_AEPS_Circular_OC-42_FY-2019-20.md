Sub: AePS – Standardization of SMS alerts to customers 
 
AEPS has seen significant growth in number of financial transactions in the recent months. 
To provide a uniform experience for the AePS product across all member banks there is a 
need to standardize SMS alerts sent to customers on their registered mobile no. for any 
debit or credit transactions. 
 
This matter was discussed at the AEPS Steering Committee (SCM) held on March 19, 2019 
wherein it was agreed that -  
 
1. Member / Sub member Banks shall send SMS alert to customers, for every AePS 
Onus/Offus transaction and for entries passed post reconciliation as per the sample 
format shared by NPCI. Please refer to Annexure A wherein SMS format are 
provided.  
 
Implementation date: 
 
The process of Standardization of SMS alerts to customers to be implemented by Sept 30, 
2019 
 
You may please make note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein 
to the concerned officials. 
 
Kindly send the confirmation of the implementation at aeps@npci.org.in
