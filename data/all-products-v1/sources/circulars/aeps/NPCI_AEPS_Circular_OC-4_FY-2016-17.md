Demographic Authentication transactions to be performed between 8 p.m.and 7 a.m
Aadhaar enabled Payment System has been a game changer for promoting Aadhaar as a
financial address and strengthening financial inclusion.
NPCl provides facility for Demographic Authentication as well as online bio-metric
authentication services of UiDAl, to member entities. It allows an entity to verify
demographic"details provided by customer along with his/her Aadhaar number
[Demographic (Address, DOB, Name etc.)+ AADHAAR] as well as bio-metric authentication.
The number of transactions in Demographic Authentication has been increasing steadily
over past few months. The nature of these transactions is off-line as no customer is
waiting on the other side to be serviced.
In order to segregate the on-line and the off-line transactions, it has been decided that
entities will perform Demographic Authentication transactions only during non-peak hours
i.e. 8 p.m. to 7 a.m.
Theabove mentioned timingforperforming Demographic Authentication
transactions is applicable withimmediate effect.
Member entities are requested to kindly take a note of the same.
