ImplementationofDebitBCaccountinAEPSCashDeposit
Respected Sir/Madam,
In 12th AEPS Steering Committee Meeting held on 18th December at NPCl, Goregaon
office, NPCI has informed member banks that for AEPs Cash deposit transactions the
process flow has been suitably amended to include BC's account status validation.
The amended transaction flow will be as under:
The Cash Deposit transaction will be initiated from Acquirer bank's MicroATM and will
be sent to Acquirer switch, Acquirer Bank has to debit BC account first and if the BC
account has been debited successfully then only bank should send request to NPCl
AEPS switch.
Inviewof above,a separateemail intimationonimplementationof DebitBCaccount
hasbeensenttoallthememberbanks.Wehavereceivedconfirmationfromfew
member banks on successful implementation at their end, however there are many
banks who areyet to implement such account validation.
Memberentitiesare hereby requestedtokindly implement thesame immediatelybut notlater
than 30th June 2016.
