Sub:Memberbanks to mandatorilygo live on Offus Revised Flow
AePs transactions had witnessed a significant increase in volume in the month of April' 20,due
to the release of DBT by Government of India. With an increase in volume, technical declines
transaction flow for Off-us transactions. To enable the implementation, the following are
applicable:
a) Member Banks to share the License Key and AUA Code of Bank with NPCl. NPCI shall
issue a detailed standard operating procedure (SOP) in this regard, in due course of
time.
b) In the revised Off-us flow, lssuer banks will receive only successful authenticated
transactions as they were getting in UIDAl API 2.0.
c) All the Member Banks are requested to approach NPCl for certification, post completion
of development at their end and Go live latest by 31st December'20.
Please make a note of the above and disseminate the instructions contained herein to officials
concerned.
