Sub:Guidelines for Self-attestation bymembers/participants
We refer to our OC. NPCl/AePS/2020-21/008 dated 11th January, 2021 regarding adherence to NPCl
guidelines by members/participants. In this connection, we are pleased to inform that we are
implementing a system based process of self-attestation which shall simplify the task of confirming by
members/participants on adherence to various NPCl guidelines/circulars.
The self-attestation check-list is based on documents such as Procedural Guidelines, Operating
Circulars, Technical/Data specifications, etc. Members/participants shall submit self-attestation through
the portal confirming compliance. The Self-attestation activity shall be on a periodic basis such as
annually or as and when required.
User details need to be submitted through in the format given in Annexure A for allowing access to the
15th February, 2021. Members/participants who have still not taken access to the portal are requested to
submit the details in the format URGENTLY.
Members/participants shall submit the self-attestation by 3oth April, 2021.
Please make a note of the contents herein and disseminate the information contained herein to the
officials concerned.
Yours faithfully,
