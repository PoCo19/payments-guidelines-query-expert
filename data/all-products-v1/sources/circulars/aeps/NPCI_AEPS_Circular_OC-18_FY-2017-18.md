NPCI

To,
AllthemembersofAadhaarEnabledPaymentSystem(AePS)
DailyReconciliationofAePStransactions
Dear Sir/Madame,
With the recent initiatives taken by the Government of India, the Aadhaar Enabled
Payment system (AePS) has been growing at an exponential rate.
It is imperative of Banks to perform daily reconciliation of the AePS transactions. Delay
in reconciliation may lead to customer not getting money in timely manner or may lead
to monetary loss for the Bank depending on a cash withdrawal transaction or a cash
deposit scenario respectively.
NPCl has also been emphasizing on daily reconciliation, in all the AePS steering
committee meetings and at various forums.
We request member banks to revert with their written confirmation to the undersigned
by March 31st 2017 on performing daily reconciliation at their end.
