All Members participating in AePS Network
Sub: Enabling non-compliance penalty for Cash Deposit in AePs
Reference may be taken to AEPS operating settlement guidelines wherein it was mandated to the banks to
cash deposit transaction type (O1) RC-71 is considered as Deemed Approved and fund is settled with Issuer
Bank. However, issuer banks have to reconcile their accounts and accord Manual credit to customer account,
if it is not credited in online and update appropriate action in back office system TCC-1o2 for Online Credit &
TCC-103 for Manual Credit.
In view of the above, it has been decided that non-compliance penalty shall be levied on the banks that are
not updating the status in ARCS as per the defined TAT. Please refer below table for the penalty structure.
Txn Type Adj Type Status update Type Penalty Amt Penalty TAT
25 T+2 CD
Cash Deposit (01) TCC Deemed Approved (TCC-102/103) 50 T+3 CD
100 T+4 CD
25 A+2 CD
Cash Deposit (01) CAC Credit Adjustment Confirmation 50 A+3 CD
100 A+4 CD
Note:
i. CD = Calendar Day, A = Adjustment Day. Penalty TAT shall be processed on Calendar Day basis for all
AePS adjustments.
Penalty shail be settled on all 365 days and DSR shall contain separate narration.
Cutover for updating the status in ARCS every day will be night 23 hrs.
iv. Outer limit of the status update compliance penaity is Rs.175/- (25+50+100). This may be revised as
and when there is need and separate communication will be broadcasted.
This shall be effective from February 01, 2024. Please disseminate the information contained herein to the
concerned officials and ensure to make necessary arrangements for compliance. Should you need any further
