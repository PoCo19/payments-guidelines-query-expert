Sub:XMLServices and isOServicesof memberbanksmigrationtoBharathSwitch
Objective:Objective of this circularis to communicate the End of AePs transaction support
on existing NPCI switch and deadlines to migrate the XML & ISO services to new switch.
NPCI supports AePS member banks to interface with NPCI systems using XML and ISO
protocols. NPCl is migrating into new switching solution (Bharat Switch) and all the member
banks were issued with a circular in the past tomigrate to new solution by pointingtheir AePs
systemstonewtargetsysteminNpCl.Whilemostofthebankshavealreadycompletedthis
migration, fewbanks arestill connecting ontheold system.
1)XMLservices migration
All the member banks of AePS opting XML services from NPCl on old switch are hereby
intimated to migrate the services on Bharat Switch (NPCI New Switch) by 22nd December
2017, as the services on existing switch will be completely stopped by December 2017.
Banks which fail to migrate the services to Bharat Switch by 22nd December 2017 will
experiencethedecline intransactionsonexistingNPCIswitch.
2)Iso ServicesMigration
All the member banks of AePS opting IsO services from NPCI on old switch are here by
intimated to migrate the services on Bharat Switch (NPCI New Switch) by 22nd December
2017,as the services on existing switch will be completely stopped by December 2017.
Banks which fail to migrate the services to Bharat Switch by 22nd December 2017 will
experiencethedeclineintransactionson existing NPCl switch.
If you have already migrated, you can ignore this circular.
