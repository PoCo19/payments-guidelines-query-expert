Subject: Dual Authentication interoperable transactions in AePs
We refer to the NPCl operating circular vide reference no. NPC/2021-22/AEPS/004 dated
27th October, 2021 on implementation of SHG transaction.
NPCl is glad to inform operationalising Dual Authentication (Auth) transactions in AePs to
facilitates financial transactions such as cash withdrawal, cash deposit and fund transfer by
authenticating two or more authorized bank account signatories of participating bank at BC
outlet of any other participating bank. The signatories shail be authenticated using Aadhaar
based biometric authentication modality.
Member banks having SHG accounts to enable this service as Issuer & Acquirer (both), and
other participating banks as Acquirer. Existing interchange and NPCl switching fees for
Authentication and AePs OFFUs transactions shall be applicable for Dual Auth transactions.
Refer annexure for more details.
Member are advised to complete necessary system changes, certification with NPCl and Go-
live for supporting Dual Auth interoperable financial transactions in AePS at the earliest.
