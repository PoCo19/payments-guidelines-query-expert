TimelineforAePScertificationofVirtualID&UIDToken
To strengthen privacy and security of Aadhaar number holders, UiDAl had introduced concept
of Virtual ID & UID Token. In line with the changes mandated by UIDAl, updated AePS
AePS member banks. UIDAl vide Circular 03 of 2019 dated 5th April,2019 has extended the
timeline for implementation of Virtual ID and UID Token till 31st May, 2019.
To avoid last moment hassle and any unwarranted action by UiDAl, it is hereby requested to
all AUAs/KUAs to complete the development and line up for certification pertaining to ViD &
UID token by 20th April, 2019 and move to production by 15th May, 2019. Any delay in
implementation of UIDAl APl version 2.5 by AUA/KUA which may result in levy of penalty by
UIDAl on non-compliant transactions shall be sole responsibility of respective AUA/KUA and
NPCl shall not bear any liability of financial disincentives resulting from delay in
implementation ofAPl2.5.
