Executive Overview:
As recommended by UlDAl, this circular introduces Aadhaar OTP as a
step-up authentication in addition to biometric for CW and BAP Member:
transactions undertaken through AePs. Acquirer
From the Effective Date, Acquiring Members must: Region:
1. Ensure that each transaction initiated by the customer for CW and/ Domestic
or BAP exceeding INR 5000, must also be authenticated with
Aadhaar based OTP, mandatorily. Transactions will not be Circular type:
processed if Aadhaar authentication is unsuccessful. Technical changes
2. Such Aadhaar based OTP authentication shall be based on the
mobile number registered with UIDAl for Aadhaar.
3. The OTP provided by the customer shall be mandatorily captured Reference:
NA
and transmitted in addition to customer's biometric data in the
Personal Identity Data ("PID") block in the transaction request to
NPC!. Published Date:

The financial threshold of INR 5000 may be reviewed and revised Effective Date:
periodically based on fraud trends and risk assessment. All Acquiring 30th June 2026
members are requested to incorporate the necessary changes in their
AePS applications by 30th June 2026 and disseminate the information
contained herein within their organization for implementation.
