Sub:Mustdo activities for participating banks
We have highlighted some must to do activities for Aadhaar Enabled Payment System
participating Banks.
1) Bank nodes should be up and running 24*7 as lot of business correspondents are
working at late hours as per the convenience of the customers and the availability of
power.
2) Banks need to comply with NPCl specification and should not make any changes in
application post completion of certification. In case bank wants to make changes they
should inform NPCI and again perform one round of sanity check through NPCI to
ensure that the new changes in software has not impacted existing specifications.
3) Banks need to perform daily reconciliation. Delay in reconciliation may lead to
customer not getting money in timely manner or may lead to monetary loss for the
Bank depending on a cash withdrawal or a cash deposit scenario respectively. The
Bank needs to log in to theNPCi DMS system onadaily basis to extract the rawdata
files and carry out the reconciliation at their end.
