DearSir/Madam,
We are glad to inform you that NPCl has enabled e-KYC Setu System facilitating Regulated Entities of
Reserve Bank of India (RBl) to do e-KYC for verification of identity of a client or its beneficial owner
through online Aadhaar authentication under the Aadhaar Act.
All Regulated Entities of RBl can now avail Aadhaar based e-KYC services by being member of NPCl's
e-KYc Setu System through a simplified single window onboarding process. The functionality shall be
provided through (a) Web based URL re-direction to NPCI Webpage and (b) SDK to facilitate e-KYC
authentication.
The e-KYC Setu System members shall be guided by UIDAl Circular no. 1 of 2023 dated 23rd March
2o23, Procedural Guidelines and Circulars issued by NPCl, UiDAl or Regulator from time to time.
We hereby invite all the Regulated Entities of RBl to reach out to NPCl on ekycservices@npci.org.in to
getonboarded one-KYC SetuSystem.
