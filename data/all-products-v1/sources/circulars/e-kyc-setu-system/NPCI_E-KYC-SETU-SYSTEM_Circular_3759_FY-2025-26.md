In reference to the Circular No. 1 - NPCl/2024-25/eKYC/001 dated 2gth August 2024, NPCI has enabled
e-KYC Setu System for the Regulated Entities of Securities & Exchange Board of India (SEBl), Insurance
Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAl) and Pension Fund Regulatory and Development
Authority (PFRDA). This shall facilitate e-KYC for verification of identity of a client or its beneficial owner
through online Aadhaar authentication under the Aadhaar Act.
All Regulated Entities of RBI, SEBI, IRDAl and PFRDA can now avail Aadhaar based e-KYC services by
being member of NPCI's e-KYC Setu System through a simplified single window onboarding process.
The functionality shall be provided through (a) Web based URL re-direction to NPCl Webpage and (b)
SDK to facilitate e-KYC authentication.
The e-KYC Setu System members shall be guided by UIDAl Circular no. 1 of 2023 dated 23rd March
2023, Procedural Guidelines and Circulars issued by NPCl, UIDAl or Regulator from time to time.
We hereby invite all the Regulated Entities of financial sector regulators to reach out to NPCl on
ekycservices@npci.org.in for onboarding on e-KYC Setu System.
