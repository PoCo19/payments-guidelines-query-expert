# NPCI/2017-18/CTS/004 Issue of new NPCI ID cards at P2F Centre in Mumbai - All

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2017-18/CTS/004

NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2017-18/CTS/004 24thMay,2017
To,
All memberbanksparticipatinginCTSWGP2FsessionatMumbai
Issuanceof newNPCIIDcardsatP2FcentreinMumbai
DearSir/Madam,
Please be informed that the existing ID Cards issued to bank officials by NPCI for
participatinginP2FsessionatMumbaiarenolongervalid.
All bank officials participating in P2F session at Mumbai are requested to submit their
currentlyissuedNPCl cards to NPCl official at P2Fcentre.No new cardswill beissued to
bank employees and the bank staff shall carry and display their bank's identity card for P2F
session.
Out-sourced participants (non-bank staff) attending P2F session, will have to return the
currently issued NPCl cards and collect new identity cards having new NPCI logo. For this
purpose the out-sourced staff will have to get an authorization letter from concerned bank
along with 2 passport size photographs by 02nd June 17.The new ID cards will be issued by
12th June17.Out-sourced staff will need to carry and display NPCl issued new identity card
forP2Fsessions.
