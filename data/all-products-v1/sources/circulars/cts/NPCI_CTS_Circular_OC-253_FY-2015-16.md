Cheque Truncation System-Uniform Holiday Listfor CalenderYear 2016
In terms of Circular DPSS. CO.CHD.No./458/04.07.05/2013-14 dated August 23, 2013

uniform holidays for image based cheque clearing under Cheque Truncation System for
the Calender year 2016 for Western Grid.The Western Grid comprises of States of
Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh.The Uniform Holidays
for calendar year 2016 for the Western Grid are as under:
No. Sr. Date Day Occasion
January26,2016 Tuesday Republic Day
March 25,2016 Friday Good Friday
3. April 01, 2016 Friday Annual closing of Banks
4. August15,2016 Moday IndependenceDay
5. October11,2016 Tuesday Dasara/Vijaya Dashami
