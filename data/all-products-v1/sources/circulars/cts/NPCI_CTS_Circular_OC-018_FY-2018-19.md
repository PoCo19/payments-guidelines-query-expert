Subject: Commencement of Paper to Follow (P2F) centre at Wardha
Dear Sir/Madam,
This is to inform you that NPCI has Commenced Paper to Follow (P2F) centre at
Wardha with effect from 14th August 2018(Thursday) and all banks has to attend the half
hour training session on Thursday at 03:00 PM. On 16th August 2018 P2F Session will be
conducted to exchange instruments presented on 14th August 2018.
All the banks are requested to attend the session daily as per the timings.
