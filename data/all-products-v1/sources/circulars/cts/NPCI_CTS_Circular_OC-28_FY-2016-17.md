CentralizedPositivePaySystem(CPpS)specificationforbanks
NPCI will launch theCentralized Positive Pay System (CPPS)for cheques processed through
the-Cheque Truncation System (CTS):The system interface will be made available to the
member banks subject to execution of SLA and commercial terms.
The CPpS file specification format (Version 1.6) is enclosed along with this circular. We shall
conduct a technical session for CPPS during the 2nd week of July 2016 to the banks that have
confirmed participation..Communication to this effect will be sent separately. Member
banks may prepare themselves for generating the interface recognizable file formats.
Member banks are expected to be technically ready with all the necessary interfaces before
August 10, 2016.
The soft launch of CPps will be done by August 16, 2016.The member banks that are
desirous of participation in the pilot are advised to intimate NPCI before July 05, 2016,
