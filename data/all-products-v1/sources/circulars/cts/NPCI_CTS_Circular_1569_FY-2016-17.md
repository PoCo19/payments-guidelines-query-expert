Newclearingtypeforspecialclearing sessions
Special clearing sessions are conducted during the half yearly and annual closing of accounts as per the
directive of RBl. To maintain the session identify, it has been decided to introduce a new "Clearing Type" for
the special clearing sessions.
The CTs system works on the clearing type and the following are clearing types which are currently under
usage.
RegularCTSclearingitems-01
Non CTS clearingitems -11
The clearing type for instruments to be presented in special clearing sessions will be “gg9".
All the member banks are advised to use the new clearing type “99" only in CXF files while presenting
cheques for processing in special clearing session. Please note that special session will be enabled with this
clearingsession.
All the member banks are advised to take a note of this change and make necessary changes in the capture
systembereadywithnewclearing typeby31st December2016.
