Sub:RecoveryofNetworkchargesfrommembers-F.Y.2017-18
We,at NPCl arecommitted to create comprehensive digitally inclusive nation andourMembers participation is
pivotal in its success. To ensure near zero downtime, connectivity with our Members is very crucial.All NPCl
Members (ortheir service providers)connect toNPCI using NPCI's data communication private networkknown
as NPCINET.Members areprovided withmultiple network connectivity linesfrom different serviceproviders for
PR and DR based on their volumes. The charges for the network is recovered yearly in advance from NPCl
Members.
The network cost to be recovered from all NPCl members, including 55memberbanks who joined NFS during
initial years, were reviewed as per the Board's direction. The network cost to be recovered from All NPCl
members (including sub-members) and other entities for connectivity to NFS, CTS and other products for
financialyear(F.Y.)2017-18isgivenbelow:
1.For F.Y.2017-18, the annual Network cost recovery for connectivity to NFS, CTS and other products shall
be based onthe fixed annual rate finalised forbandwidth utilized by a Member, irrespective of service
provider.
2.If any Member intends to have new link during the F.Y., the billing to the said Member will be on the basis
prescribed for annual bandwidth rate, irrespective of when the link is made live.In other words, there will
notbeanypro-ratabilling.
3.During the F.Y.,ifanymember,intends toupgrade the link bandwidth, theinvoicefor differential cost will
be raised proportionatelybased on bandwidthopted forthe remaining months of the F.Y.
E.g. Bank 'A' up-grades from 2 mbps b/w to 8 mbps b/w on 2oth Jan' 2018. In such case, the additional
billing will be proportionate for 3 months (Jan to Mar '18) @ Rs. 17,000 for F.Y. 17-18. [The difference of
Annual rate for 8 mbps and 2 mbps which is Rs. 68,000 (see table below)). This will be in addition to
Rs.52,000 billedfor2mbps.
4.The equipment costof router&switchesandAMC will be recovered upfront onactuals.
