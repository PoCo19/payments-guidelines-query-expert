are beneficial in terms of building a database of frauds and trend analysis so that control
measures can be implemented to avoid / minimise such instances in future.
NPCl is sharing ATM related fraud trends and suggested mitigating controls for proactive risk
management with member banks on regular basis. Also any new fraud trends identified through
bank reporting or through industry sources are shared immediately in the form of advisory so that
banks can take appropriate action to avoid such instances. Banks also report frauds in the
specified format immediately on detection to NPCl which help in identifying any fraud trend and
implementationofcontrolsforthesame.

AnnexureI-Fraudtypedefinition/description
CounterfeitCheque/ChequeCloning-
Cloned / Counterfeit cheques are manufactured or printed on non-bank paper to look exactly like
genuine cheques but never issued by the respective issuing bank. In most of such cheques the
account number does not exist against which these have been issued.
ChequeForgery/ForgedSignature-
A genuine cheque that has been lost OR stolen and used by a fraudster with a forged signature.
ChequeSpotAlteration-
When alteration performed on specific locations of a cheque e.g. payee's name OR amount it is
calledChequeSpotAlteration.
ChequeWashing-
When alteration is performed to erase information from entire cheque, it is called cheque
Washing.
