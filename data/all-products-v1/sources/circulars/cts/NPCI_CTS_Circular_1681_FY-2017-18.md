Annexure-1
Responsibilitiesof SBl
1. Verifying the identity of the participants.
2. Participant Banks Attendance to be maintained on daily basis.
3. Provide premises and other amenities for conducting the sessions.
4. Maintenance of complaints register so that the member banks can register the same in case of
any cheques are not received or received in excess.
Responsibilitiesofmemberbanks
1.CarrytheP2Freporttothesession.
2. Carry the identity card of your respective Banks for participation in P2F.
3. Exchange the physical instruments with the member banks.
4. Deliver the outward instruments against acknowledgement from the recipient.
5. ReceivetheinwardP2Finstruments.
In case of discrepancy register the complaint maintained at the premises during the session.
