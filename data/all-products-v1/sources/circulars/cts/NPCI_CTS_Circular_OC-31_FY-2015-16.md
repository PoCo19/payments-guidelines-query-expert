RBl mandated NPCI to operationalise Cheque Truncation System (CTS) across the country and as on date
there are 121 locations in the country that are participating in Cheque clearing process through three
gridsviz., Northern, Southernand WesternGrid.
CTS in Western Grid covers five states namely Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and
Goa.This provides a platform for a single clearing settlement within these five states.
As part of the CTS presentment session, banks have internally allocated different cut-off timings for their
locations that participate in CTS. It is reported that some banks are keeping too low a cut-off time (e.g.
not accepting cheques beyond 12:30-1:00 pm) which is against the interest of customers.
This was also one of the concerns raised by RBI in the recently held General Body Meeting (GBM) of
WesternGridClearingHouse.
In view of the above, you are requested to maintain the cut-off timings as much close to the presentment
session closure timing aspossible so that a larger window period for presentment of cheques on the same
dayismadeavailabletocustomers.
You are also requested to adhere to the above and confirm the cut-off timings observed by your bank to
thefollowing personnel by15th Jul'15.This is required tobe shared with RBl.
