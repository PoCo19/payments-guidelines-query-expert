18th CTS steering committee meeting met on January 19, 2016 at Mumbai. The following decisions
are taken by the steering committee.
DR setup
Please refer various communications/Circulars of RBl on BCP/DR arrangements for Payment
Systems. All the member banks should make necessary arrangements to ensure that the
infrastructure and trained resources are in place to handle DR scenario. Banks should also have
capability toprocess the inward from alternate locations in the event of primary location not in
a position to handle inward due to local holiday, bandh or any other technical issues.
Quality
With more than 95% the instruments presented are in line with the CTS 2010 standards the P2F
%age on account of IQA failure should be miniscule however the current gird wise IQA percentage
for the month of January, 2016 is as follows.
S.No. Grid IQA%(PercentagetototalPresentationVolume)
SouthernGrid 0.67%
WesternGrid 0.58%
Northern Grid 0.57%
It has been decided that all member banks should work to reduce the IQA % at the earliest. The
benchmark hasbeen set as 0.5% and all member banksshould work towards achieving the same
by March 31, 2016. Please refer to Annexure -l on the precautions to be taken for reducing IQA
%age.

NationalArchivalServices (NAS)
NAs provides facility to member banks particularly those are participating on sub-member or
indirect member model the access to reports and images. This will completely eliminate the
dependency on the sponsor banks for such reports and images. As on date only 58 Indirect
member banks out of 510 indirect/sub-member banks are live in NAS. The member banks may
refer to NPCI circular/letter no. - NPCI/2015-16/CTS/CIRCULAR NO.13, 14, 15&16 dated May 29,
2015& June 10, 2015& June 11, 2015 & July 16,2015detailing the process to be followed for
getting the access to NAS. It has been decided by the steering committee that all the direct
member banks should work with their indirect/sub-member banks for on-boarding them to
NationalArchivalservices (NAS).
