NPCI

NPCI/2018-19/CTS/026 27th Sept, 2018
To,
The member banks of Dhule Banker's Clearing House,
Subject: Commencement of Paper to Follow (P2F) centre at Dhule.
Dear Sir/Madam,
This is to inform you that NPCI shall Commence Paper to Follow (P2F) centre at Dhule
with effect from 28th September 2018 (Friday). On 28th September 2018 first P2F Session
will be conducted to exchange instruments presented on 27th September 2018.
All the banks are requested to attend the session daily as per the timings.
Address: 1350, Galli No. 5 Nagarpatti, Dhule - 4240001.
Contact person: Mr. C. S. Patil - Branch Manager.
Contact No.: 9422788418 / 8888891651.
P2F session timings: 09:00 AM to 09:.30 AM.
Requesting all member banks to make note of the same.
Thanking You,
Yours.faithfully,
