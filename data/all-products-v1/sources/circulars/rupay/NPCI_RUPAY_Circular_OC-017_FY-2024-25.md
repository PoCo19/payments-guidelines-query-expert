Sub: Revised Timeline for DMS Presentment TAT
Reference may be taken from our circular No/2019-20/RuPay/020 dated October 07, 2019
(attached) on revision of RuPay domestic guidelines. After consuitation with various
stakeholders and as per the RuPay Steering Committee endorsement, the timelines for
acquirer presentment for DMs transactions shall be revised from existing 60 days to 10 days.
This change shall be implemented effective December 10, 2024.
Member are reguired to note the following information related the change:
a. Acquiring banks should note that there shall be no revision to the "Late Presentment" TAT
of T+4 calendar days, transaction date being T+0 i.e. Harmonization of TAT. Any
presentment done after the defined TAT wil be considered as late presentment.
b. For any transaction presented by the acquirer after T + 4 days, the issuer shall recover the
amount from the cardholder on a best-effort basis. If the amount is not recoverable, the
issuer may raise a chargeback under Reason Code 1081-transaction not settled within
the specified timeframes.
c. The RuPay system shall accept DMs Presentment up to 10 calendar days only i.e.
T+10 days. Transactions presented beyond TAT will be rejected by the system with the
reason code 3207-The TAT for the Dispute CyclelAdjustment you trying to raise has
expired.
d. All acquiring banks are advised to ensure that the transactions presented with the TAT
defined (as explained above).
