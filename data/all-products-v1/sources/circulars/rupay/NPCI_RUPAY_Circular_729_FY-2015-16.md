Sub: Settlement Holidays for RuPay
Ref: C 157 dated 2oth March, 2015 on settlement holidays for RuPay
We refer RBI Circular No. RBl/2015-16/168 DPSS (CO) RTGS No. 492 / 04.04.002/ 2015-16 dated 1$t
September, 2015 on changes in RTGS time window. As advised therein, RTGS will not be operated on
second and fourth Saturdays from 1st September, 2015.
We wish to inform you that with effect from September, 2015, RuPay settlement entries shall not be
posted in RTGS on second and fourth Saturdays (This is in addition to Sundays and RTGS holidays
published by RBl). For Sundays, RTGS holidays and for second and fourth Saturdays, the settlement shall
be done on the next working day.
All members of RuPay are requested to take note of the above mentioned settlement holidays and
disseminate the information contained herein to the officials concerned.
