Sir/Madam, 
RuPay Contactless v2.0 (qSPARC) specifications was released to the ecosystem on 1st 
August, 2017. Post release of the specifications, we have successfully certified cards and 
terminal devices for RuPay Contactless v2.0. RuPay Contactless v2.0 specifications were 
released with support for RuPay Contactless v1 .0 to provide backward compatibility. 
We have reviewed the support for RuPay Contactless v1 .0 and it has been decided to end 
the support for legacy specifications v1 .0 on 31 st December 2020. 
Post 31st Dec 2020, Card and Terminal applications built on v1 .0 specifications will not be 
supported and the certification service for the RuPay Contactless v1 .0 specifications will be 
discontinued. 
All concerned are requested to take note of the aforesaid. For any query, please contact 
your NPCI relationship manager.
