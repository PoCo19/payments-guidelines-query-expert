RuPay system generates domestic BIN Management files (81,82,91,92) post each settlement cycle in 
BCS portal. In order to facilitate better reconciliation for the member banks a few changes were 
implemented in BIN Management files 91 & 92.  The details of additional parameters included in BIN 
Management files 91 & 92 are detailed in Annexure I.  The modified format of 91 & 92 is being already 
consumed by the member banks. 
With the above changes the files formats 81 & 82 have become redundant as all the required information 
is provided in 91 & 92.  In order to reduce the load on the system it has been decided to discontinue 
the formats 81 & 82 with effect from February 20, 2025.   
Kindly refer the RuPay RGCS Technical Specification version v1.38 or above, for further information 
Member banks are advised to take note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein 
to all concerned stakeholders. In case of any further clarification please reach out to your respective 
customer success relationship manager.
