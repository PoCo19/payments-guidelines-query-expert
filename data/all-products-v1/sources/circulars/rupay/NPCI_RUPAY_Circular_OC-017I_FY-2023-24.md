Subject: Enhancing transaction limits for Small Value Digital Payments in Offline Mode for 
NCMC 
Offline Payments is one of the mandatory transactions to be supported for RuPay by NCMC 
certified banks. On January 03, 2022 RBI released the circular RBl/2021-22/146, 
CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S1264/02-14-003/2021-2022 mentioning the upper limit of an offline 
payment to be INR Ü200. (attached as Annexure A) 
We wish to bring to the attention of member banks that RBI has now issued the Circular No: 
RBl/2023-24/57 CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S526/02-14-003/2023-24 on August 24, 2023 (attached as 
Annexure B) wherein the upper limit of an offline payment is enhanced to INR f500, with 
immediate effect. 
In light of the above, Ru Pay member banks certified for NCMC are requested to make necessary 
changes in the systems to support offline payments upto INR 500. 
Members are hence advised to bring the content of the circular to the notice of all relevant staff.
