NPCI
NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCl/2023-24/RuPay/008 31st Jul'23
To,
All RuPay Member Banks
Subiect: 5o% NPCl switching fee rebate for Cooperative and RRBs Issuer Banks on
RuPay Debit card PoS and eCom domestic transactions for FY 23-24.
This is in reference to NPCl circular, NPCl/2021-22/RuPay/049 dated 28th Mar'22 (Annexure
A) on switching fees rebate for RuPay Debit card PoS and eCom domestic transactions.
To support Cooperative Banks and Regional Rural Banks, 50% of NPCI's switching fee on
RuPay Debit Card PoS and eCom domestic transactions is being provided as rebate upto 31 st
Mar'24.
For all other Issuer member Banks, except for above, switching fee will be applicable as per
the circular, NPCl/2019-20/RuPayl023 (Annexure B).
The rebate structure shall not be applicable for Credit, Prepaid and Specific value services
like AutoPay, EMl, cash@POS, NCMC offline transactions, etc.
The rebate shall cease to exist upon roll-back of the Zero MDR regime.
Member banks to take note of the same and cascade the information contained in this circular
to their concerned officials.
