Platinum Debit Card PoS & e-Comm On-Us transaction data.

program already launched is"RuPay Hi Flyer Program".) In order to process incentives on such
offers on usageof RuPayCards, Member Banks are requested to upload the On-Us transaction data
of RuPay PoS &e-Commfor all RuPay BINs in RGCS on monthlybasis by5th of the succeeding month.
Member Banks will be in a position to upload the RuPay Card On-Us data only on completion of
CertificationtoUploadthisdatainRGCS.
We request all MemberBanksto completethe certificationprocessof uploadingdataforall RuPay
BINs immediately.
Please ensure that correct and complete RuPay PoS & e-Comm On-Us data is submitted well in time
forprocessingoffersonRuPayCardsbyNPCl.
