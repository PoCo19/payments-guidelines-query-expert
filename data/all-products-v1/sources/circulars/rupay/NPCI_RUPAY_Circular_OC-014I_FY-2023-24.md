Subject: RuPay Credit Card -Classic Variant on UPl
NPCl has launched RuPay Credit Card program as an alternate home-grown network in 2017,
wide NPCl/2017-18/RuPayl003 dated 10th April 2017 with subject "RuPay Credit Card
Program Circular" with three retail variants i.e. Classic (Mass), Platinum (Premium) and
Select (Super Premium). In September 2022, linking of RuPay Credit Cards on UPl (RCC on
UPl) was introduced, where the user can link their RuPay Credit Card on UPI apps to make
merchant payments wide NPCl/2022-23/RuPayl019 dated 4th October 2022 with subject
"Operating circular for RuPay Credit Cards linked to UPj"
To increase Credit Card penetration, there was an ask from ecosystem (Credit Card issuers
and new age fintech companies) for acceptance of Classic variant through Credit Cards on
UPi to target mass and new to credit customers.
Members to take a note, like other Card variants customers can link their Classic Credit Card
variant on UPI and make transactions. The interchange on UPl shall remain same as per
RuPay POS interchange for Classic Variant.
This will be applicable from 15th September 2023
