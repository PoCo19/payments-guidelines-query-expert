Subject: PA-DSS and PCl-DSS Compliance for Transit Acquiring
This is with reference to RBI circular DPSS (CO) PD No.1462 / 02.14.003 / 2012-13, dated
February 2013 on Security and Risk Mitigation Measures for Electronic Payment Transactions.
In compliance of the above circular, members participating in providing NCMC Acquiring
Infrastructure should ensure that the terminals and other infrastructure installed at the
(Payment Card Industry- Data Security Standards) and PA- DsS (Payment Applications - Data
Security Standard) standards.
Request members to bring the contents of the circular to the notice of all relevant staff.
