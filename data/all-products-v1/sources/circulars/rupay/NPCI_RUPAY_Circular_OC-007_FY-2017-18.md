Sub:Migrationof RuPay Switch(lssuer&Acquirer)Onlinetransactions toNew Switch
During thelast 7years of operations, NPCl has grown manifold-both in terms of range of services and
volume of transactions. On a typical day, we process about 3o million transactions pertaining to various
products offered by NPCl.
Toaugment its system capability,NPCl is in the process of changing its Switch to anenhancedand better
version.
Presently, all RuPay member Banks are connected to NPCl either directly or through AsP model. As part
of the Switch migration, all RuPay (Pos) Issuing & Acquiring Member Banks' switch connectivity will be
migrated from the current Switch to the New Switch in a phased manner. The first set of 5 Banks
identified for migration, would be migrated to the New Switch on 17th April, 2017. The remaining Issuing
& Acquiring Banks would be migrated in a phased manner over next few months. Existing Clearing and
Settlement systems will remain the same as available today and there will not be any change.
We shall intimate respective Banks as and when such Banks aretomigrate (both as Issuerand Acquirer)
to our New Switch. It may be noted that there is no change required to be done at Banks end, except
pointing the connections to New Switch.
All the members of RuPay (Pos) are requested to take a note of the above and disseminate the
information containedtotheofficials concerned.
