Modification of Age criteria forRuPay PMJDY Cards (with insurance coverage of INR2
Lacs) for FY 2019-20
1. This is in reference to the circular issued to member banks pertaining to "RuPay Insurance
Program for FY 2019-20 -RuPay Non Premium Cards"dated 23rd May 2019. Attached
as Annexure A.
2. We wish to inform the member banks that the age criteria for claiming insurance for
RuPay PMJDY cards on accounts opened after 28th August 2018 with insurance
coverage of INR 2 Lacs has been modified as below:
Insurance Age Criteria - Age Criteria -
Program
Cover Current Modified
PMJDYCardsIssued 18 years to 65
INR 2 Lacs 5 years and above
after 28th Aug'18 years
3. The above mentioned modification in age criteria will be effective 1st April, 2019. All other
terms and conditions pertaining to RuPay Insurance Program for FY 2019-20 for RuPay
Non Premium Cards will remain the same.
