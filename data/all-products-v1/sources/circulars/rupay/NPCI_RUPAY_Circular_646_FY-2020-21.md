Subject: Enablement of RuPay Contactless Offline and Supporting (Online) Transactions
RBl had approved offline transactions in transit & recently approved the pilot in retail
2020). Member banks live on RuPay Contactless are certified to process offline retail and transit
transactions along with supporting (online) transactions.
We wish to bring to the attention of member banks that an open loop transit project (Multi Bank)
with Delhi Metro Rail Corporation, Airport Line is going to be launched in Nov 2o20.
In light of the above, we reguest RuPay Contactless lssuer banks to activate offline & support
(online) transactions for their customers on an immediate basis. Following steps are provided
below to enable offline and supporting transactions.
1) Enable Money Add Transactions (cash, account & balance update) to pre load the offline
wallet
2) Enable Offline Balance Management (creation of virtual account, i.e. replica of wallet
balance at bank's end)
3) Maximum permissible wallet balance is INR 2,o00 at any point of time.
4) Enable Service Creation for the Service IDs as below:
Common Service Area- FFo1
Delhi Metro Airport Line - 6010
Request members to bring the contents of the circular to the notice of all relevant staff.
