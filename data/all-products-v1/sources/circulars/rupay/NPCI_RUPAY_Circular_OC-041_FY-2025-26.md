Subject: Validation of RuPay Fuel Surcharge Fee in Presentment Transactions 
All system participants are hereby directed to adhere with the following guidelines regarding the 
levy and validation of fuel surcharge fees on RuPay card transactions. 
RuPay permits Acquiring Participants to levy a surcharge up to maximum allowed capping or fuel 
transactions. However, it has been observed that certain participants are submitting surcharge 
amounts in the presentment file that exceeds the permissible limits, which is not as per the norms 
endorsed in the RuPay Steering Committee Meeting dated 27th June 2025. 
Effective November 25, 2025, NPCI will implement a validation mechanism to enforce 
compliance. Any presentment containing a surcharge amount exceeding the maximum 
permissible limit will be declined with Reason Code 2028 – “Cap Amount Exceeded”. Such 
transactions will not be processed for settlement. 
While NPCI is implementing system-level controls to validate and settle permissible surcharge 
fees, this does not absolve banks from their responsibility to perform proper reconciliation. 
Member banks should adhere strictly to the surcharge fee guidelines and proactively ensure 
accurate fee details are updated in the presentment file to avoid declines and ensure seamless 
transaction processing.  
The applicable surcharge caps for various RuPay card types are as follows:
