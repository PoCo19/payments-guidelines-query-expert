Sub: MACing of Card based Transaction routed through NPCl
Your reference is invited with respect to NPCI Circular Ref: NPCI/2018-19/RMD/009 issued on 18th
September 2o18, all members of National Financial Switch (NFS) and RuPay were advised to
implement MACing for card based transactions by 3oth November 2018.
However it has been observed that the above implementation timelines has not been adhered by
many banks. Considering increased threats of various cyber-attacks, it is prudent to be proactively
prepared against such events. Therefore you are advised to complete MACing implementation by 31 st
March 2019.
