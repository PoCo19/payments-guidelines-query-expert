Issuance of RuPay Cards with International Profiles
RuPay has received regulatory approval to go global. NPCl International Payments Limited
(NIPL), a wholly-owned subsidiary of NPCl is working towards building inter-regional partnerships
to enhance the global acceptance of RuPay Cards.
1. To facilitate acceptance of RuPay Cards across international ATMs and Point of Sale
(POS) terminals, member banks are requested to refer RuPay EMV Dual Interface Card
Profile Specification V2.0.12 and ensure issuance of RuPay Cards on the below profiles
going forward 
Profile 0o4: For member banks live on RuPay Contactless
Profile Oo8:Formemberbanks live on RuPay Contact
2. Existing cards issued on RuPay BIN by Banks on Domestic Profile need to be centrally
enabled by default for international transactions.
3. In line with RBl notification on enhancing security of card transactions, RBl/2019-
20/142/DPSS.CO.PD No.1343/02.14.003/2019-20 (Annexure A), member banks to
comply and to provide card holders with facility to switch ON/ OFF/ manage enablement
of international transactions.
Member banks to note that this circular comes into effect immediately.
