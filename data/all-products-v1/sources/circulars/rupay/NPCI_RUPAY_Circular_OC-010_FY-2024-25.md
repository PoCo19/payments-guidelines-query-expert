Subject: Adoption of PCI Card Production and Provisioning Guidelines for Assessments 
of RuPay Cards 
NPCI – RuPay certifies various card manufacturing and personalization Card Bureaus for 
issuance of RuPay cards. RuPay member banks can procure cards only from these certified Card 
Bureaus. The certification of Card Bureaus is done basis the guidelines provided by NPCI. The 
guidelines provide details related to physical and logical security to be implemented at the 
premises of Card and Personalization Bureau along with the details of policies and procedures to 
be followed.  
Please be informed that the following changes shall be implemented as per the given timelines 
w.r.t. the RuPay card Assessments standards: 
a) RuPay Card Assessments: RuPay shall be adopting the Payment Card Industry (PCI) 
Security Standards Council (SSC) Card Production and Provisioning (CP) Physical and 
Logical Security Requirements guidelines w.r.t. 1stAug, 2024 for the Card Bureau 
assessment. The process is given in Annexure A.  
b) Please note that of ongoing Card Bureau assessment through the existing agency i.e. 
Fime can continue and the assessment conducted & concluded up to 30th September, 
2024, shall be considered. 
c) Card Bureau to ensure that the NPCI annual fees is paid, before applying for Certificate 
of Compliance (CoC) with NPCI. 
We request members to make a note of the above and disseminate the information to the 
concerned teams.

Annexure A 
 
Process for Card Bureau Assessments compliance for RuPay cards: 
 
1. RuPay shall be adopting the Payment Card Industry (PCI) Security Standards Council 
(SSC) Card Production and Provisioning Compliance (CP) Physical and Logical Security 
Requirements guidelines w.r.t. 1st Aug 2024. 
2. NPCI-RuPay mandates Card Bureaus to engage Payment Card Industry Compliance (PCI 
CP) Security Assessors Card Production Security Assessor (CPSA) company(s) listed in 
the Payment Card Industry Security Standards Council (PCI SSC) website for their initial 
assessments for new sites and annual assessments for existing sites. 
3. The Card Bureaus are required to engage with the Card Production Security Assessor 
(CPSA) Company and complete the on-site assessment 90 days prior to the expiry of their 
Certificate of Compliance (CoC) annually. 
4. The Assessments of the Card Bureau’s facility is carried out as per the guidelines given 
in - Payment Card Industry (PCI) Security Standards Council (SSC) Card Production and 
Provisioning (CP) Physical and Logical Security Requirements and related updates in the 
PCI CP FAQ. 
5. Assessors or Card Bureau shall submit the Report on Compliance (ROC) and Attestation 
of Compliance (AoC) to NPCI along with a request letter. The draft of request letter to be 
submitted by Card Bureau is given in Annexure B.  
6. Upon closure of all the observations and issuance of AOC by the Assessor, NPCI shall 
consider issuance of the Certificate of Compliance (CoC) to the Card Bureau. The CoC 
shall be issued to the Card Bureau, only after payment of the annual fees.  
7. Reassessment should be done if there is any alteration in logical, technical, physical or 
structural form, as well as during decommissioning or change of ownership.
