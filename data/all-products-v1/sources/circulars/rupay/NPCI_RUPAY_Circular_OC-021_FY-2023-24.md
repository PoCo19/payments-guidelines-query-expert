Subject: Acguirers to Comply with RuPay Guest Check-Out Tokenization (GCOT)
implementation
With reference to the RBl communication vide letter dated 21st April, 2023 on Restriction on
Storage of Actual Card Data, RBi directed that an alternate solution shall be depioyed for
transactions where cardholders decide to enter the card details manually at the time of
undertaking the transaction (commonly referred to as "guest checkout transactions"), by
October 31, 2023. We also refer to NPCl issued circular No. NPCl/2022-23/RuPay/012 dated
7th June, 2022, and the Advisory No. NPCl/2023-24/RuPay/033 dated 17th July, 2023 on the
matter.
On a review of the issues involved and after detailed discussions thereon with all stakeholders,
RBl has extended the timeline for implementing alternate solution for guest checkout
transactions by three months, i.e., till January 31, 2024.
Please note that the above timeline is also applicable for re-sequencing the COFT (Card on
transaction).
Kindly note, this is a high priority security mandate, and appropriate penal action,
including imposition of business restrictions, may be considered in case of any non-
compliance.
We look forward to your support to get detailed project plan with dates to implement the RuPay
Guest Check-out Tokenization solution at the earliest.
