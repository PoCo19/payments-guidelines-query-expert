RuPayeCommerce-NewFlowofCard&OneTimePassword
1.TheexistingtransactionflowforRuPayeCommercerequires csustomerauthenticationthroughOne
Time Password (OTP), image selection, pass phrase and ATM PIN for first time inflight registration,
image and ATM PIN for subsequent lowvalue transactions and OTP and ATM PIN for subsequent
highvaluetransactions.
2.The member banks have been giving suggestions on the RuPay E-Commerce transaction Flow. In the
RuPay Steering Committee meeting dated 18th November, 2015. NPCI had proposed and the
member banks agreed that NPCI should introduce a simplified alternate flow of Card & OTP for RuPay
eCommercetransactions.
3.ThenewflowofCard&OTPaimsto:
a. Simplify the transaction flow
b. Improvethesuccessrate
C. Reduce the payment time for the customer
d. Eliminate the drop outs on the image selection & ATM PIN pad page.
4. The Issuing Banks are required to do a minor changes in their switch post which a brief round of
certification will be required for migrating to the new flow. No changes are required in the banks
IssuerAuthentication System (IAS)/ACS interface with NPCl.
5. Member banks are hereby advised to carry out the necessary development at their end so that new
transactionflowof Card +OTPcanbe made liveonorbefore15th April 2016.
6.Please contact your relationship manager for the specifications document with regards to the change
required in banksswitch.
