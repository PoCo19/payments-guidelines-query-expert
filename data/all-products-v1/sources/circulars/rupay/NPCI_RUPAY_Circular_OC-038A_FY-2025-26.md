To, 
All Members of RuPay 
 
Sub: – Addendum to the implementation of NRP & revision in PRD process of RuPay  
 
With reference to OC 38, it was communicated that the revised NRP process shall be applicable 
to chargeback cases raised from 30th September 2025. 
  
It is hereby clarified that the said reference shall be read as applicable to arbitration cases raised 
from 30th September 2025. 
  
Members are advised to take note accordingly.
