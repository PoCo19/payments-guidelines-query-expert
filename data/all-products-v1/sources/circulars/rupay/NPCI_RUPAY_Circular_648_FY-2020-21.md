NPCI/2020-21/RuPay- Bulletin/ 001                                                                         29th June 2020 
Enhancing Security of Card Transactions – Extension Update 
 
This is in reference to the Product Bulletin released by NPCI dated 8th April 2020 pertaining to 
Enhancing Security of Card Transactions – Extension Update. 
 
We wish to inform the member Banks, as per the circular released by RBI Ref. No. RBI/2019-
20/251 DPSS.CO.PD NO.1897/02.14.003/2019-20, dated 04th June, 2020 the implementing 
provisions on Enhancing Security of Card Transactions has further been extended till 30th 
September 2020. 
 
We thus request all member banks to take a note of the same and take appropriate actions 
accordingly. 
 
We thank you for continued patronage.  
 
For any queries, please write to us at rupay@npci.org.in or contact your NPCI Relationship 
Manager.
