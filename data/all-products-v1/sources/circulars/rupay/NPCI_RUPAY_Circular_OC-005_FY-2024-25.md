RuPay Platinum Debit Card Lounge Program
To,
All Members - RuPay Platinum Debit issuers.
Dear Sir/Madam,
We refer to the NPCI Operating Circular vide NPCl/2023-24/RuPay/010 dated 22nd September
2023, wherein the features and benefits of RuPay Platinum Debit Card program are detailed.
In the RuPay Steering Committee (SC) Meeting held on 28th May 2024, the updated proposition
for RuPay Platinum Debit Card was discussed and the SC endorsed the following:
a) For RuPay Platinum Debit Cards:
a. 1 (one) Domestic Airport / Railway lounge access per quarter
b. 2 (two) International Airport Lounge access per annum
RuPay Platinum Debit Lounge program will be effective from quarter starting 1st July 2024.
The Domestic Lounge Program for RuPay Platinum Debit Card provides lounge access to
Lounges. (Domestic Lounge refers to all the lounges within geographical boundary of India will
be considered as domestic lounge.)
Issuers shall communicate the updated Lounge program to the cardholders through bank's
communication channels including but not limited to, welcome kits, e-mails, SMs, and website.
lounges is available on - https://www.rupay.co.in/lounges.
Please Note: All the other features and benefits will remain the same as detailed in NPCl/2023-
24/RuPay/010 dated 22nd September 2023.
Request all the member banks to make a note of the above and provide communication to RuPay
Platinum Cardholders about the features and benefits of the card.
