Subiect: 50%NPCl switching fee rebate for Cooperative and RRB Issuer Banks on RuPay
Debit card PoS and eCom domestic transactionsforFY2022-23
This is in reference to NPCI's circulars, NPCi/2019-20/RuPay/50 dated 31st March 2020 (Annexure
A) & NPCl/2021-22/RuPay/040 dated 18th February 2022 (Annexure B) on switching fees rebate for
RuPay Debit card PoS and eCom domestic transactions.
To support Cooperative Banks and Regional Rural Banks, 50% of NPCi's switching fee on RuPay
Debit card PoS and eCom domestic transactions is being provided as rebate* up to March 31, 2o23.
For all the other Issuer member Banks, except the above, switching fees will be applicable as per the
circular, NPCl/2019-20/RuPay/023 dated 19gth November 2019 (Annexure C).
#Rebate structure shall not be applicable for Credit, Prepaid and specific value services like AutoPay,
EMl, cash@POS, NCMC - Offline transactions, etc.
Member banks to take note of the same and cascade the information contained in this circular to their
concerned officials.
