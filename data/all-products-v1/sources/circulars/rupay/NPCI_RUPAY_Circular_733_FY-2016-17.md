# 10-Apr-2015 - RuPay/03/2015-16 - Incremental cards linked to PMJDY accounts to be issued on a distinct RuPay IIN

Date: 10th April, 2015

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCi
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
Circular:RuPay/03/2015-16 10th April, 2015
Incremental cards linked to PMJDY accounts to be issued on a distinct RuPay lIN
KindAttention:All PublicSectorBanksandPrivateSectorBanks
1. All banks may please note that effective 1't August, 2015, all incremental RuPay cards linked
to Pradhan MantriJanDhanYojana (PMJDY)accounts must be issued ona distinct RuPay lIN.
2. Banks not having an existing separate RuPay lIN for RuPay cards linked to PMJDY accounts
should get in touch with their NPCI Relationship Manager.
3.Banks are requested to exhaust the existing card inventory by31st July, 2015.
4.All new card designs must be approved only using unique liNs taken forissuing cards linked
toPMJDYaccounts.
Forfurtherqueries inthis regard, pleasecontact your NPCIRelationshipManager.
