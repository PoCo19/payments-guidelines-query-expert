RuPayCreditCardsRewardsParityw.e.f.1stSeptember2o24
Credit card rewards points are a key lever to drive spending on credit cards. It has been observed
that the rewards points, benefits & features for RuPay credit cards on UPi transactions are not
equivalent to the rewards points, benefits & features offered on Card transactions.
1. lssuers shall ensure that the rewards points, benefits, features and other ancillary offers
should not be lower (directly & indirectly) for RuPay Credit Cards on UPl transactions and
RuPay Credit Cards, except for transactions where issuer does not earn any interchange
fee.
2. Member banks are expected to adhere to the circular by 1st September 2024.
We kindly request member banks to acknowledge this directive and ensure that the contents of
this circular are effectively communicated to relevant officials.
