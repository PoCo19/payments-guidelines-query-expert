Product Bulletin                                                                                                        8th May 2020 
Subject: Arrangement with “Reliance General Insurance Company Limited” for providing COVID-
19 Insurance coverage to RuPay Cardholders 
This is in reference to the coronavirus (COVID-19) outbreak which is causing widespread concern and 
increasing economic hardship for consumers, businesses and communities. With the current outbreak of 
COVID pandemic, cover for COVID-19 is the need of the hour coverage. 
In reference to above, we wish to inform you that NPCI has got into an arrangement with “Reliance General 
Insurance Company Limited” for providing a benefit COVID Insurance coverage to RuPay Cardholders. 
This is a core benefit plan wherein, benefit shall be provided to the cardholder irrespective of hospitalization 
(subject to fulfilment of terms & conditions).     
RuPay Cardholders may choose any of the coverage amount ranging from INR 25,000 up to INR 2,00,000.

RuPay Cardholders may click on the below mentioned link and purchase the insurance coverage by making 
the premium payment using their RuPay cards for the coverage which they wish to avail. 
RuPay website - https://www.rupay.co.in/covid-insurance OR  https://bit.ly/2LdatX2  
The terms & conditions, along with the Claims process & Exclusions have been enclosed as Annexure to 
this bulletin. We urge the Banks to intimate the same to all RuPay Cardholders through various channels 
(E-Mail, SMS, Website, etc) so that the RuPay Cardholders will be able to take the benefit of the same. 
We request member Banks to take note of the same and bring the contents of this bulletin to the notice of 
relevant staff.
