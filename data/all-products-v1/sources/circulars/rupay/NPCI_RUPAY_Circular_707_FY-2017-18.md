Integrationof Bharat QR withUPI credentials
Dear All,
Asper the direction received from Reserve Bankof India inOctober 2o16,NPCl and
International card schemes jointlyworkedtowards implementing common interoperable QR
code based card payment solution namely “Bharat QR". Bharat QR was launched on 2oth
February,2017.
Further, under the guidance of Indian Bank Association (IBA),NPCIhad facilitated to integrate
UPIcredentials (VirtualPaymentAddress (VPA),Account+IFSC&Aadhaarnumber)asapart
ofBharatQRstandards.
In this regard, NPCI had released technical specifications (Merchant QR Code Specification V
4.0) to integrate UPl credentials in Bharat QR to all RuPay acquiring member banks on 18th
May, 2017.
ItmaybenotedthatRuPayacquiringmemberbankshavealreadystartedthedevelopment
tointegrateUPlcredentialswithBharat QRandvariousbanksareunder certificationwith
NPCI.
We request all RuPayacquiring member banks to complywith Merchant QR Code
Specifications V 4.0 and ensure that all Bharat QRdeployed post 15th Sep 2017 should contain
UPIcredentials.
