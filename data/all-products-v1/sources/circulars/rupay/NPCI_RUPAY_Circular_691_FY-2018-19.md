Sub: Enhancements in RuPay Global Clearing & Settlement System (RGCS) for RuPay PoS &
e-CommDomestictransactions
We are pleased to inform that the below mentioned enhancements will be implemented for RuPay
Pos & e-Comm Domestic transactions (based on suggestions received from Member Banks and
approved by RuPayOperations Working Group in the meeting held on July 26,2017)
Revisionin DMSPresentmentTAT
Currently the TAT for Presentment of DMS transactions in RGCS is 365 calendar days.
EffectiveApril 10,2018theTATforPresentmentofDMStransactions inRGCSwill berevisedto120
calendardays fromtheexisting365calendardays.
The Good-faith window will be availableas per the existing TAT of 365 calendar days.
This revision will help and assist Member Banks in effectively handling their operational activities
and dispute resolution process through timely Presentment of DMS transactions in RGCS.
Inclusion of McC (Merchant Category Code) and Merchant Name in Incoming files (o1 file)
On processing of RuPay PoS &e-CommDomestic transactions in RGcS,response files (Incoming files)
are provided to Issuing as well as Acquiring Member Banks for reconciliation of their transactions.
Currently the O1 Incoming files generated for Member Banks in RGcS, do not contain McC
(Merchant CategoryCode)andMerchantName.
Effective April 24,2018 thebelow 2xml tagswill beincluded in the01 Incomingfiles.
Field Name XML Tag
CardAcceptorBusinessCode <nCrdAcpBussCd>
CardAcceptorName <nCrdAcpNm>
Member Banks are requested to make a note of the above and make necessary changes in their
systems for smooth functioning of their daily reconciliation process.
