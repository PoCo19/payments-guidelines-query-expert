Adhering to the Telecom Commercial Communication Customer Preference Regulations” 
(TCCCPR) Guideline 
Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) is in the process of implementing the “The Telecom 
Commercial Communication Customer Preference Regulations” (TCCCPR) guidelines by 31st 
May 2020. In adherence to the upcoming implementation of these regulations, all businesses, 
enterprises (principal entities) and Telemarketers are mandated to register with operators on the 
Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) platform, along with their headers or sender IDs and content 
templates. Additionally, TRAI has now directed all operators to restrict traffic for principal entities 
who have not registered themselves and their respective headers or sender IDs on DLT. 
We request all our member banks to kindly complete their registration immediately on the DLT 
platform, with respect to commercial communication (Transactional, Service or Promotional).  
Please note that Banks might be restricted to send any communication if they do not complete 
their registration by 31st May 2020 as TRAI plans to fine Telecom operators that do not comply 
with this from 01st June, 2020. 
For further reference, request you to please refer “NOTIFICATION” of Telecom Regulatory 
Authority of India as attcahed. 
We thank you for continued patronage.
