Madam/ Dear Sir,
Sub: MACing of Card based Transaction routed through NPC
We refer to NPCI Circular NPCI/2018-19/RMD/009 dated 18th September 2018 and circular
NPCI/2018-19/RMD/019 dated 14th February 2019 on implementation of MACing for Card based
transactions routed through NPCl. The timelines advised for implementation was 31st March 2019.
It is observed that the implementation is yet to be completed by certain Banks.
Considering the benefits of MACing, Banks are advised to implement MACing by 15th November
2o19. Please note that no further extension of timelines will be granted on the same.
Please make note of the above and disseminate the instructions contained herein to the officials
concerned.
