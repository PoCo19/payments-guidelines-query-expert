RuPay Contactless Cards Issuance
In delivering a smooth payment experience, innovation plays a pivotal role. Contactless payments
exemplify this by enabling seamless transactions, allowing customers to tap their cards near
point-of-sale terminals via near field communication technology.
RuPay issuers to take a note that all the RuPay Cards shall be enabled with contactless features
for domestic (online and offline-NCMC) and international transactions. All issuers shall only
procure and issue contactless cards for their RuPay portfolio (Debit, Credit and Prepaid Cards).
1. Ail new issuances, renewal or replacement of cards shall be on contactless cards.
2. RuPay Prepaid Cards-Gift PPls may continue to be issued with or without EMV Chip as
per the RBl Master Directions on Prepaid Payment Instruments (refer master direction no.-
RBI/DPSS/2021-22/82).
Issuers to take a note to issue all cards on RuPay Global Card Profile 004/003 and 003 for
RuPay contactless debit and credit cards respectively (refer circular no. NPCl/2o21-
22/RuPay/017 dated 15th October 2021).
Issuers to ensure that the contents of this circular are effectively communicated to the relevant
stakeholders.
This circular shall be effective from 1st April 2024.
