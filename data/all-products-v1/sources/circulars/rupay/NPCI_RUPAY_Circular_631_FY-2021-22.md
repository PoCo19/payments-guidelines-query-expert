Dear Sir / Madam,
Subject: Update on Introduction of new Lounges under RuPay Lounge Access Program
This is in reference to RuPay Domestic Lounge Access Program, where, RuPay offers two
complimentary free domestic lounge access per quarter for Platinum and higher variant of
RuPay Cards. Currently, there are 20 Lounges across 11 cities which are a part of the RuPay
Lounge Access Program.
In our continuous endeavour to support the Banks & benefit RuPay Cardhoiders, we wish to
inform you that we have added 4 new lounges across 4 new cities under RuPay Lounge
the said Program effective 01st March 2020.
In addition to above, effective 01st March 2020, we shall also be adding 4 more lounges which
shall be exclusively for RuPay Select Cardhoiders only. Since these lounges will be exclusively
for RuPay Select Cardholders, RuPay Platinum Cardholders will not get complimentary
enclosed as Annexure A.
We request mernber Banks to take note of the same and bring the contents of this Circular to
the notice of relevant staff.
