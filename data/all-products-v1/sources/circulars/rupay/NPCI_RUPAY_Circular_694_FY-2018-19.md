Sub:Enhancements in RuPay Global Clearing & Settlement System for RuPay PoS &e-Comm
DomesticandInternational transactions (RGCS&iRGCS)
We have made enhancements in RuPayGlobal Clearing and Settlement System for RuPay Pos&
e-Comm domestic and international transactions (RGCS & iRGCs) to facilitate member banks in
effectivelyhandling their dayto day operational activity.
Thefollowingenhancementshavebeen implementedinRGCS&iRGCS.
Revision inDispute Document upload size in RGCS&iRGCS
Member banks need to upload documents as part of dispute management process at every stage of
thedisputelifecycleinRGCS&iRGCS.
Wehaverevisedthedocumentuploadsizefrom200kbto500kb.
Member banks will now be in a position to upload documents upto 5oo kb per document for all
dispute life cycles in RGCS&iRGCS.
Revisionin Filestagingcutoverin RGCS
At the file staging cutover outbound files submitted by Member banks consisting of, SMs Tip &
Surchargeadjustments,Credit/Debit adjustments, various disputecycle messages,DMs
presentments, refunds etc that are submitted to RGcs will be considered for clearing and
settlement.This cutover doesn't have any holiday considerations and would occur on all calendar
days. Messages submitted after this cutover will be considered for clearing in the subsequent
clearing cycle.
In order to facilitate earlygeneration of settlement files & reports in RGCS,we have revised thefile
stagingcutover inRGCSfromtheexisting0600hrsISTtoO500hrsIST.
With this revision the settlement fund transfer and process of generation of settlement files &
reports for Menber banks in Rccsis completedwell in time so that Member banks can handle daily
reconciliationeffectively.

Merging&Discontiuationof SettlementReportsin RGCS
We have merged &discontinued few settlement reports n RGCS for RuPayPoS&e-Comm domestic
transactions. Discontinuation & Merging of reports was discussed and approved by the RuPay
OperationsWorkingGroupMeetingheldonMay26,2017.
We have discontinued only those settlement reports which were duplicate and the details of which
arealsoavailableinanalternatereport.
E.g. There were three formats of DsR report (Daily Settlement Report) that was being generated
dailyin RGCS such as:
DSR Report
Gross DSRReport
DSR Summary Report
DSR Summary Report is a detailed report which is continued whereas DSR Report & Gross DSR
Report which were duplicatehave beendiscontinued for Sponsor as well as Sub-member banks.
This exercise has benefitted Member banks as the time for generation of reports is reduced and
daily settlement files & reports are made available to Member banks well in time to handle their
day-to-dayreconciliation and operationalactivities.
Member banks are requested to note that settlement files & reports are being purged in RGcS and
these are available PID (Participant ID) wise only for 3 months prior to current date.
The details of reports merged & discontinued aregiven in Annexure A for reference.
We request you to take note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein to
officials concerned.
