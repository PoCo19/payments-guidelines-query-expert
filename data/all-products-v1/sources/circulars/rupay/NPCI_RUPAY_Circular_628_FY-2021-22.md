RuPay Insurance Program FY 2021-22-RuPay Premium Cards
1. We wish to inform that the RuPay Insurance Program for RuPay Premium cards has
been extended for financial year 2021-22, j.e. from April 1, 2021 up to March 31, 2022.
TATA AlG General Insurance Company Ltd shall continue to be the Insurance partner
with NPCI for RuPay Premium cards.
2. All terms and conditions along with the claims process pertaining to RuPay Insurance
Program for RuPay Premium cards for FY 2021-22 will remain the same as mentioned
in the Circular: RuPay/2020-21/RuPay/048 dated 27th March'2020.
