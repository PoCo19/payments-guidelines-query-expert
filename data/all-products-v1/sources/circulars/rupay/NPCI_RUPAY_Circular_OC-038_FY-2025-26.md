Subject – Implementation of NRP & revision in PRD process for RuPay  
RuPay, India’s domestic card payment network has an established dispute resolution framework 
to ensure timely and fair redressal of customer disputes. To enhance process efficiency, expedite 
customer issue resolution, and bring the discipline within the RuPay ecosystem, a revised process 
has been formulated. Under this new framework, the revised lifecycle will be as follows 
i) Chargeback Raised – Accept/Re-Presentment/Deemed. 
ii) Pre-Arbitration Raised – Accept/Re-Presentment/Deemed. 
iii) Arbitration Raised – NRP Verdict. 
iv) PRD Raised – PRD Verdict. 
The stakeholder against whom the verdict has been given by NRP can appeal to PRD, if not 
satisfied even with PRD verdict, then appeal to RBI as appellate authority. Detailed flow of dispute 
rules and lifecycle is provided in Annexure A, B, C & D. 
Further to the changes detailed above, revised fee structure shall be implemented for both NRP 
and PRD processes as per the details provided below: 
• 
NPCI Review Panel (NRP) Processing Fees of Rs.500 + GST per arbitration. 
• 
Panel for Resolution of Disputes (PRD) Processing Fees of Rs.3,000 + GST per 
arbitration. 
This shall be effective for the chargeback cases raised from September 30, 2025. 
Member banks are advised to make necessary arrangements to handle the chargebacks 
(disputes) accordingly. The information contained herein may please be disseminated to the 
officials concerned.

a) NPCI Review Panel (NRP) 
• 
The NRP consists of three NPCI officials, including the Panel 
Chairperson. 
• 
NRP reviews cases based solely on the documents and 
supporting evidence submitted by member banks during the 
chargeback and pre-arbitration stages and issues a verdict 
accordingly. 
• 
An NRP processing fee of INR 500 + GST is charged per 
case from the participant against whom the verdict is passed. 
• 
If the losing party is not satisfied with the NRP verdict, they 
may escalate the case to the PRD. 
• 
Reversal of NRP fees will occur in the following scenarios: 
1. If the losing party in NRP escalates to PRD and 
the PRD verdict is in their favor – the NRP fee (INR 
500 + GST) previously debited will be reversed and 
recovered from the counterparty. In such cases, the 
participant against whom the PRD verdict is passed 
will bear both the NRP fee (INR 500 + GST) and the 
PRD fee (INR 3,000 + GST). 
2. If the PRD verdict is accepted by the other party – 
the NRP fee (INR 500 + GST) earlier debited from the 
NRP losing party will be reversed and charged to the 
party accepting the PRD decision. 
b) Panel for Resolution of Disputes (PRD) 
• 
The PRD consists of five members: four nominated by direct 
member banks (with at least 25% representation from 
Steering Committee members) and one NPCI official as 
Chairperson. 
• 
A PRD processing fee of INR 3,000 + GST per case is 
charged from the losing party (in addition to NRP fees, 
wherever applicable). 
• 
The PRD reviews the cases based on the documents and 
supporting evidence submitted by member banks during the 
chargeback and pre-arbitration stages and delivers its verdict 
accordingly. 
c) RBI (Appellate Authority)
