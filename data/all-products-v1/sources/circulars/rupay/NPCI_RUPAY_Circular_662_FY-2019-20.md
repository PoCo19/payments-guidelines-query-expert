# 27-Nov-2019 - RuPay/2019-20/025 - SMS Communication for Offline transaction of National Common Mobility Card (NCMC)

Date: 1st February, 2019

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
Circular/RuPay/2019-20/025 November27,2019
To,
AllMembers-RuPay
DearMadam/Sir,
Subject:SMS Communication for offline transactions of National Common Mobility Card
(NCMC)
This is with reference to the Circular/RuPay/2018-19/035 dated 1st February, 2019 (enclosed as
Annexure A) for issuer banks to send alert to the customer for NCMC offline transaction as soon
as transaction details are received by the issuer.
Based on the confirmation received from RBl, members to take note that:
It is left to the discretion of the issuer, to either send a single SMs providing summary of
all the transit offline payments during the day; or
Send a unique message for each offline transit payment.
Request members to bring the contents of the circular to the notice of all relevant staff.
Yours faithfully,
Praveena Rai
