ThisisinreferencetotheCircular:RuPay/03/2015-16(enclosedasAnnexureA)dated10thApril
2015 regarding incremental cards linked to Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)accounts
tobeissuedonadistinctRuPay lIN.
PMJDY,thefinancialinclusionschemeunderwhichtheGovernmentof Indiaintendstoprovide
every household with a bank account and RuPay Debit Card. The scheme was launched by
Honourable Prime Minister in the month of Aug 2014. As on July 2018, over 240+ million RuPay
Debit cards issued underPMJDY.
NPCI had issued separate lIN for RuPay PMJDY Card to the member banks. It may be noted that
initiallymanybankshaveissuedPMJDYcardsontheirexistingRuPayClassicllNs.Further,ithas
been observed that some of the Banks still issuing PMDY cards on the IINs assigned for RuPay
Classic. Hence, it becomes very difficult to categories RuPay PMJDY data in terms of card
issuance, activation, transaction and usage.
Inviewthereof,the issuers shouldadhere to thefollowing guidelines:
1.:All incremental cards, linked to PMJDY account must be issued on distinct RuPay PMJDY
IIN only.
2.Banks whichhave been issueda distinct lIN for PMJDY Card will continueto use the same.
As Banks are re-carding their exiting magnetic stripe debit cards to EMV based debit cards,
PMJDY Cards issued on existing RuPay Classic lINs should be re-carded with distinct
PMJDY IINs only.
3.All new card design must be approved only using uniquellN'sissued forissuing card linked
toPMJDYaccounts.
4.BankswhichhaveissuedPMJDYcardsunderRuPayClassicllNshouldensurethatthedata
for the same is maintained and reported under the PMJDY cards.
Werequestmembersto bringthecontents of this circulartothenoticeof all relevant staff and
ensure compliancetothe above mentionedguidelines.
