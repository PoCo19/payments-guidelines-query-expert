Dear Sir/Madam,
BHlM Aadhaar Pay (BAP) service allows merchants to accept digital payments from
customers using Aadhaar biometric authentication. The obligation to acquire, manage and
monitor Merchants for BHIM Aadhaar Pay rests with Acquiring Member Banks.
Acquiring Member Bank shall continue to be responsible for merchants onboarded by them
directly or through aggregators/ partners. Hence a proper process on the merchant acquisition
risks. In this regard, NPCl is providing reference guidelines (Ref Annexure A) on merchant
acquisition and Merchant transactions.
Reference is given to Acquiring Member Bank's key risk responsibilities and accountabilities
when managing merchants. These reference guidelines are to be read in conjunction with any
regulatory directions in this regard.
