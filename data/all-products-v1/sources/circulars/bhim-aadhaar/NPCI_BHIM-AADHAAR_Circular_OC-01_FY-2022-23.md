To,
All Members of the Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS).
Subject: Standardization in McC (Merchant category codes)for BHiM Aadhaar
Pay
Dear Sir/Madam,
BHiM Aadhaar Pay(BAP) allows merchants to accept digital payments from customers
using the Aadhaar biometric authentication.
As per the current implementation of BHIM Aadhar Pay by the members, acquiring
banks send MCC in the BHIM Aadhaar Pay online transaction request. However, the
MCC value is set to a default value of ‘6012' for all merchants, thereby creating
considerable difficuity in classifying merchants into specific categories based on the
type of business OR trade or services supplied by the merchants.
In order to ease the process of classifying merchants and tracking BHIM Aadhar Pay
transactions, all the members are advised to ensure that the appropriate MCc is
passed in the online transaction request as per the specifications defined by Iso
(International Organisation for Standardization).
All members are advised to implement the above instructions of sending the
appropriate MCC for BHIM Aadhar Pay in online request to AePS (NPCI) latest by 28th
February 2022.
