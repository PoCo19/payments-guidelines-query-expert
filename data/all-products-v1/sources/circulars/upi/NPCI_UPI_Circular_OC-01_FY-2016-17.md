AllOurMemberBanks
Dear All,
UPl was launched with member banks on the 11th of April 2016. Post the initial launch,
member banks had agreed to ready the Apps functionally and open up the testing in a
controlled environment to their staff (1oo) to begin with. We have been working with the
banks to enable the Applications and the Closed Usergroup testing has been continued in the
productionenvironment,since.
With the Public Launch imminent during the present month, it is only prudent that the Banks
open up theirApps to internal staff (At least 1o0o)basis our earliercommunication issued on
June 23, 2o16.This will ensure that the Bank customers can use their own Apps with their
own accounts (Onus linking)and also evolveways to have the Apps of other banks shared
with their internal employees for (off-us linking). This exercise will help on the following 2
counts:
1) Acquaint the employees of banks at large with the Apps / Flows and the
functionalities.
2) Complete Financial / Non-Financial transactions and check if the transactions are
seamless. Any specific issue can be highlighted to the Internal IT teams and corrective
actions be taken, avoiding customer raising issues post public launch.
The testing must include downloading app, creating profile, adding account and performing
UPItransactions.
Banks may please to submit the report on enabling 1ooo users and the corresponding
feedbacktoNPClattheearliest.
