Subject: Enhancement of UPI Autopay
To provide flexibility for users and merchants, enhancement in the existing UPl Autopay framework has been
introduced. The enhanced framework shall allow users to view all their active UPl autopay mandates in any
app of their choice. Users shall also have an option to port mandates from one UPl App to another. It shall
also allow merchants to port and execute UPl autopay mandates through their preferred Payee PsP as
deemed fit.
In addition to extant guidelines, members are required to adhere with the following requirements:
1. Payer PsP banks/ UPl apps:
Payer PsPs and UPl apps shall ensure complete lifecycle management, including viewing and
porting of user's UPl Autopay mandates on the respective app only under the "Manage Bank
accounts' or the dedicated 'UPl Autopay' section. This will be in addition to existing operations
like revoke, pause, modify etc.
All payer-initiated UPl Autopay operations, as per existing guidelines, shall require UP! PIN.
The ‘Port Mandate' shall be strictly user-driven only from the mandate details page fetched with
View Mandate'. No inducements, including cashback, incentives, in-app notifications, banners,
nudges, alerts, any app based/non-app-based implementation etc., shall be employed to solicit
mandate portability.
iv. Payer PsPs and their UPl Apps shall not utilize mandate details obtained through the 'view
mandate' for any other purpose.
'Port Mandate' can be done only once in a rolling 90-day period.
2. Payee PsPs:
Payee PsP shall generate a unique Merchant ldentifier Code (MIC) for every merchant in a
standardized manner. This MlC shall be a mandatory parameter for all UP1 Autopay mandates
with purpose code ‘Az'.
Merchants can choose to execute UPl Autopay mandates through any UPl ID provided the
appropriate MIC and other mandate details are passed.
Merchants shall be able to update their existing UP! ID without alteration of the MIC and other
mandate parameters.
