To All UPI Members,
Subiect: Mandatory measures to be implemented by Bank, PsPs and TPAPs for UPl
To safeguard customer interest, it is essential that ecosystem members including Banks, PsPs and
TPAPs build enhanced controls and hygiene limits internally at their end as well for UPl transactions.
1. Separate Mobile App IDs for different Operating Systems (OS)
It has been observed that few members are having same UPI App ID for all of their UPI Apps which
are on different Operating Systems. It is hereby informed that UPI Apps shall have different App IDs
for their applications which are on different operating systems to have a clear demarcation on Os
specific actions that may be required from time to time.
2. Member to support RegValAdd API for Account Number + IFS transactions
prompt the customer to verify the beneficiary name before initiating the transaction. This is in line with
the RBI Payment Vision 2025 wherein it is emphasized the need for payment system to provide
beneficiary name before initiating the transactions and reduce complaints about credit to unintended
beneficiary due to inadvertent wrong account number entry. All members are required to make
necessary changes at their end to support the payee/beneficiary name verification.
It is also re-iterated that members shall ensure adherence to all UPl guidelines released by NPCl from
time to time which shall include various technical specifications, compliance requirements, etc. Members
shall ensure that practices which are detrimental to the UPl ecosystem and actions which are against the
spirit of existing regulatory guidelines shall not be extended by them including the UPl APl services
(Reference is drawn to NPCI/UPI/OC-95/2020-21). If the members are involved in any such practices,
NPCl will be constrained to take suitable action(s) as appropriate.
Members are required to take note of the same and comply on or before 31st December, 2023.
The contents of the circular are confidential and to be shared with relevant stakeholders only on a need
to know basis.
