Subject: Furthering the Interoperability in UPl ecosystem
UPl has become one of the most preferred digital payment mode for Indian citizens because
of customer experience of ease, security and interoperability. NPCl vide circular
NPCi/UP1/OC-73/2019-20 and NPCI/UPI/0C-142/2021-22 has outlined the approach of equal
opportunity to all apps to comply with UPi interoperability as the first principle. The circular
outlines the first set of compliances towards the objective of furthering the interoperability in
the UPl ecosystem.
UPIPaymentApps
1. To instil confidence in the customer that she/he can send money to a beneficiary using
any UPl app or scan any UPI QR code with any UPI application, it is advised to include
the text of: ‘Send Money to any UPl App' or 'Scan any UPI QR'] and display the UPIl logo
along with prominent apps'logos on send money and QR scan screens within the apps. It
is also advised to include the message in all customer and merchant communications (incl.
advertisements). (Refer UPl brand guidelines)
UPIOnline Merchants
2. UPl as a payment option should be clearly and prominently visible 'over and above' any
individual UPl app for the merchant payments especially for online transactions. To ensure
this, checkout standardisation for all UPl based merchant payments (One-time, Autopay
etc.) is being introduced as below:
I. App based and Mobile website based merchants
Under the UPl Section, the 'Pay by any UPl app' button should be the ‘first and prominent"
option which will call for UPl Intent. UPi Intent, when clicked, should display all the active
UPl apps without any discrimination.
all UPl apps and it should not be restricted to any specific app.
2. The deep/direct integrations, if any, shall only be permitted to appear after the Pay
by any UPlapp'option.
3. Once intent as a functionality is enabled in iOs (Apple manufactured devices).
