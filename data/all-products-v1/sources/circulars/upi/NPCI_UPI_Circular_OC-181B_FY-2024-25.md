All Member Banks of Unified Payments Interface (UPl)
Madam/Sir,
Subject: Addendum to OC181A- Implementation of UPl limits for P2P trans actions
Vide reference to NPCI/UP/OC 181/2023-24 dated 16th November 2023 and NPCI/UPI/OC
181A/2024-25 dated 7th June 2024 the limit for user to receive funds in their bank account via
P2P in 24 hours was defined. With reference to the same, we will have additional limit on count
of transactions via P2P regardless of the transaction value.
Sr Circular Reference &
Description
No Remarks
P2P credit is capped at 25 successful transactions OR
NPCI/UPI/OC 181A/2024-25
cumulative Rs 4 lacs in a period of 24 hours and
transactions < Rs 2,000 are excluded. (Existing - No change)
P2P credit will be capped at 50 successful transactions NPCI/UPI/OC181B/2024-25
in a period of 24 hours (New)
The mentioned changes shall be applicable with immediate effect and member banks are
required to ensure the limits are implemented latest by 30th September 2024.
