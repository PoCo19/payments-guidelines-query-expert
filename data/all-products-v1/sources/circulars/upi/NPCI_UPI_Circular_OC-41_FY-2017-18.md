Sub: UPl & IMPs - Process to handle chargeback for wrong account transfer
Obiective:
Objective of this circular is to explain the process to handle wrong credits in IMPs & UPI which are
initiated by the remitting bank customers due to typographical errors and resulting in wrong transfers
to unintended customer's account.
The above matter was discussed in the Steering Commitee Meeting (SCM) held on 12th May 2017.
The SCM members approved that remitting banks can raise chargeback in RGcs for wrong transfers
on 'Good Faith' basis.
Process:
RGcs if the customer submits that funds have been transferred to wrong customer's account.
RGCS shall provide “browse' option to upload indemnity.
2. For Beneficiary Banks: Beneficiary banks are requested to follow their internal process for
recovering funds from the wrong customer's account and accept the chargeback, wherever
applicable. Beneficiary banks may please use appropriate reason codes for accepting or
rejecting such chargebacks. If beneficiary bank does not accept or reject the chargeback
within TAT, then such chargeback will be treated as deemed accepted. The Beneficiary bank
will not be allowed to raise re-presentment after the TAT.
