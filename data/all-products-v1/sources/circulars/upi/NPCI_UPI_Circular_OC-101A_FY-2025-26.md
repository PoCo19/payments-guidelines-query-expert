Subject: Addendum to OC-101: Strengthening beneficiary name verification and
display during UPl transactions
Dear Members,
With reference to NPCI circular NPCI/UPI/OC No. 101/2020-21 dated on 21st February 2021,
NPCl has mandated ultimate beneficiary is to be showcased in UPl transaction. This shall
be displayed on the payer app as well as during display of transaction statement/history.
To instil confidence in customers that they are sending money to the correct beneficiary and
to avoid potential risk, the following changes need to be implemented by UPl apps:
1. Display of only Ultimate Beneficiary Name (CBs name) for P2P and P2PM
transactions: UPl applications shall ensure that only the ultimate beneficiary's name
(Banking name as fetched from Validate Address APl) is displayed to the user on the
pre-transaction details page. Names extracted from QR codes, user-defined names of
the payee, or any other logic should not be displayed to the payer in the Upl App.
2. Restrict user detail modification of beneficiary name: UPl Apps shall disable any
features allowing users to modify their Beneficiary Name' for transaction purposes in
the app interface.
The UPi ecosystem members shall adhere to above latest by 3oth June 2025, post which it
shall be treated as non-compliance.
