Subiect: Introduction of Auto Top-up on UPl LITE
This is in reference to NPCI circular NPC1/UPI/OC No. 138/2021-22, dated 16th March 2022 and
NPCl/UPI/OC No. 169/2023-24, dated 31st August 2023, whereby guidelines to operationalize
UPI LITE small value transactions have been introduced. UPI LITE enhances the user experience
by allowing PIN less transactions under 500 with a maximum UPI LITE balance limit of 2000 at
any point of time.
To further augment the UPI LITE user experience, Auto Top-up on UPI LITE is being introduced.
With this new feature, the UPl LITE balance will automatically be reloaded by an amount chosen
limit set by them. This increases the convenience and adaptability of UPI LiTE by automating the
process of reloading funds. Additionally, users shall be provided with the option of revoking the
Auto Top-up mandate at any point of time by the PSP/App.
Since the balance resides with user's bank and the UPI LITE can be deleted anytime with instant
refund of UPl L.ITE balance to user's main bank account, the requirement of pre-debit notification
(PDN), which is a usual mechanism for merchant mandate payments, has been dispensed with.
Members (Banks & PsP/App) are required to ensure below guidelines are followed
1. Issuer banks live on UPI LITE shall support the functionality of Auto Top-up on UPI LITE
wherein the banks should allow creation of UPl LiITE mandate and allow debits whenever
the request comes from the PSP/App.
2. UPl Apps shall support the necessary functionality and interface on their App for the
customer to use Auto Top-up on UPl LITE.
3. Members shall ensure that the mandate created successfully are executed successfully.
All the necessary validation should be present at the time of mandate creation.
4. PsP/Apps shall put necessary controls to limit the number of auto-replenishment
transactions to 5 per day per LITE account.
