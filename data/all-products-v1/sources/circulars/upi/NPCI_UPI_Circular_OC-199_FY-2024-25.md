Subject: Update to NPCI CL version1.8 or above
UPl has become a preferred digital payment mode across the country. It provides ease of use,
convenience and superior user experience with security and interoperability. To ensure the safety
and security of the UPl systems, NPCl constantly reviews the security checks of all components
in the UPl ecosystem and regularly upgrades them to address evolving threats in cyber
landscape.
There are security checks implemented as part of the code embedded in the Common Library
(CL) along with the feature release to enhance user experience on UPl platforms. Integration of
latest versions of CL is important to ensure enhanced security and safety in UPl ecosystem.
With this objective, NPCI will be retiring the support for CL prior to Version 1.8 after 3oth
September 2024.
To ensure compliance PsP Banks shall certify and enable CL 1.8 or above and their respective
UPl Apps shall integrate the latest version of CL within their application, on or before 3oth
September 2024.
Members are requested to take note of this compliance and communicate it to relevant
stakeholders and their respective partners.
