Payer App Behavior for Intent Based Transaction on UPl
Intent call based implementation is one of the most preferred use case in the UPl merchant
ecosystem owing to below reasons:
a) It ensures UPl Interoperability
b) Provides seamtess checkout experience to the customer
c) It provides flexibity to a customer to pay using UPl ID of his choice
Further, NPCl UPI Circular 15 C mandates UPI enabled apps to respond to intent call initiated
from merchant app on the same phane. However it has been observed that UPl enabled apps
are responding to the intent call even when the customer is not registered on the app. Once
customer selects that UPl app (where he is not registered for UPl but has downloaded on his
phone) he is prompted for UPl registratian to complete payment. This feads to timeouts and hence
low success rates.
that UPl enabled Apps must respond to Intent call from merchant App only in cases where the
customer has registered & has also SET UPl PiN for the specific App, This was suggested to
avoid clutter on the screen and also increase success rates for merchant transactions.
Member Banks are requested io make requisite changes on their Apps and the TPAPs sponsored
by the banks to align with the guidelies as stated above, latest by 31st of October 2019.
