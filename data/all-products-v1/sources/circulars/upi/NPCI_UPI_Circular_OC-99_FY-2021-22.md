Madam / Dear Sir,
Sub: Enabling non- compliance penalty in IMPS & UPl - effective 15th February 2021,
We refer to IMPS & UPI Working Group Meeting held on 27th Nov 2020 & 16th Dec 2020, wherein, we have
communicated that NPCl will start non-compliance penalty tentatively in Jan-2021.
In view of the above, we wish to update that w.e.f. 15th February 2021, non-compliance penalty shall be
implemented for IMPs & UPl on all pending transactions (refer below table for details) if the status is not
updated in BCS/URCS as per the TATs & process defined herein. In case of any deferment in go-live date,
we will inform ail member banks through email notification.
Corresponding OC for references: - NPCl/IMPS/OC No. 65/2015-16 dated 24th November 2015,
NPCI/UPl/OC No. 19/2017-18 dated 22nd May 2017, NPC1/UPI/OC No. 38/2017-18 dated 02nd November
2017&NPCl/UPl/OCNo.42/2017-18dated15thDecember2017.
