All MemberBanks,UnifiedPayments Interface (UPl)
Dear Sir / Madam,
Subject:To reduce the high Technical declines & Business declines.
UPI transactions are gearing up day by day with all the support provided by Member banks
within the ecosystem. Initially UPl started with 21 member banks which have now been
increased to 55 member banks out of which 38 banks are live as a PSP and 17 banks are live
as an issuer only. As the volumes are picking up, stability and efficiency are the main quality
aspects which are desired by the higher authorities to provide better services to customers
all overthe country.
However, in the interim NPCl has observed that with the increase in volume of UPl
transactions, there is an increase in the percentage of Technical declines and Business
declines within the ecosystem.
Further, we also refer to RBI letter DPSS.cO.0D.No. 683 / 06.08.001 / 2017-18 dated August
14th, 2017 under the subject Update on UPI for the month of July 2017 (Annexed). It has
been communicated that RBl has taken a note on high technical and business declines and
thus technical declines cannot be more than 1% ("for transactions and Meta APl") for any
bank to participate in UPl or is already a part of UPl. As NPCl is already initiating appropriate
measures to monitor and progressively bring down the technical declines to less than 1% on
continuous basis, member banks herewith are also requested to take the necessary measures
and maintain/bring down the technical declines to less than 1%.
Further, to bring down the high business declines member banks are advised to initiate
measures forcustomertraining/awareness.
The above is for your kind consideration and needful action.
