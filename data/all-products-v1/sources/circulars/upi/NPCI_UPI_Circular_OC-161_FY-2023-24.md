Sub: Extension to UPI circular NPCl/UPl/OC No.60/208-19 Crediting/Debiting Non-
Resident (NR) accounts in UPl
We invite reference to our circular NPCl/UPI/OC.No 60/2018-19 dated 15th October, 2018 on
"Crediting/Debiting Non-Resident (NR) accounts in UPI and IMPS domestic transaction"
wherein it has been permitted to transfer funds between NRE accounts and other perrnissible
accounts as per the extant FEMA regulation and adherence to the guidelines/instruction
issued by concerned regulatory departments of RBl from time to time.
We have been receiving requirement from the eco-system with regards to the Non-resident
(NR) accounts and other permissible accounts having international numbers to be allowed to
transact in UPl. And there has been customer demand in the ecosystem to enable UPl for
their NR accounts have international number and experience the seamless and instant journey
of UPI.
Accordingly, we would like to advise to the member of the UPl eco-system the following
instructions:
1. Non-Resident account types like NRE/NRO accounts having international mobile
numbers shall be allowed to get on-boarded/transact in UPl, provided:
a. Member banks to ensure such types of accounts are only allowed as per the
extant FEMA regulations and adherence to the guidelines/instructions issued
by the concerned regulatory departments of Reserve Bank of India from time
to time.
b. All the necessary Anti-Money Laundering (AML)/ Combating of Financing of
Terrorism (CFT) checks and compliance validation/account level validations
as per the extent rules applicable under the regulatory guidelines shall be the
responsibility of the remitter/beneficiary banks.
