Subiect: Introduction of new features for Credit Accounts
Referring to NPCI/2022-23/RuPay/019A and NPCI/UPI/OC No. 171/2023-24, RuPay credit
cards and pre-sanctioned credit line can now be linked on UPl, enabling safe and secure
their credit cards and pre-sanctioned credit line, and merchants have benefitted from the
increase in consumption by being part of the credit ecosystem with acceptance of credit
accounts using QR codes. To further enrich the offerings to customers and merchants on
credit accounts (RuPay Credit Card on UPl and Credit Line on UPl), the following new features
are now introduced:
1. EMl: User shall be able to apply for an EMl facility on the linked credit accounts on Upl
app. UPI App shall take UPI PIN as user consent on acceptance of relevant terms &
conditions. This shall enable the user to convert their:
i. Past purchases, performed using credit account into EMI through the linked UPI App
from transaction history
ii. Purchases into EMl at the time of making the transaction.
The UPl app shall display the existing EMl's in the relevant section. Since the EMl shall be
termed as loan, the user shall not be allowed to modify / delete the EMl, except the foreclosure,
as per terms of the Issuer entity.
2. Credit Account Bill Payment/ Instalment Payment: This shall enable user to make one-
time payment or setup UPi AutoPay to pay outstanding credit card account bill/ credit line
instalment for self and others using UPl app. To do this,
i. Payer PsP shall create UPl ID for receiving credit account bill/ instalment payment.
UPl App shali provide a view for the user to access the details of linked credit card
outstanding bil/ credit line instalment details (minimum amount due, total amount due,
bill due date etc).
Issuer bank shall clear the dues in real-time upon receiving payments, and make the
enhanced balance availabie to the user for usage.

With the objective of fulfilling user needs and enhancing the user convenience by providing
