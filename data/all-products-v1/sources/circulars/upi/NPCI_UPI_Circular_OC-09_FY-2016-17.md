3) We may therefore recommend as under for UPl reconciliation:
a) Member banks should download "UPI settlement files and raw data files" from NPCI portal
on daily basis after every settlement cycle.
b) Member Banks should do a three way reconciliation using bank's UPI switch data, CBS data
and UPl raw data. Member banks should consider UPi raw data file as the base file for
reconciliation.
c) Member banks should ensure to handle'exceptional scenarios' as per the NPCI'Operating &
Settlementguidelines'.
d) Member banks should raise the customer complaints received on PSP App and also respond
to complaints raised by other PSPs as per compliance defined in UPl 'Operating and
settlementguidelines'.
You may please make a note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein to all
officials concerned to set up automated daily reconciliation process.
