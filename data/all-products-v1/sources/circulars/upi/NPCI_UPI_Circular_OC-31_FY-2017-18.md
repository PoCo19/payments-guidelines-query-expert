All Member Banks - Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and BHIM App 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Subject: UPI – Inclusion of UPI Banks in BHIM Merchant Cashback and Referral Bonus Scheme 
We invite reference to our Circular No: NPCI/UPI/OC No.27/2017-18 dated August 18th, 2017 on inclusion 
of UPI member banks in BHIM Merchant Cashback and Referral Bonus Scheme.  
The member banks were advised to confirm inclusion of BHIM in their PSP name and other changes as per 
the aforesaid Circular to NPCI on or before 25th August, 2017. Confirmation from the member banks on or 
before 25th August, 2017 was considered eligible for the Schemes from 1st September, 2017. However, 
based on requests from the member banks, we have taken into consideration all confirmations received 
on or before 1st September, 2017 for the Scheme to go live from 1st September, 2017 onwards instead of 
25th August 2017 as indicated in the above mentioned circular. 
 
Going forward member banks need to confirm this enablement by 20th of every month to be part of 
scheme from the next month (eg: confirmation of change by 20th September 2017, will be considered as 
part of the scheme from 1st October 2017 onwards and so on). Banks are expected to confirm via email 
on brand@npci.org.in as well as to respective NPCI Relationship Managers along with screen shots. The 
disbursement of scheme amount shall be done in line with the existing process as followed in BHIM 
For further details, please refer the above mentioned NPCI Circular.
