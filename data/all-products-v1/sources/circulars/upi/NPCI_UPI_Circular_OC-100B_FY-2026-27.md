With reference to NPC! circutars NPCl/UPl/OC-100/2020-21, NPCl/UP//OC-100A/2022-23
and NPCI/UPI/OC-190/2023-24, this circular outlines revised BHIM UPI Merchant QR Brand
Guidelines to standardise the customer experience while scanning merchant QR. The updated
brand guidelines are enclosed in Annexure A of this circular and shall be applicabie in
accordance with the timelines specified below:
1) All new Merchant QR codes issued after 1st December 2026 shall mandatorily be
compliant with the updated BHIM UPI Merchant QR Brand Guidelines.
2) All existing Merchant QR codes which are being replaced from 1st December 2026 shall
mandatorily be compliant with the updated BHIM UPI Merchant QR Brand Guidelines.
3) QR code designs are required to be approved by NPCl before implementation (approval
process is enclosed in Annexure B).
Acquiring Banks/ PsPs are hereby requested to take note of the above and communicate it to
relevant stakeholders and their partners to ensure timely compliance.
