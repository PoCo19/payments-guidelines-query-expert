Subject: Addendum to "Aadhaar OTP Authentication in lieu of Debit Card for Customer.
Onboarding on Unified Payments Interface (UPl) “on revised timeline.
NPCI introduced NPCI/UPI/OC-116/2021-22 dated 8th September 2021 with subject -"Aadhaar
OTP Authentication in lieu of Debit Card for Customer Onboarding on Unified Payments Interface
(UPl) “to enable customer onboarding on UPI using Aadhaar +OTP, in addition to debit card.
Along with the feature details and guidelines, compliance timeline was mentioned as 15th
December, 2021. Due to delay in readiness owning to other priority product feature launch across
ecosystem, compliance timelines are extended to 15th March, 2022.
All UPI Member Banks, PsPs and Third Party App Providers to take a note of this and adhere to
revised timelines
