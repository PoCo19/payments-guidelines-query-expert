DearSir/Madam,
Reminder on "Final implementation timelines for "oDR- Enhancing Complaint
handling&ResolutionprocessforallUPlusers”
Reference drawn to RBI Circular No. RBl/2020-21/21 DPSS.CO.PD No.116/02.12.004/2020-
21 dated August 06, 2020, on Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) System for Digital Payments
and NPCi Circular No. NPCl/UPi/OC - 145/2022-23 dated 11th April, 2022 on implementing
UDIR by all Banks, PSPs and TPAPs for all the UPI users latest by 3otrh September, 2022.
Following the issuance of the aforesaid circular, it has been observed that many members
have gone live on issuer / PsP / Apps, while few members are yet to provide UDiR
functionalities including financial legs of the transaction (i.e. credit processing and reversal,
wherever applicable) to their customers and on their UPl App. Also members live / ready need
to enable end to end transaction including credit processing.
All member banks/ PSPs/ TPAPs participating in UPI are advised to provide this facility
September, 2022. For non-complied member banks/ PsPs / Apps, new customer onboarding
shall be stopped effective from 1st October, 2022. This is also applicable for multi-bank TPAPs
and they shall only be permitted to onboard new customers ONLY on enabled PsPs post this
date.
Thus, NPCl again advises all UPl participants to make this functionality available well before
3oth September, 2022. Members are also advised to bring to the notice of their partners at an
appropriate level.
