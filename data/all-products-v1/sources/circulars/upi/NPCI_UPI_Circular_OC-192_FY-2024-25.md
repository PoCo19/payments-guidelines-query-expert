Vide circular NPC/UPl/0C-70/2019-20 dated 17th June 2019, a new category "P2PM" catering to
small merchants and unorganised retail sector was introduced to bring small merchants with low ticket
size into the digital framework along with the onboarding guidelines required to be followed by
Acquirers. Further, vide circular NPC1/UP/OC-72/2019-20 dated 30th August 2019, NPCl shared
revised guidelines for P2PM category on changes in monthly inward credit Jimits and stated that P2PM
category is applicable oniy for merchants receiving instant credit for UPl payments into their own
account.
In addition to the guidelines referred above and considering the constantly expanding merchant
ecosystem;
1. Acquiring Banks / PsPs shall ensure implementation of the maximum UPl inward credit limits
allowed for P2PM merchants as prescribed below and confirm cornpliance before 3oth April 2024.
Particulars Maximum UPI inward credit to P2PM merchant VPA (in Rs.)
Per transaction Rs. 10,000
Cumulative per day [24 hours] Rs. 25,000
Cumulative per month [30 days] Rs. 1,00,000
2. Merchants with UPl payment inward credit of Rs. 1,00,000 per month or more consecutively for
three months must be formally acquired by the Payee PsP under P2M category with applicable
MCC.
NPCl reserves the right to penalize and take necessary action against the Acquiring Banks / PsPs in
case of non-compliance.
