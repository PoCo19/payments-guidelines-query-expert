Subject:  Change in cutover cycles in UPI – (w.e.f – 28th January 2019)  
 
NPCI implemented the process of four settlements for UPI effective December 2016. 
1) NPCI cuts over the transactions for the 4th settlement cycle during the weekdays at 
17:30 hours at present and funds settlement for this cutover are completed as per 
the settlement processing in RBI RTGS system on the same day.  In cases of public 
holidays and RTGS holidays, NPCI carries out fund settlement/s on the next working 
day. 
2) Volumes in UPI have seen significant growth in the recent past. We have also received 
inputs from members with regard to few delays observed in the settlement timelines 
for 4th cycle. The transaction cut-over for 4th cycle being 17:30 hrs and in view of the 
exponential growth in the transaction volume, the back office system generating 
settlement files have taken more time for the 4th cycle. 
3) In order to bring in further efficiencies in settlement process, we herewith advise 
change in the transaction cutover for the settlement cycles in UPI as per the below 
table so that settlement processing are carried out on the same day (except on

4) NPCI shall continue to create the settlement files over the week-end where RTGS is 
not available and post settlements on the next working day.  
5) May we submit that NPCI is in the process of migrating to a new back office system 
for UPI which has enhanced processing capability. We expect the new back office

6) Please note that this change shall be effective from 28th of January 2019.  
 
In addition to the above, we wish to highlight that NPCI shall be providing four raw files and
