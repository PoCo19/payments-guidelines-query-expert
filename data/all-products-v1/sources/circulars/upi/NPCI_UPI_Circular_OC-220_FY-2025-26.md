# UPI | OC No. 220 | FY 2025-26 | Discontinuing the service of UPI Collect Request for Person to Person (P2P) transactions

Circular/reference number: NPCI/UPI/OC/220/2025-26
Date: 29th July, 2025

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI ALWAYS
FORWRD
NATIONAL PAYMENTSCORPORATION OFINDIA
NPCI/UPI/OC/220/2025-26 29th July, 2025
To
All UPi Member Banks, PSP's and Third-PartyApp Providers
Dear Sir/Madam,
Subject: Discontinuing the service of UPI Collect Request for Person to Person (P2P) transactions
With reference to circular no. NPCI/UP/2019-20/OC/76 dated 31st October 2019 on Revision in transaction
limits based on merchant & transaction types, NPCl released guidelines on transaction limits for Person to
Person (P2P) Collect. Further it is hereby informed that by 1st October 2025 UPI P2P Collect shall not be
allowed to be processed in UPl.
All member banks, Payment Service Providers (PsPs) and UPl Apps are hereby directed to implement the
necessary changes in their systems and operational processes to ensure that no P2P Collect transaction is
initiated, routed, or processed on UPi beyond 1st October 2025.
