All Member Banks, Unified Payment Interface (UPI)
Madam / Dear Sir,
Subject: Disabling Interchange Fee movement in dispute & adiustment life cycles for UPI Domestic
Transactions
This has reference to the member banks request submitted to NPCI for disabling Interchange fee + GST in
disputes & adjustmnent life cycles to limit raising of credit & debit notes under GST Law. NPCI had put up the
member banks request in Steering Committee Meeting (SCM) held on 10th Mar 2021, the committee has thereby
agreed and approved the same. Please take note of the below list consisting of life cycles for which Interchange
Fee + GsT movement will be disabled:
S. No Dispute /Adjustment Type S. No Dispute /Adjustment Type
Wrong Credit Chargeback Pre Arbitration Acceptance
Wrong Credit Chargeback Re- Deferred Chargeback Acceptance/ Deemed Acceptance
Presentment
Chargeback Deferred Pre Arbitration Acceptance / Deemed
Acceptance
Re-presentment RET
Fraud Chargeback Credit Adjustment
Fraud Chargeback Re-
Presentment
Disabling of Interchange fee + GST shall be applicable on the aforesaid list from 1st January 2023 (settlement
date) onwards. There will be no reversal of Interchange fee + GsT on disputes/ adjustments raised prior to this
date i.e. 1 st January 2023. Effective date of the aforesaid changes will be Is January 2023.
Please note, there will be no change in the report formats however Interchange & GsT values will be
shown as zero.
Member banks are requested to take a note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein to the
concerned officials.
