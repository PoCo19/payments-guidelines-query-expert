# Circular 68 – Levy of Surcharge by Merchants & Channel partners prohibited on UPI

Circular/reference number: NPCI/UPI/OC-68/2019-20

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCU
NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/UPI/OC-68/2019-20 May 16, 2019
All Member Banks (PSPs and Third Party Applications) - UPl
Respected Sir/Madam,
Levy of Surcharge by Merchants & Channel partners prohibited in UPl
It is observed that some banks / bank partners / merchants are passing surcharge to
customers opting for UPl payment mode. The practice of surcharge is prohibitive in nature
and we therefore wish to advise as under:
a) UPl must be treated at par with any other debit payment modes such as debit card,
NEFT, Net banking at all times.
b) No surcharge to be levied to customers for UPl transaction.
) Any 'Convenience fees' if charged must be mentioned upfront to the customer on the
merchant payment page/s prior to selection of 'Payment mode'.
d) Such 'Convenience fees' must be consistent for available debit payment modes (i.e.
Net banking, Debit card and UPI etc.).
e) NPCl reserves the right to take appropriate action against the Sponsor Bank for any
act of non-compliance to the above stated.
Member banks are advised to take note of the same and cascade this information to the
relevant stakeholders.
Yours Faithfully
Praveena Rai
Chief Operating Officer.
