Sub: UPI – Enhanced penalty for not updating TCC, RET, DRC & RRC after T+1 working day 
 
Objective: It has been observed that few banks are not updating the status in RGCS even after T+1 working 
day for Deemed Approved transactions, un-responded reversals, credit adjustments & returns (i.e. TCC, 
RET, DRC & RRC), which are resulting  in customer complaints. 
 
The above matter was discussed by member banks in a meeting held on 13th Nov 2017.  It was deliberated 
and approved therein to levy enhanced penalty (please refer below table for details) in addition to the 
existing penalty i.e. Rs.25+GST so as to ensure proper handling of transactions by banks and avoid customer 
complaints.

i. 
Note: The above process will be reviewed after three months. If there is no further improvement, 
then we may propose Rs.25 + GST every day till the status is not updated in RGCS. 
ii. 
Applicability: The above penalty is applicable for UPI transactions (i.e. TCC, RET, DRC & RRC). 
iii. 
Effective Date: The penalty, as aforesaid, will be implemented with effect from 15th Jan., 2018. 
All members are requested to take a note of the above and ensure to put in place proper process so as to 
ensure compliance to NPCI guidelines of handling transactions. Kindly disseminate the information 
contained herein to the officials concerned.
