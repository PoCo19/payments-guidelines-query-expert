From 1st January 2022, it will be mandatory for all UPl enabled feature phone based solutions to
conform to interoperability standards as per circular NPCI/UPl/OC-115/2021-22, dated 20oth July,
2021.
NPCI has introduced Server-Side Common Library (S-CL) for secure transaction authentication
functionality as it is done in the existing UPl common library for UPl Apps. Member Banks and
Payment Service Providers (PsP) are required to enable the following processes:
ensure interoperable payment option to the user even in the absence of smart phone based
app. All UPl payment requests from the customer interface will be routed to UPI via the
Payment Service Provider (PsP), who will host the S-CL and be responsible for maintaining
it securely. The PsP can provide the S-CL directly or extend its usage to a third party service
provider, who has been on-boarded by the PsP after suitable risk and security evaluation.
i. Transaction limit check: Currently, the maximum transaction limit for feature phone and
voice based payments cannot exceed Rs. 10,000/-.
ii.  Implementation of UPI Number functionality through integration with UPl Numeric ID Mapper.
