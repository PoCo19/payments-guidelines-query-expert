Subject: Addendum to circular NPCl/UPl/OC-115/2021-22 on revised timeline
NPCI had introduced UPI ID Mapper vide NPCI/UPI/OC-115/2021-2022 dated 20th July 2021 with
subject- Rollout of Numeric UPI ID Mapper' to enable ‘UPl Number' with the aim to accelerate
adoption of feature phone based UPl solutions and bring interoperability in mobile number based
UPl payments. Pilot phase was planned for eco-system from 1st Oct, 2021 to 1st Dec, 2021 with
maximum of 5 million seeding per app. Further, timelines for pilot was extended to expand the
coverage (details in addendums NPCI/UPI/OC-115B/2021-2022 dated gth Dec 2021 and
NPCl/UPl/OC-115C/2021-2022 dated 28th Feb 2022). Thereafter, Pilot was successfully executed
by 15th May 2022 with 12 milion users seeded on the mapper.
Subsequently, for scaling up users on UPI ID Mapper, PSPs/TPAPs started onboarding 25 million
users per App from 16th May 2022 (ref circular NPCl/UPl/OC-115C/2021-2022). As on date, 85
million users have been seeded on mapper and daily one lakh transactions are being routed via
UPl ID Mapper. In this scale up phase, eco system performance has been monitored regularly
and in consuitation with the stakeholders, following timelines have been drawn.
