Subject: Process of handling NPCl compliance penalty waiver request for UPl.
We refer to the Operating Circular (OC) No. 99 dated 02nd February, 2021 on enabling non-
compliance penalty in UPl wherein, we have communicated UPl members about the
implementation of non-compliance penalty, if the transaction status is not updated in URCS within
the TAT.
Some of banks are approaching NPCl to waive the compliance penalty on account of non-
updating the transaction status (TCC, RET, RRC and DRC) in URCS due to technical issues
faced by them for various reasons as mentioned below. This matter was discussed in UPl and
IMPS Operations Working Group Meeting held on 23rd August, 2022.
1. Technical issues - Switch / CBS.
2. Migration / Upgradation activities - Switch / CBS.
The following activities are identified to provide the waiver towards NPCl compliance penalty.
1. Closing activities carried out by member banks during the month end, quarter end or year
end.
2. Any activities such as migration / upgradation to a new platform or data center.
3. Maintenance activities.
4. DR Drills.
Please note importantly that member banks are requested to submit the waiver request letter duly
signed by senior management official within 30 days from the date of occurrence of the incident
which the waiver is sought and same would be examine by NPCl on applicability of waiver
conditions.
The date of providing waiver is effective from 1st August, 2022. For the incidents occurred in the
Member banks may please make the note of the above and disseminate the instructions
contained herein to the officials concerned.
