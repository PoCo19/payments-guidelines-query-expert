Subiect: Adherence to RBI PPl Interoperability guidelines by 31st March, 2022
As per the RBI Master Directions on Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPIs) - RBI/DPSS/2021-22/82
(Updated as on 12th November, 2021) regarding issuance and Operation of Prepaid Payment
Instruments, it shall be mandatory to give holders of fuil KYC compliant PPls interoperability through
UPI for the PPls (in the form of wallets) by 31st March 2022. This is pursuant to PPI guidelines issued
on October 11, 2017 on issuance and Operation of Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPi-MD).
InteroperabilityguidelinesforPPl Issuers throughUPl
PPl issuer shall facilitate all basic / standard features of interoperability of UP!
PPI issuer shall act as Payment System Providers (PSP) in UPl. NPCI shall issue handle to
the PPl issuer as per policy / guidelines taking risk management aspects into consideration.
PPI holders shall be on-boarded for UPI by their own PPI issuer only. PPl issuer shall only link
its customer wallets to the handle issued to it.
Authentication shall be completed by the PPI holder as per existing wallet credentials.
For the purposes of settlement, a non-bank PP! issuer shall participate through a sponsor
bank. Non-bank PPl issuer shall adhere to the requirements of sponsor bank arrangement in
UPl and also meet all relevant requirements of NPCl.
