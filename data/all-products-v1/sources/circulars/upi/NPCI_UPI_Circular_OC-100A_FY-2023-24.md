Sub: Addendum to “Mandatory Brand Guidelines for BHiM UPl"
As the UPl ecosystem is growing towards 300 million transactions per day, it is essentiai to
educate users in order to maintain their faith in UPl. As new users are added to UPj on a daily
basis, an awareness video will assist users, particularly those with limited knowledge of
snartphone usage, to start using the app safely.
All UPl Apps (Bank Apps & TPAPs) should ensure to have the foilowing:
1. When user installs and opens a UPl App, video on "How to use UPl safely" should be
availabie on the first screen of the App in a prominent place. In case playing the video in
the App is not feasible, link to fhe video may be provided.
2. There should be a provision io change the language of the Video.
3. These videos should be displayed to both existing and new users.
Member Banks, PSPs, TPAPs are hereby requested to take note of the above and ensure
compliance before 31st March 2023.
