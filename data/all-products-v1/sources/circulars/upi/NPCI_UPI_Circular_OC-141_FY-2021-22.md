Subject: Safeguarding Users on UPl
The UPl payment platform provides a safe and secure experience to User thereby ensuring trust in the
ecosystem
In view of'the above, UPl Members are required to ensure the following:
P2P Lending platforms (as defined under extant reguiatory guidelines) shall be tagged by the
respective Acquirers under MCC 7408 only.
2. Online gaming (apart from categories restricted under Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming
Bill, 2025) (Mcc 5816) shall be allowed by Acquirers via Push payment (transaction initiation mode
00 with transaction sub type 'Pay') and In-App intent only (transaction initiation mode 04 & 05) for
Users with Android-based device only
3. Gift/Prepaid cards acting as prepaid instrument shall be classified by Acquirers as MCC 6540
henceforth.
4. Rent payments (Mcc 6513) & all Gift/Prepaid Cards shall be allowed by Acquirers only via Push
payment (transaction initiation mode 00 with transaction sub type 'Pay') and In-App intent (transaction
initiation mode 04 & 05) and other permitted initiation modes as mentioned in NPCI/UPi/OC-
141D/2024-25(Addendum) dated 2nd April 2025.
5. NPCl has mandated the UPl Members to ensure the visibility of the Ultimate Beneficiary vide
NPCI/UPl/OC-101/2020-2021 and NPCI/UP/OC no 101A/2025-26 dated 25th February 2021 and 24th
April 2025 respectively. The Acquirers shall ensure that each Ultimate Beneficiary shall have a unique
UPt ID for doing business on UPI Platform. Please bear special note of it for following MCCs:
MCC 7408: Entity facilitating P2P Lending
MCC 5816: Digital Goods: Games
MCC 6540: Walet top up
MCC 6513: Real Estate Agents and Managers - Rentals

6. This circular is not applicable for Mandate Transactions (One time and Recurring Mandates)
7. It is reiterated that Acquirers are responsible for correct assignment of MCCs and also ensure
compliance to all guidelines for its aggregators/sub-merchants (Please refer NPCl/UP1/RuPay/OC-
118/2021-22 dated 8th September 2021). Frauds due to non-adherence of NPCi guidelines shall
continue to be the liability of respective Acquirer.
8. UPI Members shall ensure adherence to the P2P inward credit limit as defined by NPCl (UPI OC
NPCI/UPl/OC 181B/2024-25 dated 4th September 2024).
UPl Members are advised to ensure that the contents of this Circular are promptly cascaded to their
respective sub-members and other concerned entities for necessary compliance.
