Introduction of UPI Plug-in Service
To improve payment acceptance and UPl integration for merchants, UPl Plug-in service is
being introduced wherein, the sponsor bank extends their PSP bank SDK (Parent App) to
integrate with Merchant's App (Partner App). Since there is an existing PSP app (Parent App)
means of entering the UPI PIN in the Partner App (SDK of Parent PSP), and promote Parent
apps for full UPl functionality
The below direction shall be followed for UPl Plug-in integration:
1. The Partner App shall have to provide UPl merchant functionalities basis the partner's
business use case (only for Merchant Payments) as approved by NPCl.
2. The Partner App shal operate as a non-exclusive PsP bank handle (where the handle
is common across multiple apps), where the Partner App will have the same profile
(UPl ID and remitter account of the user) already present in a PsP app which manages
UPI IDs (to be called as Parent App). This wifl provide Partner App to leverage existing
user base and aiso support new user registration on Parent App using the UPl Plug-In
solution.
3. UPl Plug-in service shall be managed in bank controlled infrastructure oniy (on premise
or cloud) and data communication for payment transactions is permitted only between
PSP SDK and PSP server directly.
4. The Parent PSP app must be certified as a UP! app with NPCl, must support
interoperabity, and be enabled for all UPl mandatory features inciuding services
provided by their Partner Apps.
5. The Partner (merchant) shall not have access to customer sensitive data including
personal information and Sensitive Personal Data. It may have information except as
may be required by the merchant to support customers to raise dispute using UPl Help
(ODR) with respect to UPI transaction made using the Partner App. Further, the
Partner shall not share any UPl transaction data with any other entity without the prior
consent of Sponsor PSP Bankand NPCl.
