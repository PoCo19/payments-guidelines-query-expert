UPl OD accounts for merchant acceptance - Terms and Awareness
UPl has emerged as a popular payment mode in India, and it possesses flexibilities to deepen and emerge as
preferred payment mode among Businesses/Merchants. To facilitate the same, UPl 2.0 was launched, with one
of the features that allowed linking of OverDraft as the underlying account in UPI (called OD).
charges, it is advised that the merchant acquirer banks, their partners & aggregators shall ensure following
1. Inform & educate the merchants regarding the UPl linked to OD transactions, and the terms associated
with Overdraft account on UPl for existing as well as new merchants.
2. Clearly outline to merchants, the charges for OD accounts in UPl.
3. If Merchant is not enabled for OD transactions for any reasons, the end customer to be notified
accordingly & the responsibility lies with all the UPI banks and TPAPs apps.
NPCl had issued circular NPCl/UPI/Oc No. 62/2018-19 dated December 4, 2018 and NPCl/UPI/OC No.
79/2020-21 dated 21st February 2020, for the communication of application OD interchange of 1.5% of the
transaction value for the Unsecured OD account (UOD) in UPl. The banks, TPAP's should pass the OD account
flag in end to end transactions, and include in the notifications appropriately.
This circular references circular RBl/2016-17/59 DPsS.CO.PD No.639/02.14.003/2016-17 issued by Reserve
Bank of india on September 1, 2016.
Member banks should provision Overdraft Accounts in UPl.
