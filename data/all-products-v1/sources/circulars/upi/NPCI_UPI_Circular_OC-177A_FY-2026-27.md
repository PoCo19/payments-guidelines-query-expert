Subiect: Addendum to UPl Global Acceptance Limits and Country Code Enablement
With reference to NPCI/UPI/OC-177/2023-24, dated 31 st October 2023, UPI Global transaction limits along
with the list of Country/Currency code to be enabled for UPI Global Acceptance were introduced. To expand
the reach of UPl Global Acceptance, the Limits and List of Countries for Global Acceptance are being
revised as below. All members are requested to note the following revisions and implement them as needed.
1. UPI Global Acceptance Limits
The per transaction and per day limit for UPI Global Acceptance in Europe (P2M) are being increased to 2
lakh. Each member bank may set their own limits, subject to the overall applicable limits, specified herein.
For regions other than Europe, UPl Global Acceptance transaction limits will continue to be governed by
NPCI/UPI/OC-177/2023-24.
2.Addition of New Countries for UPIGlobal Acceptance
The list of countries enabled for UPl Global Acceptance is also being updated. Members may enable the
codes of the newly added countries and currencies as needed. Please refer Annexure I for consolidated list
of country and currency codes and find below the new additions,
Sr. No. Country Country Code TransactionCurrency Code
Bahrain BH BHD
Cambodia KH KHR
Canada CA CAD
Kuwait KW KWD
New Zealand NZ NZD
Oman OMR
South Africa ZA ZAR
Switzerland CH CHF
Issuer Banks, PsP Banks and UPl Applications are requested to take note of the above revisions, which
shall come into effect from 31st May 2026.
Further, it was also incumbent upon the banks to abide by the extant and future RBl, FEMA and other
relevant guidelines.
