This is in reference to NPCl circular NPCl /UPI/OC No.138/2021-22, dated 16th March 2022 with
subject 'lntroduction of "On-Device wallet' - UPI LITE for Small Value Transactions' and
transaction limit to 500', whereby guidelines to operationalize UPIl LITE small value transactions
have been introduced.
The objective of introducing UPl LITE was to reduce the need for infrastructure scale up at bank end
to cater the growing volumes of UPl small value transactions. UPl LITE is fast and seamless, as UPl
PIN is not required to complete the transaction. In addition, UPI LITE reduces the demand of
availability of the issuer infrastructure for completing the transaction and hence provides better
success rates. To grow UPI LITE and further enhance the customer experience, UPI Apps shall carry
out the below actions:
1) For customers who have not enabled UPI LITE -- UPl apps must create customer awareness
by highlighting the faster checkout experience. UPl Apps shall send a pop-up/ notification with
call to action to enable UPl LITE, when customer performs UP! transaction with PIN below
#500. The notification shall be sent after the UPl transaction and there shall not be any change
in the customer experience for the UPl transaction.
2) For new UPl customer onboarding, UPl apps shall define journey for enabling UPi LITE also
during UPi onboarding with consent from the user.
3) Post UPI LITE enablement, UPI LITE shall be the default instrument for transactions value
below 500.
4) Top-up notifications/ pop-up with call to action shall be given to the customer, if UPl LiTE
balance goes below R200. Pop-up/ notifications shall be given in below ways:
a) Pop-up/ notification when user opens the App and the UPI LITE balance is below 200.
b) After UPl transaction, if UPl LITE wallet balance is below 200 then pop-up / notification
shall be given to the customer.
