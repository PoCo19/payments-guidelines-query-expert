Date: 12th September 2025

NPCI
NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATION OF INDIA
NPC1/2025-26/UP//222A Oct 29, 2025
To,
All members of Unified Payments Interface (UPl)
Subject: Addendum to Segregation of UPl Settlement Cycles for Auth and Dispute
transactions.
We refer to the UPI URCS Operational Circular (OC) issued vide reference NPCl/2025-
26/UPl/222 dated 12th September 2025, wherein it was communicated that the
segregation of AUTH settlements and Dispute transactions shall be executed through the
1 ↑ th (DC1) and 12th (DC2) settlement cycles, effective 3rd November 2025.
We hereby inform all members that the implementation timelines have been rescheduled
from 3rd November 2025 to 15th December 2025, considering ongoing rollouts, feedback from
members and to ensure operational readiness across stakeholders.
Effective from 15th December 2025, adjustment reports will be shared only three times a
day (refer Annexure - 1 for details).
Please note that starting 15th December 2025, adjustment reports for settlement cycles 1
to 10 will be discontinued, as adjustments will now be processed only in the 11th (Dc1)
and 12th (DC2) settlement cycles.
Member banks are advised to take a note of the above and disseminate the information
contained herein to the officials concerned.
