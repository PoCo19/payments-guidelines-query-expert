International merchant payments acceptance through UPl - UPl Global
To enable Unified Payments Interface (UPl) globally, NPCl has introduced International
merchant payments acceptance through UPI wide NPCI/UPI/OC No. 93/2020-21 dated 04th
Sept'20 with subject "Introduction of International Merchant payments through UPl (UPl
Global)".
This enablement has provided extended functionalities to users, and has enabled the UPi
payments solutions for international merchants. This functionality is already live in Singapore
and Bhutan. Subsequently, NPCl will also be enabling other countries in the coming months.
It is necessary for all stakeholders i.e. Banks and TPAPs, to provide this feature to all UPl
providers shall enable the International merchant payments on UPl as mandatory feature by
31st December 2021.
