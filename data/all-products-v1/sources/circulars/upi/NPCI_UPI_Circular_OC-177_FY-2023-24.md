1. Revision for UPl Global Acceptance Limits
The per-transaction limit for UPl Global to be the same as that of UPl, and UPl Global
transactions to be included in the per-day limit of UPI. For existing limits refer to NPCI/UPI/OC
No.120/2021 dated 24th September 2021 (this is subjected to change from time to time).
Member Banks (Payer PsP and Remitter) and UPI Application are requested to take note of
the above and undertake required changes. Members are requested to make the necessary
changes by 30th November 2023.
2. Launch of International Mobile Application Payment Acceptance through UPl QR
code in India
In order to promote UPl payment platform globally, International Mobile Application Payment
Acceptance through UPI QR code in India has been introduced. This will enable Indian
Merchants to accept payments from international customers through their International Mobile
Application by scanning UPl QR codes in India.
In online transaction request, purpose code of these transactions will be “6o".
Member Banks (Payee PsP) and Merchants are requested to take note of the above
enhancement and undertake required changes. Members are requested to make the
necessary changes by 31st December 2023.
