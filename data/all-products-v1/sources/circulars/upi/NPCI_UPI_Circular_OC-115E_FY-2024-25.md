Subject: Addendum to circular NPCl/UPl/OC-115/2021-22 on the Numeric UPI ID resolution
This has reference to UP1 OC NPCl/UPl/OC-115/2021-2022 dated 20th July 2021 on ‘Rollout of "Numeric
UPI ID Mapper" to enable "UPI Number", UPI Steering Committee Meeting held on 16th July 2024 and multiple
Working Group discussions for UPI Number aimed at enhancing interoperability and user convenience in UPl
number-based UPl payments.
Following are the additional guidelines for UPl Number:
1. The Banks, PsP App shall use the Mobile Number Revocation List/Digital Intelligence Platform
(MNRL/DIP) and update their database accordingly at regular intervals, at least on weekly basis. The
activity of recycled or the churned mobile numbers reflecting correctly in the bank and PSP/TPAP
databases will reduce the chances of errors due to churned mobile numbers.
2. UPl App shal obtain explicit user consent with clear opt-out option (by defauit checked out, and user
needs to click on opt-in) for seeding or porting UPl Number.
a) UPl App shall ensure clear, non-intrusive communication without misleading/forceful
messaging. Consent shall not be taken before or during a transaction in any condition.
b) Seeding or porting communications of UPl number shall exclude miscommunication
(For example - the user will stop receiving the money using UPl without taking an action
on seeding the number in mapper)
In case of NPCI mapper response time is not as per requirements, then the PsP app can resolve the
number locally, subject to the PsP App report such occurrences on monthly basis to NPCl as listed in
Annexure A
All members are advised to comply before 31 st March 2025.
