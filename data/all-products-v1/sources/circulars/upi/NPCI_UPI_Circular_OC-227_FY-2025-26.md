Subject: Introduction of "UPI HELp" - a pilot with Al powered support for UPI payments
UPl now"processes approximately 2o billion transactions each month and has emerged as a preferred
mode of payment for users. As the volume of transactions grows, it is imperative to provide simple
and intuitive support channel for the users. NPCl introduces UPI HELP Assistant, powered by a
proprietary financial domain-specific language model, to enhance user engagement by providing
intelligent conversational support. As this Assistant is powered by the Al model, like any other, this
model too, gets better with more users using it and learnings. This is launched as a pilot, and based
on the user and ecosystem feedback, the next steps shall be communicated on this Assistant.
To begin with, Assistant shall -
a. Respond gueries on digital payments: Assistant shall answer user queries related to digital
payments, enabling better understanding of various payment features or guidelines.
b. UPl Transaction Grievance Redressal: UPl users shall be able to check their transaction status
Assistant shall provide Issuer banks with relevant additional information to facilitate informed
decision-making. This method shall be useful for incomplete transactions (status unknown),
and to raise a complaint for merchant (P2M) transactions for service-related issues which are
presently supported as part of UDiR process.
C. UPl Transaction Mandate Management: UPl users shall be able to see all their active
cycle management via simple intuitive keywords such as pause, resume, revoke, etc. through
deep links to the respective UPl Apps. This method will empower user to manage their
AutoPay mandates in an easy way
Assistant shall be accessible to UPl users of participating Members via (i) the Member Banks
customer interface channels such as website, chatbot, etc., (ii) DigiSaathi website/chatbot, etc., and
in due course with (ili) UPI app - by redirecting users to the UPI HELP assistant and providing API
integration to participating members. The UPl Help is offered by NPCl on behalf of customer's bank
and all the decision making shall be done either by the customer and/or the Issuer bank. Initially, the

Members (Banks and PsP/App) that are participating in the pilot are required to ensure the following
guidelines are followed:
Grievance Redressal:
1. Participating Banks and PsPs / apps to provide prominently visible and easily accessible links
to the UPl Help Assistant on their official channels (website, apps, etc.)
2. Participating Banks shall ensure all complaints received via this channel are acknowledged,
registered, and resolved within the prescribed Turn Around Time (TAT) and other rules defined
for grievance redressal and related actions as per the extant guidelines.
3. All member banks live on UDIR must consume complaints raised via UPl Help and act
promptly on auto-resolution mechanisms for transactions marked as pending or deemed via
UDIR process, providing timely updates within stipulated TAT.
4. Participating banks shall maintain consistent communication with customers regarding their
complaints, referencing the Complaint Reference Number (CRN) and guiding them on the
dispute status and further steps.
