Subject: Modification of BHIM incentive scheme for individuals and withdrawal of BHIM 
incentive scheme for merchants 
This is with reference to the notifications shared on 28th March 2018 by Government of India (No-
12/13/2017-DPD-MeitY) regarding the extension and modification of BHIM Cashback Scheme for 
individuals and NPCI circular OC 47 dated 24th April 2018. The revised BHIM Scheme has been 
implemented by Government of India from 1st April 2018 onwards, for BHIM & BHIM UPI Apps users 
(customers & merchants) and for a period of 12 months i.e. from 1st April 2018 till 31st March 2019. 
Post completion of three months of scheme, the government has done its performance review. 
Based on the review, the government has now modified the BHIM Scheme further through 
notification No. 12/13/2017-DPD-MeitY dated 4th July 2018 and brought following changes in the 
operation of the scheme:  
 
The revised scheme provides incentive only to the new BHIM app users and no other 
incentive shall be applicable. 
 
Under the “BHIM cashback scheme of Individuals” the incentive shall be paid ONLY to the 
new users of BHIM app as per the below mentioned details.

 
Modified BHIM incentive scheme will not be applicable for existing customers/users of BHIM 
/ BHIM-UPI App/s of the bank/s. 
 
“BHIM incentive scheme of Merchants” will be discontinued from 1st July 2018. 
The distribution of scheme amount shall be done in line with the existing process of BHIM scheme 
and shall be done directly into the customer’s accounts.  
For further details, please refer to the attached notification from MeitY for modified BHIM 
incentive scheme 2018.
