Subject: Introduction of "UPI Circle" - Delegated Payments for secondary users
UPI has emerged as a preferred mode of payment for users. UPI has created a seamless
digital experience to enable users to transact via their mobile devices. There are users who
are still dependent on cash for their daily payment needs because they do not manage their
own money through their own account. With the objective of addressing these unmet needs,
introduce "UPI Circle" is introduced, wherein UPl users can delegate to their trusted secondary
users for making payments. UPl Steering Committee meeting held on 3rd August 2o23 had
endorsed the introduction of “UPI Circle - Delegate Payments".
UPl Circle is a feature where a UPl user acts as a primary to link with their trusted secondary
users on their UPl App for either partial or full delegation. Primary user can authorize a trusted
secondary user for performing transactions within the security protocols/ limits.
Full Delegation - Primary user authorizes a secondary user to initiate and compiete UPl
transactions as per defined spend limits
Partial Delegation - Primary user authorizes initiation of payment requests from secondary
users, Primary user shall complete UPI transaction with UPI PIN
Members are required to ensure the below guidelines are followed:
1. UPl apps and PsP to offer independent user journey for Primary and secondary users.
User shall have their own choice of UPl app
2. UPl Apps shall ensure App passcode/ biometrics (finger/face), etc. mandatory for all
secondary users
3. For linking, Primary user shall scan QR/enter UPl Id followed by selection of contact
number from the contact Jist. In the later phase, Primary user shall be able to link
Secondary user in lieu of QR code scan by selection of contact nurmber from the
