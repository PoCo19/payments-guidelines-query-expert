Subiect:SafeguardingUserInformationinUPl
Unified Payments Interface (UPl) facilitates instant digital payments by enabling transactions
through a unique UPI ID assigned to each user. UPI Member Banks and UPI Apps providing
customer interface for UPl are responsible under the extant applicable laws to ensure user
privacy.
Accordingly, amongst other measures adopted by UPI Member Banks and UPI Apps to ensure
user privacy, user's information namely UPl IDs, mobile numbers, and account numbers, must
be masked across all customer-facing interfaces and communications of the UPl Member
Banks and UPI Apps (masking format appended). Additionally, UPI Apps must allow users to
have non-mobile number-based UPl IDs (as per the defined guidelines) and set it as their
default UPIID.
All UPI Member Banks and UPI Apps must implement the necessary changes on customer
facing interfaces and communications by 4th September 2026.
