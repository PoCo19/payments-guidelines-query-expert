To
All members participating in Unified Payment Interface(UPl),
Subject: Addendum to RBI Digital Rupee - CBDC and UPI Interoperability.
With reference to NPCI guidelines NPC/UPV/OC No170 and NPCI/UPI/OC 170-A dated 2oth
September, 2023 and 7th November, 2023 respectively on RBI's Digital Rupee - CBDC and
UPl interoperability were issued to the eco-system.
In order to further streamtine the process of Digital Rupee wallet top up, the following
guidelines are being issued:
1. Payee PsPs (Acquirers) who are facilitating Digital Rupee wallet top up through UP)
2. These transactions shall be only allowed via push payment methods (No Coillect
allowed) and has to be ensured by all the members.
3. As mentioned in UPI OC 170-A, it is re-iterated that "loading of CBDC wallets via
Rupay Credit Cards on UPl as the payment mode shall not be allowed. And this
restriction shouid be implemented at the CBDC wallet issuer end."
All members are requested to take note of the above and undertake the requisite changes in
their respective systems immediately.
