Subject: Addendum to OC 185 -lmplementation of Rs 5 Lakh limit per transaction for
specific categories in UPl
With UP! emerging as a preferred payment method, there is a need to enhance the per
transaction limit in UPl for specific categories. This proposed enhancement aligns with
previous limit enhancements implemented through circulars such as NPCI/UP/OC-127/2021-
2022 dated gth December 2021and NPCI/UPI/0C-185/2023-2024 dated 1gth December 2023.
In view of the above,the per transaction value limit in UPl has now been enhanced to  5
lakhs for entities under categories aligned to tax payments. This enhanced limit shall only be
applicable to Verified Merchants', in the applicable category.
To enable this enhancement:
1. Banks/PsPs/UPi Apps shall ensure that per transaction limit has been enhanced for
the categories of verified merchants. (Refer Annexure)
2. Acquiring entities must ensure that the classification of their merchants within 'MCC-
9311' strictly adheres to the tax payments only. They shall ensure that entities are
added to the 'Verified Merchant' list after proper due diligence.
3. Merchants shall ensure UPl as a payment mode is enabled for the increased limit for
the tax payments category.
With reference to the above and its annexure, Members (PsPs & Banks), UPl apps,
Merchants and other payment providers are requested to take note of the above
enhancement, undertake requisite changes.
Members are requested to ensure compliance with the same by 15th September 2024.
