Madam / Dear Sir,
Subject: UPl Limit Standardization
Standardization is the essence of Unified Payments Interface and has helped UPl to become very
popular payments method in the country. Based on recent customer feedback, it has beer
observed that there is a need to standardize the transaction limits across ecosystem, to irmprove
the overall customer experience of UPl.
To facilitate the rich, standard and seamless digital payments experience for the UPl end user, it
is essential to impiement consistent per transaction limit for UPl, excluding NPCl standard risk
rules, across all UPI members. NPCI has issued transaction limits guidelines in UPl/OC76/2019
dated 31st October 2019, UPI/OC82/2020 dated 3rd March 2020, and UPI/OC86/2020 dated 20th
April 2020. Annexure A has limits that are to be standardized across all UPl ecosystem members.
UPI members are advised to comply with above guidelines by 31st October 2021.
