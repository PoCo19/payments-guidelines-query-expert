Implementationof Rs5Lakh limitpertransactionforspecificcategoriesinUPl
With UPl emerging as a preferred payment method, there is a need to enhance the per
transaction limit in UPI for specific categories, as per the RBl Statement on Developmental
and Regulatory Policies, dated 8th December 2023.
In view of the above, the per transaction value limit in UPl has now been enhanced to  5
lakhs for merchants under categories aligned to hospitals and educational services. This
enhanced limit shall only be applicable to “Verified Merchants.
To enable this enhancement:
1. Banks/PsPs/UPl Apps shall ensure that per transaction limit has been enhanced for
the above-mentioned categories of merchants.
2. Acquiring entities shall ensure that merchants are added to the Verified Merchant' list
after proper due diligence.
3. Merchants shall ensure UPl as a payment mode is enabled for the increased limit for
these categories.
With reference to the above, Members (PsPs & Banks), UPl apps, Merchants and other
changes.
Members are requested to ensure compliance with the same by 1oth January 2o24.
