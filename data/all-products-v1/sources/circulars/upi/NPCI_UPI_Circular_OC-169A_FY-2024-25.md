Subject: Addendum to Enhancement in UPI LITE Limits
With reference to the NPCI/UPI/OC No. 138/2021-22, dated 16t March 2022 and NPCI/UPi/OC No.
169/2023-24, dated 31st August 2023, UPI LITE was introduced and transaction limit was increased from
200 from 500 respectively.
CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S1264/02-14-003/2021-2022 with subject ‘Framework for Facilitating Small
Value Digital Payments in Offline Mode', for UPI LiTE, the enhanced limits shall be 1,ooo per
transaction with 5,000 being the total limit. Replenishment of used limit shall be allowed only in online
mode with AFA. Customer will further have the option to set Auto Top-up for limit replenishment as
per NPCI circular, "Introduction of Auto Top-up on UPI LITE', vide No. NPCI/UPl/OC No. 205/2024-
25, dated 27th August 2024. The details of the LITE limits are as below:
UPI LITE UPI LITE X (Offline)
Description Existing New No Change
Per Transaction Limit 500 1000 R500
Total Limit at any point in time 2000 5000 2000
The new limits shall apply to and govern all existing NPCl circulars pertaining to UPI LITE limit
increase immediately.
2. Issuer banks live on UPI LITE shall identify all the UPI LITE accounts that have had zero transactions
in the past six months. Any balances in these dormant LiTE accounts shall be refunded to the source
bank account. Issuer banks shall implement the changes by 3oth June 2025.
