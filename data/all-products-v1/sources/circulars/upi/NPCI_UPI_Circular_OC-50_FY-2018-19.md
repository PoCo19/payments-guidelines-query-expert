Subject: Introduction of Bill Pay functionality (BBPS) on BHIM APP 
We wish to submit that NPCI has operationalized the Bill Payment functionality in BHIM, through 
BBPS platform w.e.f. 25th May 2018. 
This functionality is allowed to those banks which are live in BBPS as customer OU and live in BHIM. 
All the BHIM customers whose banks fall in this category shall be able to make bill payments using 
BHIM App. 
Banks who are willing to participate for the bill pay functionality in BHIM needs to provide, an 
amendment to the common app (format annexed) and also provide necessary account details for 
crediting BHIM bill pay transactions in UPI.  
Please refer Annexure attached for the process of settlement, disputes, complaints & other 
process.  
All members are requested to take a note of the above and ensure to put in place proper 
process so as to ensure compliance to the guidelines of handling BBPS transactions routed 
via BHIM app.
