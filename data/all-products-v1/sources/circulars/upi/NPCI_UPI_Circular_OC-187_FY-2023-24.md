Subject: UPI Reporting requirements (Acquiring entities & UPI Apps)
The impressive growth of the UP1 platform in becoming the preferred digital payment choice for merchants is
exemplified by its rise from contributing 45% to Person to Merchant (P2M) transactions in May 2021 to 59%
in Nov 2023 (Source: NPCl data). This success can be attributed to UPl's simplicity, real-time transactions,
interoperability, and cost-effectiveness. The Reserve Bank of India (RBl) highlights the deployment of over
3o8 million UPI QR codes for P2M payments, underlining the importance of maintaining stability and
efficiency to uphold strong success rates.
In the Steering Committee Meeting dated 3rd August 2023 and subsequent working group discussion, a
consensus was reached on the necessity for centralized monitoring of UPl success rates. Hence proposed
reporting guidelines for merchant transaction data monthly submissions encompassing failure reasons, active
merchants, payment modes, UDiR etc. are issued. By collecting the data, collaborative efforts can be fostered
for root cause analysis and the development of solutions aimed at enhancing overall success rates.
The following guidelines are issued to UPi Members for the enhancement of customer experience that all
Acquirers, shall submit the data as per the data submission framework outined in Annexure-1 from January
2024 onwards.
UPl Members are advised to ensure that the contents of this Circular are promptly cascaded to their
respective sub-members and other concerned entities for necessary compliance
