All Members participating in Unified Payment Interface (UPl)
Subject: RBP's Digital Rupee - CBDC and UPI Interoperability
RBl's Digital Rupee facilitates fast and efficient transactions, which aims to enhance financial
inclusion. It further provides a secure and regulated digital alternative to physical cash.
In line with RBl's decision to enhance interoperability between UPl and Digita! Rupee, it is
intended to extend the benefits of UPl Quick Response Code (QR Code) to the Digital Rupee
users. This will enable all the merchants that are already using UPl QR Code to be enabled
for receiving payments from Digital Rupee users.
Members shall implement the following for enabling interoperability and acceptance of UPl
QR Codes for Digital Rupee transactions:
1. The Bank Apps enabling Digital Rupee transactions shall be allowed only for QR code
based merchant payments (P2M or P2PM). No transactions should be allowed for P2P
segment.
2. Digital Rupee transactions on UPI QR shall be initiated/identified with purpose code
92 and via Digital Rupee (Wallet id - @dr<bankname>) handle only.
3. In case of refunds pertaining to such interoperable transactions on UPl QR, the same
shall be initiated only via Unified Dispute and Issue Resolution (UDiR) APls.
Reference is drawn to NPCI/ UPI/OC 145A dated 1gth August'22 and NPCI/UPI/OC
165 dated 1gth April'23 in this regard. If the acquiring entity uses any other channel for
processing 'refund', liability of such transactions shall be with the acquiring entity.
4. Existing NPCI switching fees for UPI merchant payments (P2M or P2PM) transactions
shall be applicable for the aforesaid transactions.
5. Existing UPl Limits shall apply for such transactions.
6. Existing UPl settlement process and associated guidelines will be applicable for such
transactions.
Members are requested to take a note of the above and undertake requisite changes in their
respective systems before 3oth November '23.
