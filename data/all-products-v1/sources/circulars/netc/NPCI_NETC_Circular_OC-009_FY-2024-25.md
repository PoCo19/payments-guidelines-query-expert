All Members of NETC 
Subject: Cooling period compliance for vehicle under exception chargebacks – 
Transactions presented beyond 15-minutes from reader read time  
Reference may be taken from NETC Program Guidelines (PG) which states: “If the transaction 
is presented beyond 15 minutes from reader read time, the liability of such transaction lies 
with the acquirer in case there is insufficient balance in the customer account at the time of 
transaction processing at issuer host (applicable for reason Code 3004 - Vehicle was in 
blacklist and Reason Code 3005 - Vehicle was in low balance list) . In such cases the issuer 
can raise the chargeback after the cooling period (currently 15 days) and acquirer will not have 
any re-presentment rights”. Member banks are advised to ensure compliance to the clause 
referred above. 
System controls shall be put in place in such a way that NRCS system will validate 
chargebacks for the above-mentioned reason codes and reject those raised before the 
stipulated cooling period with the following reject reason: 
NRCS Reject Reason code 
Reason Code Description 
5224 
Cooling period not completed please raise chargeback 
after 15 days  
 
The implementation date of change in NRCS shall be communicated separately. Member 
banks are advised to ensure that necessary changes are done well in advance in their systems 
to avoid rejections. 
The information herein may please be disseminated to all the concerned officials for strict 
adherence.
