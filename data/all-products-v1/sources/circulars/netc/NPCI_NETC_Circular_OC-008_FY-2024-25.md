Revision of timelines for Vehicle Registration Number Update for FAsTag's Issued on
Chassis Number
With reference to Circular no: NPCl/2002-23/NETC/008 dated 18th January 2023 (Annexure 1),
it was directed that FASTag Issuing entities must update the Vehicle Registration Number (VRN)
of the FAsTags issued on the Chassis Number/ Vehicle ldentification Number (VIN) within 180
days from the date of issuance of the FASTag.
However, to further strengthen the compliance on VRN update, it has been decided in the NETC
Steering Committee Meeting dated 23rd January 2024 to revise the timeline for updation of the
VRN for the NETC FASTag issued on VIN from 180 days to 90 days from the date of issuance.
Further, the Issuer entities are also mandated to take below actions for better compliance:
1) Issuer shall issue the FAsTag on VIN only from dealer location of the OEM (Original
EquipmentManufacturer)of vehicles
2)  Issuer shall update VRN for the tags issued within 9 days from the date of issuance
3) Issuer shall move such FAsTags to Hotlist (O1) exception code if incase there is a failure
to update the VRN within 90 days of FASTagIssuance
4) Issuer shall close the tags if in case there is no VRN update even after 3o days from Hot
listing the tag, using the exception code (06) with the reason of non-compliance
This policy shall be effective for all FAsTag issued on Chassis NumberNIN from 1st April 2o24.

Definition
Definition of KyC Know Your Customer is the process of verifying the identity of customer. The objective of
KyC guidelines is to prevent banks from being used, by criminal elements for money laundering activities.
It also enables banks to understand its customers and their financial dealings to serve them better and
manage its risksprudently.
Importance of KYC
KYC is the means of identifying and verifying the identity of the customer through independent and reliance
source of documents, data or information
Purpose
KyC is to be provided at the time of opening a new account as well as refresh. It may be necessary to obtain
additional information from existing customers based on the conduct of the account, where there are
changes to the account or at fixed periodic refresh cycles based on the risk categorization of the customer.

Annexure 2: Standard Process of Vehicle Registration Number Update on NETC mapper
FASTag has been mandated for New Vehicle by MoRTH wide notification, MoRTH GSR1361(E) Notification
dated 2nd Nov 2o17, that aims to ensure universal coverage of FAsTag on all vehicles. The key aspects to
ensure the implementation of the notification are
Applicable from 1st Dec 2017.
FAsTag mandated on all Categories M and N manufactured, or sold, post 1st December 2017.
Vehicle Manufacturers to ensure availability of FAsTags across their respective dealers via issuer
bank
Dealer to complete the FASTag registration.
With.reference_to.the-Direction-Guidelines-IHMCL/ETC/Operations/2017/570, the-process-of-FASTag
issuance to the new Vehicle has been defined along with the roles and responsibilities of the Stakeholders.
As the Vehicle registration number is not available at the time of sale, a provision has been made to issue
FAsTag on the basis of Vehicle ldentification Number (VIN). It is the responsibility of the respective OEM
dealer / customer to share Vehicle registration number with the Issuer Bank and Issuer bank update the
VRN on the NETCmapper.
