Subject: Revision of TAT for raising and responding to Good Faith in NRCS 
 
Good Faith is provided as an option in NRCS with an extended TAT of 160 days for the 
member banks to raise and resolve concerns on a transaction beyond TAT expiry.  
Considering the current inflow of Good Faith and to improve the latency for member banks 
while using NRCS, the below changes are being made in Good Faith process. 
1. TAT for raising Good Faith (reason code: 680) would be revised to 90 days from the 
transaction date instead of the current TAT of 160 days.  
2. Subsequently, the TAT for Good Faith accept(reason code: 681), Good Faith 
decline(reason code: 682), Good Faith deemed decline(reason code: 505) would be 
reduced to 15 days from the date of Good Faith raise instead of the current TAT of 30 
days. 
The above-mentioned changes will be implemented in NRCS with effect from 1st December 
2025. The inflow of good faith would be reviewed again after six months for revision of TAT. 
The information herein may please be disseminated to all the concerned officials.
