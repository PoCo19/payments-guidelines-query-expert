Dear Sir/ Madam,
Subject: Enablement of FASTag recharge through UPl
We refer to the NHAl NETC steering committee meeting held'on 17th August 2018 at NHAl
head office in Delhi, wherein it was decided that all Issuer banks should provide the Unified
Payment Interface (UPl) platform for reloading/top-up of FASTag.
In this regards, NPCl has already circulated the 'Approach Document' to all issuer banks for
enabling FASTag top up/recharge facility through UPl.
In order to provide a seamless reloading/top-up facility to the FAsTag customers, all existing
FAsTag issuing banks should enable UPl facility. Member Banks are requested to confirm
their compliance of the same by 31th October 2018 in this regards.
