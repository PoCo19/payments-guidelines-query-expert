Subject: Inclusion of UPI QR code in the new design of NETC FASTag. 
 
NPCI and IHMCL have been working towards creating a seamless customer experience for NETC 
FASTag users. In line with the same objective, the UPI QR Code is incorporated in the new design 
of FASTag, which the users can scan and recharge their linked NETC FASTag using any UPI app. 
Member banks and Empaneled Tag Manufacturers are requested to take note of the ‘Tag Marks 
and Specs Document Version 1.2’ for all the necessary details and make the relevant changes 
accordingly. 
For implementation of the same, the respective stakeholders must comply with the below-
mentioned roles and responsibilities. 
• 
Issuer banks shall provide Tag ID based personalized QR Code generation string/URL to 
the Empanelled Tag Manufacturers along with the EPC data file for NETC FASTag 
manufacturing purpose. 
• 
Issuer banks shall incorporate a UPI QR Code Generator functionality on their respective 
websites/apps, from where users can generate and download/print their NETC FASTag 
linked UPI QR Code as image for NETC FASTag recharge purposes. Issuer Banks shall 
enable the NETC FASTag linked UPI QR Code Generator functionality latest by 15th 
February 2024. 
• 
Issuer Banks shall stop the delivery/distribution of old design NETC FASTag after depletion 
of their existing stock as per the data provided by them. Issuer Banks must also ensure 
that no new order must be placed with old design NETC FASTag. 
• 
Issuer Banks shall run awareness/marketing campaign in order to push the market 
penetration of the New Design NETC FASTag. 
• 
Issuer Banks shall make available the New Design NETC FASTag with UPI QR Code to 
the existing customer if customer asks for replacement of their old NETC FASTag.

Issuer Banks to ensure that their empanelled tag manufacturers shall use the Tag ID based

Issuer Banks to ensure that their empanelled tag manufacturers shall terminate the 
manufacturing of NETC FASTag with old design and manufacture only the New Design 
NETC FASTags in adherence with the ‘Tag Marks and Specs Document Version 1.2’, 
from 31st January 2024.  
With the release of this circular, Issuer Banks and Tag Manufacturers shall use the New Design of 
NETC FASTag for all manufacturing purposes, thereby terminating the use of old design for any 
manufacturing needs from 31st January 2024. Further, any new tag manufactured after this 
timeline shall adhere to the New Design norms (Refer Tag Marks and Specs Document Version 
1.2). 
Members are requested to take note of the same and disseminate the information to the relevant 
stakeholders.
