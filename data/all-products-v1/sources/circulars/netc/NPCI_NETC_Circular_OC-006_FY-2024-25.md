Dear Sir/Madam, 
Subject: One Vehicle One FASTag and closure of Multiple tags linked to vehicles 
In line with the National Highway Authority of India (NHAI) initiative on ‘One Vehicle One FASTag’, 
letter received from Indian Highway Management Company Limited (IHMCL) for implementation 
of the same and NPCI circular no. NPCI/2023-24/NETC/001 dated 17th May 2023 regarding 
‘Implementation of NETC FASTag Closure and Replacement Policy’, we hereby convey the 
following actions to all the Issuers: 
1. 
Issuers shall ensure closure of all Multiple tags on same vehicle by 15th April 2024 and ensure 
that only the latest issued tag as per the NETC mapper shall be active, except for previously 
issued tags in Hotlist or Blacklist exception code. 
2. 
Issuers shall complete the necessary changes and certification required for Forceful tag 
closure as per the ‘NETC FASTag Closure and Replacement Policy’ by 30th April 2024 and 
enable the same to ensure automatic closure of older multiple tags 15 calendar days after 
registration of a new tag on same vehicle in NETC Mapper. 
3. 
Issuers shall complete the necessary changes and certification required for VAHAN 
integration by 30th April 2024 and enable the same for FASTag issuance effective 1st May 
2024. 
4. 
Issuers shall ensure issuance of new FASTag’s basis VAHAN Integration and RC copy. 
Further, the issuer shall register the tag in NETC mapper only after adequate due diligence of 
the documents within 7 calendar days of tag issuance. In case of incomplete proofs, all such 
tags shall be closed by the issuer within 7 calendar days. 
5. 
Issuers shall ensure that a new tag is issued to a vehicle only if one or no active tags are 
present in the NETC mapper effective 30th April 2024. Any new tag registration request for a 
vehicle with more than one active tag in NETC mapper shall be declined by NPCI.
