For Initial years NPCI has not levied any certification fee to member Banks. The initial heavy lifting of
certification for all NPCl products was incurred by NPCl, with effective from 2015-16.NPCI introduced
Annual Certification ee for all NPCl products with the aim to support wide range of innovative products.
The Initial Certification fee was decided Rs. 75,ooo per annum for Member Banks (as per Circular no.
NPCI/2015-16/BS/Circular058attached)
NPCI has decided to increase the Annual certification fee from INR 75,000 to INR 3,00,000 excluding
Service Taxfor Commercial Banks and INR 1,5o,ooo for RRBs and Co-operative Banks.The increase in
certification fee is on account of Manpower, Outsourcing and technology cost.
The revised fee structure for annual certification fee is as follows with effect from 1st April, 2017
