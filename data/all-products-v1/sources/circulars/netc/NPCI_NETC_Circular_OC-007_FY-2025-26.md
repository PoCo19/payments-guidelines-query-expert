Subject: Revision to implementation date of new chargebacks reason codes and 
clarification on good faith TAT reduction 
 
1. Reference may be taken from the circular NPCI/2025-26/NETC/005 dated October 28, 
2025, regarding new reason codes introduced in NRCS and guidelines to be followed by 
the member banks while raising and responding to chargebacks with an implementation 
date of 1st December 2025. 
Basis the request from the ecosystem seeking additional time for complying to the 
guidelines, the implementation date of circular 005 has been revised to 5th January 2026.  
However, the below reason codes would be removed from NRCS with effect from 1st 
December 2025.  
1. 3014 – Other Specify (Chargeback Reason Codes – Debit) 
2. 3022 – Other Specify (Chargeback Reason Codes – Credit) 
3. 4010 – Other Specify (Re-Presentment Reason Codes) 
4. 1007 – Other Specify (Debit Adjustment Reason Codes) 
5. 2007 – Other Specify (Credit Adjustment Reason Codes) 
 
2. Reference may be taken from the circular NPCI/2025-26/NETC/006 dated November 07, 
2025, regarding reduction of the good faith TAT to 90 days. It is hereby clarified that the 
mentioned 90 days is from the expiry date of the dispute TAT. If any good faith dispute is 
raised after the defined TAT, such good faith shall be rejected by the system with the 
below reason code. 
NRCS Reject Reason code 
Reason Code Description 
3207 
The TAT for the dispute cycle/adjustment you 
trying to raise has expired  
 
The information herein may please be disseminated to all the concerned officials for strict 
adherence.
