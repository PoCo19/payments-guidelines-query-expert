Subject: Addendum to NETC Circulars – 001, 002 and 003 on NRCS enhancements - Revision 
to effective date 
Reference may be taken from the following NETC circulars issued to NETC members with an 
effective go-live date of 15th May 2026. Based on requests received from member banks, the go-
live date for the circulars mentioned below has been revised to 25th June 2026. All member banks 
are advised to take note of the revised implementation date and complete the necessary system, 
operational, and reconciliation related changes to ensure readiness from the effective date.  
Please refer to the table below for the circular details: 
Operating Circular Number 
Operating Circular Subject 
NPCI/2026-27/NETC/001  
System changes in NRCS to mandate submission of 
supporting evidence for Dispute Resolution. 
NPCI/2026-27/NETC/002 
Revision of arbitration deemed continuation stage, automation 
of daily NRP/PRD fee settlement and PRD workflow 
enablement in NRCS 
NPCI/2026-27/NETC/003 
Implementation of system controls in NRCS for chargebacks 
raised under reason codes 3004 & 3005 
 
Kindly disseminate the information contained herein to the officials concerned.
