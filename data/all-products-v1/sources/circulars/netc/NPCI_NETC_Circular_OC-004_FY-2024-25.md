Dear Sir/Madam, 
Subject: Issuance of New FASTags to the Paytm Payments Bank Limited (PPBL) Consumers 
Based on the letter received from Indian Highways Management Company Limited (IHMCL), it is 
hereby conveyed to all the Issuers to take the following actions: 
1. Issuers shall make necessary changes to facilitate FASTag issuance even to the vehicles 
of PPBL consumers which are in Hotlist 
2. Issuers shall ensure adequate inventory of FASTags at all the Toll Plazas and other Point 
of Sale locations 
3. Issuers shall ensure all the channels, ground force and call centres are sufficiently staffed 
and ready to serve PPBL consumers looking for replacement of FASTag 
The above circular will be applicable with immediate effect.
