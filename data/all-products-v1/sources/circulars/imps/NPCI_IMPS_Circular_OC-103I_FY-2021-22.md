Subject: Enhanced Per Transaction Limit for IMPS Transactions from Rs. 2 Lakh to Rs. 5 Lakh 
With regulator’s approval the per transaction limit for IMPS transactions is hereby increased from Rs. 2 
lakh to Rs. 5 lakh with immediate effect, for all IMPS payment channels excluding the Short Message 
Service (SMS) and Interactive Voice Response (IVR) transactions. The SMS and IVR channel limits 
shall follow the regulator approved unencrypted channel limits for financial transactions as applicable.  
All members are advised to implement necessary changes in their systems at the earliest. For all 
purposes, the Rs. 2 lakh transaction slab in IMPS hereafter shall be extended to Rs. 5 lakh, including 
the applicable fees and charges.
