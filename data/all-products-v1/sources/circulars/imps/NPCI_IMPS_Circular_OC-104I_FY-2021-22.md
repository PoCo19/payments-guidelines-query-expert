Sub: Migration of “IMPS – Bharat Clearing and Settlement System (BCS)” to “IMPS Real Time 
Clearing & Settlement System” (IRCS) 
 
Presently, all IMPS member banks are accessing IMPS-BCS back-office system for IMPS operational 
activities (viz. clearing & settlement, disputes, downloading settlement files, adjustment files and raw 
files, GST Reports etc.).  
We are pleased to inform that NPCI will be migrating from IMPS-BCS to new back office system IRCS 
(IMPS Real Time Clearing & Settlement System).  The new IRCS application is already functional in 
a parallel-run environment processing the transactions in parallel to IMPS-BCS and the processing and 
settlement reports are getting matched. The parallel run and verification will continue till the cutover. 
Additionally, a minimum set of IMPS members will be contacted separately and enabled for pilot access 
to the new IRCS application to go through with IRCS application features, raising of disputes / 
adjustments in IRCS and verification of settlement reports / formats accordingly. 
After migrating from IMPS-BCS to IRCS, NPCI will decommission existing back office system i.e. IMPS- 
BCS. Please note that there is no change in Settlement report’s formats in IRCS (as compared to BCS).     
Training to all IMPS members  
NPCI shall arrange training on IRCS to all IMPS members with demo on test system (training details 
shall be shared separately through e-mail) before Go Live. IRCS – User manual will also be shared to 
all participants through e-mail and will also be placed in BCS for banks reference. 
Benefits in IRCS 
The migration from IMPS-BCS to IRCS would result in following benefits: 
1. Efficient Latest Technology Stack. 
2. Improved performance for on-time multiple cycle settlement. 
3. Horizontally scalable for future volumes. 
4. Admin Maker and Admin Checker concept for creating users at bank end. 
5. Bulk transaction search.

System Requirement Specification 
For accessing IRCS we recommend system requirement specifications, please refer Annexure – A for 
details.  
 
PGP Tool 
There is no change in PGP Tool. Current PGP Tool being used for BCS should be used for IRCS also. 
IRCS URL/IP:  
There is no change in IP for accessing IRCS. Members are requested to use existing BCS URL/IP for 
accessing IRCS also (banks can see IRCS with same URL from the date of go-live).  
Effective Date  
NPCI will be migrating the IMPS back office system from IMPS-BCS to IRCS during 1st week of October, 
2021 tentatively (based on closure of GST report generation for previous month). A separate detailed 
notification will be sent again to the members a week prior to the production cutover. 
All members of IMPS are requested to take a note of the above and disseminate the information 
contained herein to the officials concerned.
