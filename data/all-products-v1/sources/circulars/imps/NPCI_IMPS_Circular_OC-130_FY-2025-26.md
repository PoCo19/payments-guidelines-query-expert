Subject: Replacement of Mobile Number with Account Number in adiustment report
'Rem Mobile.No' contain mobile numbers and NBIN details. Based on requests from
participant members, the changes are being made to replace the mobile number and NBIN
with the account number. Refer to the Annexure-1, Table-A for a comparison of the existing
and proposed changes.
The above functionality will be implemented in IRCs with effect from December 05, 2025.
Member banks are advised to take a note of the above and disseminate the information
contained herein to the officials concerned.
