Best practices codes-IMPS
IMpS was launched on November 22, 2010 and currently there are 51 banks live on IMPs. With the concerted
efforts ofall concerned, the technical declines have been brought down significantly.We endeavor to bring down
technical declines to the bare minimum which necessitates collaborative action from all the member banks.
Following few best practices require being adopted by our member banks with immediate effect to meet the
objective of further reducing the technical declines.
Down Time:
A) Schedule downtime would be permissible only between 1:00 am and 6:00 am. Banks are advised to carry out
all planned maintenance activities only during this time.Please avoid any maintenance activity during the high
carried out in 2ndand 3rdweek only between 1:00am and 6:00 am.
B) Banks carrying out scheduled maintenance should inform IMPS and NFS team by way of e-mail at least 24
hours in advance.This will enable IMpS/NFS helpdesk to inform other member banks or handle other bank
queries efficiently.
ISDN/Backup Link Testing:
Banks should undertake ISDN/Backup Link testing as frequently as possible, preferably once a week during night
time. This will help ensuring minimum downtime. In case of any technical issue please coordinate with IMPs/NFS
helpdeskteam.(Detailsprovidedbelow)
Contact Details:
Member banks are responsible to update the contact details of its IT, Network and Operations team (from Level 1
to Level 4) in case of any changes at the earliest to IMP/NFs helpdesk through e-mail to the
impsteam@npci.org.in,nfshelp@npci.org.in.
