Subject: Addendum to "Sun-setting of isO 8583 messaging specification on IMPs" on
revised timelines
NPCI issued NPCI/IMPS/OC No.102/2021-22 dated 28th July 2021, whereby 1IMPS members
were communicated about migrating IMPS from ISO 8583 messaging to API based XML
format before 31st March 2022, which was further extended through circulars NPCl/lMPS/OC
No.109/2021-22 dated 31st March 2022 and NPCI/IMPS/OC No.116/2023-24 dated 2nd June
2023 where NPCI provided timeline of 15th July 2023 for all member entities to migrate to XML
messaging. But due to delay in readiness of the ecosystem, the timeline is now extended to
31st January 2024.
All member entities of IMPS shall take note and complete the migration before 31st January
2024, after which all the nodes of the banks live on IO 8583 messaging will be turned down
and no transactions will be received or sent from NPCI in ISO 8583 messaging.
