Please noted that on Sundays & holidays, settlement shall be done for the transactions from 
23:00:01 hrs. to 23:00:00 hrs. 
If any holiday is declared in any week, say, on Wednesday, then settlement for Thursday shall be for 
transaction that has happened from Tuesday 16:00:01 hrs. to 23:00:00 hrs and from 23:00:01 hrs. 
to Wednesday 23:00:00 hrs. The Settlement files and DSR shall be provided separately for 
reconciliation purpose.
