Dear Madam/Sir, 
Subject: Revised timeline for migration of IMPS system to XML messaging specification 
In terms of our circular NPCI/IMPS/OC No.102/2021-22 dated 28th July, 2021 on “IMPS 2.0: Migration 
from ISO to XML”, all IMPS members were mandated to migrate their systems from ISO 8583 to XML 
messaging specifications by 31st March, 2022, with the purpose of making IMPS platform more 
scalable and future ready. 
It was also notified to members that post the given timeline, the ISO8583 platform will sunset. 
Many members have certified, however, there are some that are not. Given the criticality of the 
ecosystem go live, the timeline for migrating to XML messaging specification has been extended till 
30th June, 2022. 
All Members are requested to take note of the above and disseminate the information contained 
herein to the officials concerned.
