Madam / Dear Sir,
Subject: Revised timelines on changes in IMPS raw data file-OC 107.
We refer to Operating Circular (OC) No. 107 dated 8th Dec, 2021 on changes in IMPS raw data
files wherein, we have requested IMPs members to complied with necessary changes by 3oth
June, 2022.
It is understood from various banks that necessary changes mentioned in Oc 107 are still under
development to handle the reconciliation based on revised raw data files. NPCI has also arranging
necessary support for banks for smooth migration of changes from old raw data format to new
format.
In view thereof, we wish to inform you that the timelines to fulfil the OC 107 compliance is
extended to 3 more months hence all Banks are requested to complied with these changes by
30th September, 2022.
Please make note of the above and disseminate the instructions contained herein to the officials
concerned.
