NPCI has receivedtheapproval from ReserveBankof Indiaforextending the capabilities of IMPS
andtoaddthefollowing additionalservices:
1.Merchant Payments
This would facilitate making Person to Merchant (P to M)payments like Utility bill
payments, payments at shop floor, payment of fees to various institutions, donation
payments etc. irrespective of with which bank the merchant maintains banking relationship.
Merchants can also pull the transactions from the registered subscribers with subscriber
confirming the payment requests.
2. Initiation of IMPs transaction through ATM and Internet channel.
With this approval, customers can initiate IMPS transactions from ATM and Internet Banking
Channel. This additional capability to initiate transactions through alternate channels would
indeed provide the necessary impetus for increasing the transactions through IMPs.The
customers, who are not in the position to download the mobile banking client application on
the mobile handset, can make use of the ATM/internet Banking to effect the payment.The
destination account would be identified by the mobile number and MMiD. Thus, the
features of real time payment and 24x7 availability would be extended to payments
initiated fromATMand Internet Bankingchannel.
