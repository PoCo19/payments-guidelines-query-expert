Sub: IMPS - Mandating Remitter & Beneficiary Name and Account Number in IMPS P2A 
IMPS P2A (Person to Account) fund transfer through Account Number & IFSC product is a major 
contributor towards the growth of IMPS. 
NPCI has received various suggestions from the user community to improve the user experience of 
IMPS. One of the important suggestions from various members of IMPS was to make the Remitter & 
Beneficiary Name and Account Number in IMPS specifications mandatory. 
During the 23rd Steering Committee Meeting {SCM) of IMPS held on 26th February, 2016, NPCI has 
proposed mandating remitter and beneficiary name and account number in the specifications and the 
SCM members had approved this changes in IMPS P2A specification.
