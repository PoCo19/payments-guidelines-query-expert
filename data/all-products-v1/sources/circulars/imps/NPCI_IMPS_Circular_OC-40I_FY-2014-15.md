Sub:MappingFullIFSCodeof Sub-MemberBanks againsttheirNBINin IMPS
Withthe Vision of Financial Deepening,NPCl has taken initiativeto expand its eco-system and extend
IMPSservicesto all Co-operativebanks andRegional Rural Banks(RRBs).ReserveBank of Indiahas
approved enablingRegional Rural Banks(RRBs)andCo-operativeBanks as a sub-memberon IMPSasa
beneficiaryonly,wherefull-fledgedMobileBanking Approvalhas not yetbeen receivedbythe Bank.
For IMPSP2A (Person to Account transferusing IFScode),theRemitter Banks &PPls are required to
populate NBiN of Beneficiary Bank in order to route thetransaction to respective beneficiary bank.As
pertheexisting architecture,banks are populating NBINbased onfirst4digits of IFScode.Most of these
UCBs/DCBs/RRBshavethefirst4digitsofIFSCodeoftheirSponsorBank.Iftheremitterbankspopulate
NBINbasisfirst4digitsof IFSCode,thentheNBINof theSponsorBankwill bepopulatedbasiswhich
NPCl will route transaction to Sponsor Bank.This might lead to credit in to the wrong account (same
accountnumbermayexist intheCBS of SponsorBank)and resultantdeclines.
Werequestall IMPSMemberBanksandPPistoconfigurethefull 11digit IFSCodeoftheSub-member
to their NBIN as and when new sub-member bank goes live on IMPs. This will ensure that the
transaction is routed to the correct beneficiary bank.
