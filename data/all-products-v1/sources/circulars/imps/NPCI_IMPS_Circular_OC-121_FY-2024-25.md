Subject: Modification of Payer McCs
IMPs operates as a real-time fund transfer system, enabling account-to-account transactions
round the clock. Hence, the use of IMPs has increased over the time for multiple use cases.
With such increase in transaction volumes, it is important to monitor the transactions with
better checks to safeguard customers from any financial frauds. For, such robust monitoring
of transactions, it is crucial to have further ciassification in JMPs transactions so that NPCl
and banks apprehend about the source of the transactions.
In the view of above, the payer MCC codes 4513, 4512 and 6010 is modified as following,
