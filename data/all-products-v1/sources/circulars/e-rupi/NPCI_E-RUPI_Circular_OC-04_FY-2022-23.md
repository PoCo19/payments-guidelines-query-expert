Subject: Limit enhancement, Multiple use voucher & Acceptance of e-RUPl vouchers
With the emerging use case for the e-RUPl voucher, RBl issued a Statement on Deveiopmental
and Regulatory Policies on 1oth February 2022, wherein the voucher limit for Government
schemes has been enhanced and all e-RUPi vouchers can be used muitiple times.
e-RuPiVoucherlssuance
1. Issuer Bank shall be able to issue One time use and Multiple time use e-RUPl vouchers.
2. Issuer Bank shall be able to issue e-RuPl vouchers for Government Schemes up to INR.
1,00,000 per voucher whereas for Private entities it shall be up to INR. 10,000 per voucher.
3. Issuer Bank shall adopt a risk-based approach in deciding the number of e-RUPl vouchers
which can be issued toa single beneficiary.
4. The vatidity of the e-RUPl voucher shall be defined by the Issuer bank as per the use case
(Max validitypervoucher -- 1year).
5. For Multiple-time use e-RUPl vouchers, once the voucher has expired, the Issuer bank
shall refund the remaining balance amount back to the Sponsor's source account.
6. For One-time use e-RUPl voucher, Issuer Bank shall refund the remaining balance
amount back to the Sponsor's source account post 1st Redemption/Revoke/Expiry
whicheveris earlier.
7. e-RUPl voucher shall be revoked by the ssuer bank at any point on a real-time basis on
the customerwith the revoke details.
8. Issuer Bank shall issue an e-RuPl voucher for each use case as per MCC & Purpose

9. Issuer Bank shall mandatorily send an SMS to the e-RUPl beneficiary with a download

10, lssuer Bank shall also have resent voucher facility.
11. Issuer Bank shall follow NPCI SMS and voucher template guidelines.
e-RUPlVoucherValidation
12. All Merchant Acquirer apps shall have a SMS scan feature along with a QR scan feature.
13. Acquirer apps shall fetch the purpose code from the QR and in the case of SMS scan
feature fetch the purpose codefromthe UUID.
14. On receiving a validation request, the lssuer bank shall verify the purpose code and MCC
asperMCC&PurposeCodeMapper.
e-RUPlVoucherRedemption
15. Post each redemption, the Issuer bank shall send SMs to the customer with redemption
details and pending voucherbalance.
16. It is the responsibility of tssuer Bank to manage the balance of each voucher as part of
theVoucherManagement System.
