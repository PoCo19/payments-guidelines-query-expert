Subject: Enhancement to e-RUPl voucher
In 2021, e-RUPl voucher was introduced as a part of the Digital India platform, with the
objective of engaging consumers who may not have access to digital payment service. The e-
RUPl voucher serves as a cashless and contactless payment instrument tailored to person
specific and purpose.
NPCl released circular vide NPCl/e-RUPl/OC No.1/2021-2022 dated 27th September 2021 for
enabling the e-RUPl voucher services. Subsequentiy the limit for the e-RUPl voucher for
Government sponsored programme was enhanced and the scope of multiple time use of
voucher was introduced vide circular NPCl/e-RUPl/OC No.4/2022-23 dated 7th July 2022.

To further increase the scope, enhancements for e-RUPl voucher are being introduced.
e-RUPlvoucherissuance
A) Government/Corporates
1. Sponsor shall request to Issuer for creation of One-time use (non-reloadable) or
Muitiple time use (reloadable/ non- reloadable) e-RUPI voucher.
2. Limits for Government as a sponsor is up to R1,00,000 per voucher whereas for
Corporate as a sponsor shall be up to 5o,0o0 per voucher.
3. At any point in time, Government sponsored voucher cannot hold more than 1,o0,000
per voucher and Corporate sponsored voucher cannot hold more than 50,000 per
voucher.
4.  The validity of the e-RUPI voucher shall be maximum - 1(one) year.
5. e-RUPl voucher shall be revoked by the issuer bank on the request of sponsor until
beneficiary has set the UPI PIN for the voucher i.e. Once UPI PIN for that voucher is
set then the voucher cannot be revoked by the Sponsor/ Issuer.
B) Individuals (bank account linked on UPI App)
1. Individuals shall be able to create e-RUPl vouchers using any UPI app.
(Annexure 2).
3. ndividuals shall create e-RUPI voucher using beneficiary mobile number and fill in the
details such as Beneficiary Mobile number, Beneficiary name, Amount, Start date (by
default - current date), End Date (by default -- 1 year from start date), Purpose of the
voucher (Annexure 2), Debit from account, Voucher Issuing Entity, Voucher use type
etc.
4. Individuals shall enter the UPl PIN for the respective account from which the money
shall be debited.
5. The limit for creation of e-RUPI voucher sponsored by Individual is up to 10,000.
6. UPl app shall not allow individuals to create reloadable e-RUPl voucher.
7. UPI app shall provide the option to share the created voucher to the beneficiary using
all the communication medium available in the device for e.g, email, messaging apps,
social media etc.
8. Individuals shall be able to revoke the e-RUPl voucher until beneficiary has set the UPl
PIN for that voucher i.e. Once UPI PIN for that voucher is set then the voucher cannot
be revoked by the Individual as a sponsor.

Il.e-RUPI voucher linking & set UPI PIN
A) Beneficiary (Bank account linked on UPI App)
1. Beneficiary who has received the voucher shall be able to link the voucher on the home
page and also in the account section of any UPl app.
2.  Beneficiary shall enter activation code of 6 digit (sent by issuer) to set UPl PIN for each
voucher received they wish to activate.
B) Beneficiary (Bank account not linked on UPl App)
1. User who doesn't have a valid bank account or have not linked their bank account to
any UPl app shall be introduced to make digital payments through e-RUPI voucher.
2. Beneficiary shall install the application, enter his/her mobile number, post successful
device binding they shall link the e-RUPl voucher and set UPI PIN for each e-RUP!
voucher.
Note:
1. For Individual/ Corporate sponsored e-RUP/ voucher, it is mandatory to link e-RUP!
voucher and set UPl PIN on UP/ app only for performing redemption.
1i. It is non-mandatory to link e-RUP/ voucher on the UPI app for Govemment sponsored,
i.e., Beneficiary shall redeem at the merchant location using QR/ SMS string or by
linking e-RUP/ voucher on UP/ app.
川. e-RUPl voucher redemption
A) Beneficiary (Bank account linked on UPI App)
1. Beneficiary shall be able to see the purpose for each e-RUPI voucher on UPl app.
2. Beneficiary shall redeem the voucher at the merchant location basis the defined
purpose of the e-RUPI voucher.
 During the redemption of the voucher the PsP/ App needs to ensure that the e-RUPl
voucher shall be shown as a default debit option where MCC defined for the purpose
code is matching with Payee MCC.
4. In case of multiple vouchers are avaifable for the same purpose with matching MCC,
then the voucher which has the earliest expire date must be the default debit option.
 5. Beneficiary shall be able to change the debit option as per his/ her choice and select
other payment instruments.
6. In the transaction history page of the app, transaction done via e-RUPl vouchers must
be displayed to the user for at ieast 3 months from the expiry date and must be
differentiable as per Brand Guidelines.

B) Beneficiary (Bank account not linked on UPI App)
1. Beneficiary who has not linked their bank account on UPl app shall only redeem
Governmenf and Individual sponsored e-RUPl vouchers.
2. Beneficiary shall be able to link the e-RUPl voucher and redeem at the merchant
3. If beneficiary needs to redeem more than 3 e-RUPl vouchers of Individual use case

4. There is no limit to the number of e-RUPl vouchers that a beneficiary shall redeem for

IV. Roles and responsibilities/Controls
A) Issuer
1. Issuer shail certify on NPCl common library for allowing user to set UPI PIN for e-RUPI
voucher.
2. Issuer Bank/Payer PsP shall adopt a risk-based approach in deciding the number of
e-RUPl vouchers which shall be issued to a single beneficiary.
3. Issuer Bank shall allow reloading of voucher for Government and Corporate use case
until the voucher expires.
4. For Multiple-time use e-RUPl vouchers, once the voucher has expired, the Issuer shall
refund the remaining balance amount back to the Sponsor's source account within T+
3 days (where T is expiry date of the e-RUPI voucher)
5. For One-time use e-RUPl voucher, Issuer shall refund the remaining balance amount
back to the Sponsor's source account post 1st Redemption/Revoke/Expiry whichever
is earlier.
6. e-RUPI voucher shall be revoked by the lssuer Bank/Payer PsP at any point on a real
time basis on the request received from the Sponsor until beneficiary has set the UPl
PIN for that voucher i.e. Once UPl PIN for that voucher is set then the voucher cannot
be revoked by lssuer.
7. Post revoke, the Issuer Bank/Payer PsP shall send SMs to the customer with the
revoke details.
8.  Issuer Bank/Payer PSP shall follow NPCI SMS, voucher template and Brand
guidelines.
9. On receiving a redemption request, the Issuer Bank/Payer PsP shall verify the purpose
code and MCC as per MCC & Purpose Code Mapper.

3. Acquirer Banks/ Entities shall guide redemption centres for onboarding them on the
e-RUPl platform.
4. Acquirer Apps shall follow the voucher redemption checklist, verification code
checklist, and app brand guidelines.
5. To ensure dispute-handling mechanism at Acquirer Bank/Payee PsP end.
6. Redemption of e-RUPI vouchers shall be covered under the extant Dispute Resolution
Framework of NPCI for UPI Help (UDIR).
7. Cash out or funds transfer shall not be permitted for such instruments.
