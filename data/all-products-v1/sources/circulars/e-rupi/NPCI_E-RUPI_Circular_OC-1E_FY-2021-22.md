Subject: Enablement of e-RUPl services
e-RUPI was launched on 2nd August 2021 by Hon'ble Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra Modi
with an intent to include consumer on Digital India Platform who may not be enabled on digita!
payments service.
One-time use e-RUPl as introduced herein shall enable services such as Covid-19 vaccine,
donations and gift vouchers, etc. and can be extended to other services including that of one-time
benefits. e-RUPl has an immediate utility in the form of healthcare vouchers to work as a payment
mechanism to support the Covid-19 vaccination drive.
e-RUPl may be issued in two core categories i.e. Person-to-Person (P2P) and Business to
Consumer (B2C). The circular outlines the scope and rules only for the B2C vouchers.
The end to end transaction involves below entities:
a. Issuer Bank (lssuer): Bank who shall initiates request to create e-RUPl.
Sponsor shall be Corporate, State and Union Government department, business
customer of the bank who shall request bank for creation of e-RUPls.
C. e-RUPI Beneficiary shall be.the person for whom the e-RUPI is issued. An e-RUPl
Beneficiary may not be a bank account holder.
d. Designated Merchant: These are specific voucher acceptance points where e-RUPi
canberedeemed/used.
e. Acquiring Bank (Acquirer): Bank who shall be providing facility/capability to the
designated merchants to accept the e-RUPI (String/QR) for redemption.

B. e-RUPlIssuance:
An Issuer shall ensure to follow below broad guidelines at the time of issuance of e-RUPl:
1. e-RUPI transactions shall have Purpose Code as 19 for Private Corporate voucher and 18
forGovernmentvoucher,as an identifier.
2. MCC for redemption of the e-RUPl shall be specified at the time of issuance. The Issuer
shall use MCC # 8493 specificaly for Covid-19 related healthcare services such as
vaccination, medical test etc. For other use cases, Issuer shall ensure redemption of
e-RUPI for appropriate MCCs as applicable.
3. Up to 10, e-RUPl can be issued on single mobile numberfor Covid-19 & related health care
services. However, the requirement of issuing multiple e-RUPl on single mobile number
shall be configurable as perthe scheme requirements.
4. e-RUPl shall have defined validity as per the use case. This validity shall not be permitted
beyond one year from the date of issuance. Validity for e-RUPls Issued for Covid-19
Vaccination and related health care services purpose shall be 31st March 2022.The
Issuer/Sponsor cannot have an option to change the validity date for Covid-19 Vaccination
and related health care services purpose.

C. e-RUPIRedemption
e-RUPl can be redeemed at a merchant location ("Merchant") which can be acquired to
accept an e-RUPl by an acquiring bank (referred to as the "Acquiring Bank").
Acquiring Bank shall ensure to follow below broad guidelines for the acceptance of e-RUPl: -
1. A e-RUPl Beneficiary can redeem the e-RUPl at any Merchant location falling under the
merchant category as may be defined at the time of issuance of such voucher.
2. Once a redemption request is received, the Acquiring Bank shall check for the correct
Merchant Category Code (MCC) and purpose code. Acquiring Bank shall use MCC # 8493
for Covid-19 related healthcare services such as vaccination, medical test etc.
3. Acquiring Bank shall provide app or capability to merchant to scan the e-RUPl or SMS
string as displayed by Beneficiary.

4. The facility to redeem e-RUPl shall be provided only to qualified Merchants as defined for
the scheme by the Acquiring Bank.
5. At the time of redemption, the Acquiring Bank shall initiate the request to NPCl system.
6. Acquiring Bank shall ensure that the e-RUPl Beneficiary must be validated by an
appropriate ID Validation process at the time of redemption to ensure that the e-RUPl is
not misused.
7. On receiving the success response from NPCl, the Acquiring Bank shall validate the
Beneficiary by means of validating the mobile number using Verification Code or other
possible offline method as may be prescribed by NPCl. For the purpose of Covid-19 related
healthcare services, Acquiring Bank shall mandatorily use Verification Code for e-RUPl
Beneficiaryvalidation.
8. Acquiring Bank shall ensure that service shall be provided happen only after the e-RUPl
has been successfully authorized by Issuer.
9. Acquiring Bank shall be responsible for the reconciliation and settlement process with the
Merchant. Bank may refer "Operational and Settlement Guidelines for e-RUPl" for more
details.
1o. The Issuer/Sponsor shall ensure to send SMS/email to intended e-RUPI Beneficiary on
creation and redemption of e-RUPls.
D.UserAwareness and Controls
Below are the broad level controls to be followed by participating member's:
1. Awareness and communication across all possible channels shall be done by
Issuer/Sponsor to ensure that e-RUPl Beneficiary is fully aware of the redemption
process.
2. Issuer and Acquirer shall follow unique branding and design of e-RUPl as defined by
NPCl in case of QR code as delivery and acceptance mechanism.
3. Issuer and Acquirer shall ensure to deploy adequate checks including validating the e
RUPl parameters during the validation and redemption to ensure that e-RUPl is not
misused.
The applicable pricing for e-RUPl (B2C) shall be informed separately. Member banks are
requested to make a note the circular for compliance and necessary actions.
