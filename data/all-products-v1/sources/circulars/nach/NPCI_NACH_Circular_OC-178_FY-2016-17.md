Presentation of ACH mandates
Please refer to our circular number 163 dated June 07, 2016, on usage of ECS Mandates. The
matter was deliberated in 23rd steering committee of NACH and it has been decided that
with effect from October 01, 2016 only ACH mandate format should be obtained from the
customer by all the constituents.
Member Banks are advised to take note of the same and disseminate the instructions
contained herein to all the concerned.
For any clarifications please write back to ach@npci.org.in
