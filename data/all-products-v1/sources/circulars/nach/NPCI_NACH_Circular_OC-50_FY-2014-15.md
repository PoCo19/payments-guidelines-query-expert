APBFileValidation
NACH system validates the Aadhaar numbers provided in the transactions at the time of file
upload by the memberbanks and the transactions are routed to the destination bank as per the
lIN mapping done with NPCl.In case of transactions where lIN numberof the destination bank
is provided then currently no validation is done on the Aadhaar number pertaining to that
transactions.
2.With effect from 14-06-2014, there will be additional validation on input files uploaded by
sponsor banks with the lN numbers of the destination banks. Even if lIN number of the
destination bank is given, the records will be validated against the mapper database.
3.Henceforth even if liNnumberis provided NACH system will validate theAadhaarnumber
and the mapping of liN numberto that particularAadhaar number. In case of mismatch all such
transactionswill berejectedwiththefollowing reasons.
a) 96-Aadhaarmapping does not exist/Aadhaarnumbernot mappedto lN
b) 80-WrongIIN
95-InactiveAadhaar
4.Memberbanksarerequestedtotakenoteofthechange
