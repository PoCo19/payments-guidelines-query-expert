Implementationof schemecodesforDBT
Reference is drawn to our following circulars:
a) Circular no. 245 dated September 22, 2017
b) Circular no. 247 dated September 30, 2017
C) Circular no. 249 dated 0ctober 12, 2017
As you are aware PFMS has commenced sharing scheme codes and ‘DBT' flag in payment
files for all transactions notified as DBT. Payment files pertaining to non-DBT schemes will
continue to come without scheme code.
You are advised to co-ordinate with PFMs if you are in receipt of any payment file pertaining
to DBT schemes without a scheme code mentioned therein. Such files with DBT flag should
not be uploaded under CS code due to the absence of scheme code.
The CPSMS/NZ user codes are meant for uploading only non-DBT transactions and are
therefore not come under the scope of Office Memorandum F. No. 32(07)/PF-ll/2011/(Vol.
Il) dated February 26, 2016 on settlement of transaction charges.
Important:
As communicated earlier banks should take at most care to use the relevant user code
allotted by NPCl for each scheme, usage of wrong user code for uploading transactions in
NACH will lead to incorrect claim of charges and incentive by NPCl. Sponsor banks will be
responsible for such incorrect claims resulting in excess or short distribution of charges to
the member banks.
In view of the above it has been decided that the non-DBT transactions uploaded in
CPSMS/NZ user codes will be charged and recovered monthly from the Sponsor banks for the
transactionsprocessedfromJanuary01,2018.
