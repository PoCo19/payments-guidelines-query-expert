Integration of Real-Time PAN and Bank Account Validation APl on Income Tax Department's
E-Filing Portal
Reference may be taken from Circular No. 009 (NPCI / 2019-20 / NACH / Circular No. 009), Circular
No. 027 (NPCI / 2018-19 / NACH / Circular No. 027) and Circular No. 27 (NPCI / 2018-19 / NACH /
Circular No. 027) regarding APl Services provided to banks / govt. departments as per the specifications
outlined in respective circulars. Additionally, an APl service is made available for PAN & Bank Account
validation.
This APl will be used by Government departments to verify the customer account details like PAN
validation/Account Status Validation/Account holder name validation from their bank CBS.
As this is a service provided to the Government of India all the Member Banks are advised to take
necessary steps for implementation on priority
Technical specifications and comprehensive integration guidelines for the NACH PAN & Account
Verification APl shall be shared separately with the banks.
The information herein may be disseminated for all concerned.
