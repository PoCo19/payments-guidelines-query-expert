UIDAl OTP verification charges for Aadhaar Authentication in E-Mandates
Aadhaar based E - mandate. has been implemented to enable the wider segment of the
society to use the Aadhaar number for registration of mandate. The Aadhaar based mandate
-telies-onr-authenticatior-of-the-requester through-UiDAt-through-Aadhaar-+-OTp-mechanism:
Reference may be taken from UiDAl circular No. 6 dated May 3, 2023, on notifying Aadhaar
(Pricing of Aadhaar authentication transactions) authentication charges. As per the circular
Aadhaar authentication services shall be charged at 0.50 + GsT for each success or failure
validation.
Authentications that failed. due to a UIDAl-specific error for OTP verifications will not be
charged. The details of error codes that will be excluded from the charging mechanism are
.provided in UIDAl circular No. 04 of 2019..
At the time of monthily billing the authentication charges of UiDAl will be passed on to the
underlying transactions shall be shared with the banks for their internal reconciliation and other
processes. This shall be effective from the go live date of Aadhaar based mandate i.e Feb,
2023..
Member banks may please take note and disseminate the above information to all their
internal teams and other stakeholders.
