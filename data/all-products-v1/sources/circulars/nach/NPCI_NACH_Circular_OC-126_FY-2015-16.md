# Circular No.126 - Removal of miscellaneous reason for ACH DR

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2015-16/NACH/CircularNo.126

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2015-16/NACH/CircularNo.126 August 31, 2015
To,
AllNACHDebitParticipating Banks
RemovalofmiscellaneousreasonsforACHDebit.
This is with reference to our circular NPCl.2015-16/NACH/Circular No.118 dated August
19, 2015.The return reason"Miscellaneous (reason code: 09)"will not be available for the
ACH debit transactions with effect from September 01, 2015. Any return uploaded with
the said reason will be rejected by the system.
Therevised list of ACH debit reason codes are enclosed (annexure I).
Member banks are advised to take note of the same take necessary action.
