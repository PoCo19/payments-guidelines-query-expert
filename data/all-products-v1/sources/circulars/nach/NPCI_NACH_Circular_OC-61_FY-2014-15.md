ACHCreditProduct-Additional files/Sessions
Madam/ Dear Sir,
With reference to our earlier communications on the DBTL re-launch, this time we will be
having both Aadhaar and Account Number based credits to the member banks.
2. Aadhaar based transactions will happen through APB sessions and the Account based
transactions will happen through ACH - Credit (306 file format).
3. Currently ACH credit transactions are processed through a single session per day and the
sessiontimingsareasfollows
ACHCreditSession Timings
Monday - Friday Presentation:10:00AMto12:30PM
Returns : 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM
Saturday Presentation:10:00AMto11:30AM
Returns : 1:00 PM to 2:30 PM
We are expecting huge volume on account of DBTL transactions therefore to help the member
banks to get more processing time we may introduce multiple sessions for ACH credit product.
Hence member banks are requested to make necessary changes in their CBs and be ready to
handlethemultipleACHCredit(lnward)files
timings.
