BHiMSchemeCustomerandMerchantcashbackprocess
Refer to our circular no.221 on “BHiM customer referral bonus & merchant cash back
schemedated April 11,2017,the Government of India has extended the BHiMpromotion
scheme till March 2019 with new features topromote the digital payments through Bharat
Interface for Money (BHiM).The pay-out for the scheme shall be computed as below:
Customer/IndividualcashbackScheme:Dailyfornewuser(forBHiMApp)andmonthlyfor
exiting user basis based on satisfying the eligibility criteria (for BHIM App and BHIM UPI App)
Merchant Cashback: Monthly basis based on satisfying the eligibility criteria (for BHIM app
and BHIM UPI App)
The customercashback scheme will be on the dailybasisand merchant scheme will be run
on monthlybasis and the related transactions will be routed throughNACH system inACH
Credit format for crediting the funds to end beneficiary account. Post the credit, the
customerbank has toupload success responsefileback to NPClconfirming that thefund is
credited.The above change is likely to bring more volume to the system.
The session timingsfor this schemeis givenbelow.
Session Type Presentation Returns (same day)
ACHCR5 BHIMScheme 07:00 hours to 12.00 hours 12:30 hours to 17.30 hours
Customer/Merchant
Government of India has directed that the incentive amount to be credited to the
beneficiary on the day of receipt of inward through NACH system.All the banks are advised
to ensure the following:
1.E Beneficiary accounts to be credited on the same day of the receipt of the inward file.
2.Automation of response file generation as per the specifications issued by NPCl.
3. Capacitatethe systemto handlehighervolume.
4. Submit response file on the same day to NPCl so that Government can get to know the
status.
All thememberbanks should strictlycomply with theprocess detailed above.
