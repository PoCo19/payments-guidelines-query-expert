Inter Bank charges are recovered from the sponsor bank and passed on to thedestination
bank.For interbank charges NPC has been adjusting TDS at the time of recovery and passing
only net off TDs amount to the destination banks.The sponsor banks have been directed to
provide the TDS certificates to destination banks on monthly/quarterly basis to facilitate
accounting at their end.
2. Now after due internal deliberations and seeking expert opinion it has been decided that
witheffect fromApril01,2014TDSwill not be leviedonthe interbank chargesbyNPCl.
3. All the member banks are requested to note the change for accounting purpose.
4. Note that the TDS already adjusted during the financial year 2013-14 should have already
been remitted to the government and the sponsor bank should have issued necessary TDs
certificates to destination bank. Member banks are requested to review the billing done last
yearandtakenecessaryactionwhereverrequired.
5.TDS on the NPCI charges will continued to be levied as per the existingpractices and
member banks are requested to remit the TDs on a monthly basis to the respective
authorities and submit theTDScertificates toNPCl officeonaquarterlybasis.
