In order to help the banks in prioritizing the processing of mandates based on value and TAT it
has been decided to batch the inward files based on the amount slabs (as per the details provide
in the table below.
Mandates will be batched with the combination of destination bank, Request type, variant and
INw identifier and generate the inward files (INw) accordingly. This is applicable for normal
mandates(MMS),APlandLegacyvariantsMandate.

the following,

MMS-CREATE-SBIN-20102015-LVAPI000001-INW.Zip
(2)Mandatesabove3Lakhs
MMS-CREATE-SBIN-20102015-HVAPI000002-INW.Zip
