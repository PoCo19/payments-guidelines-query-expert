NPCl is thankful to you for the support and encouragement for making the National

2. This is with reference to modifications done to the Aadhaar mapper upload data
validation. With effect from 22-01-2014 a validation for duplicate upload of Aadhaar
number by the same member bank has been activated. Henceforth any such duplicate
record in the mapper upload file will be rejected by the system with the reason
“Duplicate UID record”
3. All member banks are requested to take note of the change in the mapper upload
process.
