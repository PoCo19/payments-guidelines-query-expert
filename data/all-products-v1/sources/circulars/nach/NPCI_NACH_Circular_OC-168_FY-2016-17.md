Warehousing of APB andACHdebitfiles
We refer to our circular number 100 dated April 15, 2015 on warehousing facility of NACH
debit files and circular number 166 dated June 24, 2016 on extending the warehousing
feature for ACH credit transactions.
It has now been decided to provide the warehousing feature to Aadhaar based payments
(DBT & DBTL) and ACH debit (306 characters) also from July 05, 2016. Member banks will
be able to upload APB and ACH debit files 7 days in advance to the settlement date. System
will accept files for the same settlement date also.
Sponsor banks are advised to utilise the facility and upload transactions in advance,
wherever possible to avoid last minute uploading. Acceptance/rejection/partial status will
also be made available immediately. On the day of settlement, system will validate for any
blockage and final settlement will be generated. In case any bank is blocked, DEL file will
be generated and sent to the sponsor bank for the transactions pertaining to the blocked
bank, while the other transactions will get processed on the settlement date.
There will be no changes in the timing of the sessions. Destination banks will continue to
get the first inward file around 9:15 a.m.
