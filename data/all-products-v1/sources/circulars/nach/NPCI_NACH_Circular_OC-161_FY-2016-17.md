SMS alert to customers on Mandate status
Pursuant to the 22nd steering committee meeting of NACH held on May 18 2016, it has been
decided that all member Banks should send mandatory SMs to customers as per the events
and standard text given below
. a. . QOn..receipt of .mandates:
Dear customer
Mandate reference No: <provide UMRN> issued' to (Corporate name) with value
<provide mandate amount> and frequency <provide frequency> is received today for
processing. In case of any -issue 'please get in touch with your branch.
b. After rejecting the mandate:
Dear customer
Mandate reference No: provide UmRN issued to (Corporate name) with value
<provide mandate amount> and frequency <provide frequency> was rejected due to
the reason "<reason description>".
Member Banks should comply with the standard text message for all mandates. The changes
should be implemented with effect from June 15 2016.
