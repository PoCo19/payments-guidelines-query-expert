Update on mandate start date
Refer to our circular No.:NPCl/2015-16/NACH/ Circular No 118 dated August 19, on making
the mandate start date as mandatory field both on the physical mandate and the data file
that is provided by the sponsor bank.The effective date for making this mandatory will be
October01,2015.
The sponsor banks are advised to inform their corporates to start getting the start date
column filled before accepting the mandates. Necessary provisions should be made in the
internal software handling mandates for populating the start date.
The above rule does not apply to the mandates that are already submitted by the sponsor
banks and also the mandates that will be processed till September 30, 2015.For these
mandates the member banks are advised to follow the process as is being followed now as per
the NACH mandate guidelines currently in force.
Sponsor banks are advised to take note that the start date field in the data file will be made
mandatory, NPCl is working on implementing this in the NACH application. Separate
communication will be sent informing effective date of this change (Not before October O1,
2015). Member banks may initiate necessary changes accordingly.
