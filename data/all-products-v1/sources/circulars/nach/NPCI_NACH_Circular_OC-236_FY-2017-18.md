To

Mandatesprocessing-uniformity
MandateManagement System has beenoperational since2013,currentlywehave2.71crore
active mandates in the system. It has been brought to our notice that some of the member
banks have started formulating their own rules for acceptance of mandates.Such non-
standardizedprocesseswill createdisparityinacceptance/rejectionofmandatesbyvarious
banks creatingconfusionintheparticipants.
Member banks are advised to follow standard processes in accordance with the procedural
guide lines while processing the mandates. In case there is any suggestion for the overall
improvement of the current process member banks should first bring it to steering
committee (non-members can write to NPCl for putting up to the steering committee)
instead of unilaterally implementing suchnon-standard processes.
Allthememberbanksareadvisedforstrictcompliance.
