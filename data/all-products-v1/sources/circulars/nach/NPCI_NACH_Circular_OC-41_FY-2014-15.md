NACHCreditAdditional Session
Refer toour circularno.39dated March29,2014and circularno.40dated March 31,2014on
introduction of new product variant of NACH credit. We have received requests from the
member banks to extend the timeline for implementation of the proposed new variant of
NACH credit.
2. Considering the request of the banks for time extension it has been decided to continue
with the existing codes as detailed below
a) Header level code:"11"
b)Transaction level code:“22"
3.Further member banks are requested to note that an additional session (between 08 am to
11am)will be runon dailybasis w.e.f.April 03,2014.The additional session is to process the
transactions pertaining to Punjab government,This has the approval of RBl as well. The
member banks are requested to take note and arrange to process the inward transactions
received in the additional session as well and submit the returns on the same day.
