# Circular No.280 - Revised NACH RTGS Holiday

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2017-18/NACH/Circular

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2017-18/NACH/Circular No.280 March 27,2018
To
AllNACHmember banks
RTGS Holiday - April 02, 2018
RBl has declared April 02, 2018 as RTGs holiday on account of Annual closing of banks. As
per the procedural guide lines, NACH also will remain closed on RTGS holiday therefore
there will not be any NACH sessions on April 02, 2018.
Revised holiday list issued by RBI is enclosed.
All member banks are advised to take note and plan accordingly.
For clarifications please write to ach@npci.org.in
