To, 
 
All Members participating in various NPCI Products 
 
 
Madam / Dear Sir, 
 
 
Sub: Guidelines for Self-attestation by members/participants 
 
We refer to our OC. NPCI/2021-22/NACH/Circular No. 001 dated Apr 09, 2021 regarding adherence to 
NPCI guidelines by members/participants. In this connection, we are pleased to inform that we are 
implementing an automated process of self-attestation which shall simplify the task of confirming by 
members/participants on adherence to various NPCI guidelines/circulars. 
 
We have arrived at the self-attestation check-list based on documents such as Procedural Guidelines, 
Operating Circulars, Technical/Data specifications, etc. Members/participants shall submit self-
attestation through the portal confirming compliance. The Self-attestation activity shall be on a periodic 
basis such as quarterly, half yearly and annually. For the first-time submission, we shall seek self-
attestation for the period starting from April, 2020 to March, 2021. 
 
We have also sought user details through mail dated April 26, 2020 in the format given in Annexure A 
for allowing access to the system by members/participants. We would like to reiterate that 
members/participants who have still not taken access to the portal are requested to submit the details in 
the format URGENTLY. 
 
The Self-attestation activity for the FY 2020-21 shall begin from May, 2021 and the 
members/participants are expected to submit the self-attestation by 18th June, 2021. 
 
Please make a note of the contents herein and disseminate the information contained herein to the 
officials concerned.  
 
Yours faithfully, 
 
Sd/- 
Praveena Rai
