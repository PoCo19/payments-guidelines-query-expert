Migration of NACHCredit from 156 Characters format to 306Characters format
Refer toourcircular #NPCl/2015-16/NACH/Circular No 116 dated August 14,2015 on the
migration to 306 file format (ACH format).
We have migrated majority of the banks and volume to NACH platform. Now we plan to
migrate all the Credit (156 format) transactions to 306 file format. The plan is to complete
the migration latest by November 30, 2015. With effect from December 01, 2015 all the
credit transactions will be processed only in 306fileformat.
As ACH credit format is intended for end to end automation it is important that member
banks automate their inward processing
Road map for migration

All the member banks are advised to make necessary arrangements and report compliance to
adhere to the schedule of December 01, 2015 for complete migration of credit transactions to
ACH credit (306) format.
