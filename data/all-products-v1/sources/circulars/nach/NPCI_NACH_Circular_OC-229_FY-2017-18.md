# Circular No.229 Penalty on multiple representations of a transaction returned with account closed and no such account

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2017-18/NACH/CircularNo.229

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2017-18/NACH/CircularNo.229 June15,2017
To
All member banks participating in NACH
Penaltyon multiplerepresentations of a transaction returned with reason
“Account closed"&“No such account”
In order to reduce returns the member banks should avoid presenting transactions that are
already returned with various reasons like account closed, no such account.
The matter was discussed in the NACH steering committee and it was decided that banks
should not represent a transaction that has already been returned twice with the reasons
stated above.If any bank continue to present the returned transactions then from third
instance of presentation of such returned instruments will attract a penalty of Rs.25 per
instanceofpresentation.
NpCl will calculate thepenalty tobe levied on the presenting banks and recover the penalty
along with applicable taxes on monthly basis. This applies to all the products under NACH
exceptDBTandDBTLrelatedtransactions.
Member banks are advised to take note and inform all the concerned entities to enable them
to put process in place to ensure that they do not represent cases that are already returned
twice.ThiswillbeeffectivefromJuly01,2017.
