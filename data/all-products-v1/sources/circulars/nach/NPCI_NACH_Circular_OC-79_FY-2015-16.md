Waiver of “On-us” DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) Transaction Charges 
 
1. Keeping in view the national importance of the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme of 
Government of India; NPCI has decided to waive-off the “On-us” DBT transaction charges 
w.e.f. 01st January, 2015 to 31st December, 2015. 
 
2. The revised charge structure applicable for DBT transactions w.e.f. 01st January, 2015
