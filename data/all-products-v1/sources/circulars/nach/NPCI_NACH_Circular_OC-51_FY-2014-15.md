NACH Creditplatformis arobust system designed to handle large volumeof transactions.We
have witnessed significant growth in terms of number of participant banks and transactions on
thisplatforminthelastsixmonth.
2.As you are aware that various corporates pay their dividends in the months of June,July
and August. Considering the fact that these payouts are done through electronic mode NACH
Creditplatformwillwitnesshighthroughputoftransactions.
3. It is therefore essential that the banks allocate sufficient resources to ensure that the
incoming transactions are processed, beneficiary accounts are credited on the settlement
date itself and returned transactions are submitted on time with appropriate reasons thereof.
4.Member banks are requested to take note and accord top priority for making necessary
arrangements for timely processing.
