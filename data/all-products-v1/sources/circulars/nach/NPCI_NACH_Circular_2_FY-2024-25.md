NPCL
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATION OFINDIA
NPCI/2024-25/NACH/002 July 17, 2024
To
All NACH member banks
Changes in MMS data sharing frequency
allowing the customers to issue mandates for various purposes. The current frequency of sharing
inward and response files with banks is at EOD.
In order to improve the customer experience, it has been decided to provide the data of mandates
registered, at a higher frequency instead of one time i.e. at the time of EOD. This change will help the
corporates to present the transactions on the same day of registering the mandates.
The change in MMs data sharing frequency will improve customer experience and hence member
banks are required to make necessary changes in their system to consume inward and response files
as and when provided by NPCl. Timings are provided in Annexure I.
The change shall be implemented w.e.f. September 01, 2024
