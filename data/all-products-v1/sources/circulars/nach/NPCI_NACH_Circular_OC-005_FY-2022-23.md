Account Verification Service - GST on lnterchange Fees
NPCl has agreed to provide account verification [AV] service to few Government entities like EPFO,
GsTN, Government of Tripura and Government of Uttarakhand. Some other entities are in pipeline at
development stage.
With reference to our circular no 23 dated March 19, 2020 the process of payment of switching fees and
interchange by the Government departments involve the following:
1. NPCl submits the claim letters to respective Government entities on a monthlylquarterly basis.
2. The department after scrutinising the claim letters remits consolidated amount to NPCI (Switching
Fees of NPCl along and Interchange Fees of the destination banks after deduction of TDS on GST
or Income Tax if applicable.)
 3. The amount thus received by NPCl is then distributed to the destination banks accordingly.
retainirig its share of switching fees.
In view of thie above, destination banks are advised to note that it is the responsibility of the destination
banks to raise necessary invoice and discharge the GsT liability with the Government. NPCl will not be
liable for any non-compliance due to non-issuance of invoice and non-payment of GsT to the
Government.
The information may be disseminated to the concerned department/team.
