All NACHSponsorBanks/Corporates/lmplementingAgencies of DBTProgramme
NewCBSAccountnumbersforPostalAccounts
ReferenceisdrawntotheD0letterno01-03/2016-DBTCelldatedNovember6,2017from
DDG-RB&PlanningofDepartmentofPost.
As you are aware that GPO (lndia Post) is a participant in NACH platform and has been
enabled to process both Aadhaar based (APB) as well as Account based (ACH) CR transactions
as a destination bank for credit of benefits to the account holders of Postal Savings Bank
Accounts (POSB).
The account numbers pertaining to GPO that are in circulation with the banks and other
credit transactions the account numbers provided in the Inward file need to be CBS account
number.
There may be lot of beneficiaries/payees' holding account with GPO (lndia Post) receiving
benefits with old non CBS (Sanchay Post) account numbers in off-line mode. There is a need
to update the data base of Agencies with new CBS account numbers for routing transactions
through NACH and comply with the guidelines on DBT and others.
As advised by Postal Department the Agencies should approach the nodal officers (list in
Annexure I)of different states,provide the details in the enclosedformat (Annexure ll)to
receive thenew CBS account numbers forupdationof beneficiarydatabase suitablyto avoid
transactions getting returned.
Sponsor banks are advised to disseminate the information and encourage the
Corporates/Other Implementing Agencies to undertake this one-time activity to enable
seamless credit transactions to the account holders of GPO with minimal error.
