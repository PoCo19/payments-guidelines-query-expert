# Circular No.220 - Penalty for mandates beyond TAT

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2016-17/NACH/Circular

<!-- Page 1 -->

NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPC1/2017-18/NACH/Circular No.220 April 04, 2017
To
All NACH Member Banks
Penalty for mandates beyond TAT
Refer to our Circular No. NPCI/2016-17/NACH/Circular No. 211 dated February 01, 2017 on
penalty for mandates beyond TAT with effect from April 01, 2017 (copy enclosed). In view
of the annual closure of accounts for the banks, it has been decided to extend the deadline
for imposing penalty on mandates beyond TAT, by 15 days. Now the penalty will be
applicable with effect from April 17, 2017.
All the other clauses in the circular referred above will remain unchanged. All member banks
are advised to take note and make necessary arrangements to process the mandates within
the specified TAT.
