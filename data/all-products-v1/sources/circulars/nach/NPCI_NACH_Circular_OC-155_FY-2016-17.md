NarrationinCustomerStatementforNACHTransactions
Narration in the statement or passbook is very important for the end customer to know the
source of credit ordebit intheir accounts.RBl had also insisted this vide their circular DPss
(CO) EPDD No. 788/04.03.01/2010-11 dated October 8, 2010.
Werefertoourcircularnumber7datedMay31,2013andcircularnumber36datedMarch
7, 2014 on providing valid narration in the input file by the sponsor banks or corporates.
This was insisted to be given at the record level in the user name column.
It has been observed that some corporates/sponsor banks are giving some numerical values
in the user name/narration field while uploading the NACH transactions. This is leading to
a number of customer queries at the destination bank's end. End customer is not in a
position to ascertain from where credit has come to their account or to whom the debit has
gone from their account.
Sponsor Banks must advise the user institutions to provide “User Name' and "User Ref. No."
in the relevant fields with meaningful information so that the destination bank can pass on
the same to the customer which helps to identify, track and reconcile the transactions.
All sponsor banks and their corporates are requested to take a note of the same and ensure
strict adherence to providing valid narration across all transactions processed through NACH.
In case of any clarifications you can write back to ach@npci.org.in
