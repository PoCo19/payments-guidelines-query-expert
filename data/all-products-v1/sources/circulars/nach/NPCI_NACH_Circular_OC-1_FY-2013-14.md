SUB:-RETURNREASONCODESFORNACHAPPLICATION
Banks participating on the NACH Application need to ensure adherence to the NACH
Return reason codes. The list of the codes pertaining to NACH Credit and NACH Debit
transactions has been provided hereunder for your reference as an Annexure- A.
Banks need to ensure that Return reason "Miscellaneous" is used only if the reasons for
return is not implied vide the other return reason codes.
For any further help required from our side, you may please be in touch with your
respective relationship manager.
