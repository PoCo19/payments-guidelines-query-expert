To,
AllNACHSponsorBanks-MGNREGA
Sub: - Transaction charges for transactions related to components other than wages
This is to inform that the Programme Division - Rural Employment, Ministry of Rural
Development, Government of India has clarified that the transactions related to Material
and other Administrative expenses does not come under the scope of Direct Benefit
Transfers. Hence NPCI has been advised not to raise the invoice for charges pertaining to
transactions related to material on MORD. The same has the concurrence of Department
of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
In view of the above Sponsor Banks are advised to review the Memorandum of
Understanding (MoU) executed with MORD or the Implementing Agency of the states on
the compensation for the services rendered for such transactions which is similar to other
non- DBT transactions and take up with the respective agencies for settlement of charges.
Please note that NPCI will recover the switching fee of Rs. 0.15 and interbank charges of
Rs. O.25 per transaction with a debit in the settlement account of the Sponsor Banks for
such transactions uploaded to APB and ACH.
NPCl will continue to raise invoices for charges and incentive on MGNREGA related
transactions on MORD and pursue with the ministry for settlement.
