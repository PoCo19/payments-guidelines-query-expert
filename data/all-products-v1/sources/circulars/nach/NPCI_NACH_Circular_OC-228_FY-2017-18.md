PenaltyonmandatespendingbeyondTAT
Referto our CircularNo.22oon“PenaltyformandatesbeyondTAT",thiswaskeptin
abeyance as a few member banks sought time to put the process in place and augment the
resources to process the mandates as per the TAT. The process of levying penalty on
mandates pending beyond TAT will be commenced with effect from May 11, 2017. The
penalty amount willbe charged on monthlybasis,the relevant MiSwill be sharedwiththe
banks.
All memberbanks areadvisedtoensurethat mandates areprocessed and uploaded within
the specified TAT to avoid the penalty. Banks may also put necessary process in place to
reconciletheacknowledgment receivedfromNACHwiththereturn/responsefilesuploaded,
identify rejections, if any, take corrective action and re-upload suchrejected mandates.
