# Circular No. 19 - Deactivation of Aadhaar Numbers in NPCI Mapper for Closed Aadhaar Eabled Bank Accounts (AEBA).

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2013-14/NACH/Circular19

NPCI

To,
All APBSystemParticipatingBanks
Deactivation of AadhaarNumbers inNPCl MapperforClosed Aadhaar Enabled Bank
Accounts (AEBA)
Madam / Dear Sir,
As part of the APB System, all participating banks seed the Aadhaar numbers received from the
customers, first in the core banking system of the bank (making the account as Aadhaar
Enabled Bank Account (AEBA) and subsequently in the NPCl mapper on a daily basis.
2. In case, if a customer closes his/her Aadhaar Enabled Bank Account (AEBA), banks should
implement a process to deactivate all such Aadhaar numbers from NPCI mapper.This process
has to becarried out on adaily basis.
3. This is required to ensure that the Aadhaar numbers seeded in the bank's core banking
system are consistent with Aadhaar numbers seeded in the NPCl mapper. This will also ensure
that the transactions are correctly routed to the banks for existing accounts and help in
reducing the return transactions with the reason"Account Closed or Transferred".
4. All APB participating banks are advised to make a note of the same and ensure compliance.
