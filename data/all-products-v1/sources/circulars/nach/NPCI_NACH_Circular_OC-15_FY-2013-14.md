Madam/Dear Sir,
The Steering committee of NACH in the meeting convened on 23rd August, 2013, has decided to
start multiple sessions for APB System. This is to make the inward files available to the
member banks in different lots to give them more time for processing the inward transactions.
2. The revised APBs session timings are given below and will be effective from 01st October,
2013.
Days Presentation Session Timings Return Session Timings
SessionI 08:00AM-11:30AM 02:00PM-03:00PM
Week Days
Session Il 11:30AM-01:30PM 04:00PM-05:00PM
Single
Saturday** 08:00AM-11:30AM 02:00PM-03:00PM
Session
Single Session on Saturday
3. Please note that the return sessions are linked to the respective presentation sessions and
therefore member banks are requested to process the transactions within the stipulated time
and submit the return/response file as per the relevant return session timings.
4. All APB participating banks are requested to make a note of the additional session timings
and make suitable arrangements for processing the transactions at their end.
