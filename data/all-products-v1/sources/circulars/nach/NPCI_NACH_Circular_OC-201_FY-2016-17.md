# Circular No 201 - NACH Mandate Category

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2016-17/NACH/CircularNo.201

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI
NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATIONOF INDIA
NPCI/2016-17/NACH/CircularNo.201 December 05,2016
To
AllNACHMemberBanks
NACH Mandate Category
Refer to our circular No 165 dated June 07, 2016 on the pre-printed stationary wherein it
was advised that cancellation of pre-printed option of ‘as and when presented'should not
be construed as material alteration and the destination banks should not return such
mandates forthe reasonof material alteration.
It has been brought to our notice that banks are resorting to such returns for want of
customer authentication for cancellation of ‘as and when' frequency option. Such returns
are causing inconvenience to the corporates and customers. All the member banks are
advised to issue necessary operating guidelines to the staff to not return mandates for the
abovedetailed reason.
This has to be implemented with immediate effect.
