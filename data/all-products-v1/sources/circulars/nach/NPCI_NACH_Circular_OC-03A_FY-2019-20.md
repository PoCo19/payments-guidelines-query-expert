To
All NACH member banks
Subject: Implementation of E-Mandate on NACH - approval of RBl - Clarification
We refer to our circular no.003 dated April 24, 2019 on the above subject.
It is clarified that the customer sets the mandate on his underlying bank account using the
National Automated Clearing House (NACH) platform. The customer will have a choice to
use either internet banking or debit card credentials to authenticate the mandate that are
2 factor authentication (2FA) compliant validated by customer's bank. Transactions shall
be initiated by the banks using NACH platform to debit customer's account using his details
such as IFsC + account on which the mandate has been successfully registered.
All stakeholders may please take note of this and disseminate the information contained
herein to all concerned.
