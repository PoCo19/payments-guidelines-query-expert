# Circular No 24 - Facility for cancellation of mandates extension of time

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2020-21/NACH/Circular

NPCI/2020-21/NACH/Circular No. 024 
 
 
 
          December 30, 2020 
 
To 
All Member banks participating in NACH 
Facility for cancellation of mandates to be provided to customers – extension of time. 
Refer our circular no NPCI/2020-21/NACH/Circular No.013 dated September 11, 2020 on 
facility for cancellation of mandates to be provided to customers. It was mandated that all 
entities participating in NACH should enable this option for their customers before December 
31, 2020.  
We have received representation from multiple corporates and banks for extension of the date 
to March 31, 2021, accordingly the date stands extended to March, 31, 2021.  All the 
stakeholder may note that the Corporates and banks (this applies to only those banks that 
present their own mandates but not to the destination banks) providing this facility is 
mandatory for participation in NACH. 
The Corporates & Banks participating in NACH as per the above criteria should submit the 
letter confirmation (Annexure I & II) of implementation before the cut – off date.  Corporates 
should submit the form through their sponsor banks, sponsor bank should verify and satisfy 
itself about the implementation of the facility by the corporate before submitting the form to 
NPCI. 
The stakeholders may note that the entities that fail to implement and submit the confirmation 
as detailed above before March 31, 2021 shall be disabled in NACH with effect from April 01, 
2021 till they comply with the requirement. There will not be any further extension on this. 
The information herein may be disseminated for all concerned. 
With ward regards 
 
Giridhar G.M 
Chief – Offline product operations & technology.
