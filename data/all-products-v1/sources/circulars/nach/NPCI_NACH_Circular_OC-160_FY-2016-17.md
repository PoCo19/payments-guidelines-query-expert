# Circular No.160 Additional session for ACH Debit presentation

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2016-17/NACH/

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCL
NATIONALPAYMENTS CORPORATION OFINDIA
NPCI/2016-17/NACH/ Circular No. 160 June 06, 2016
To
All NACH member banks
Additional. session for ACH Debit presentation
Madam / Dear Sir,
It has: been decided to have an additional session for ACH Debit presentation with effect
from June 15, 2016. The session timings are as follows
Session name Presentation Returns
ACHDR Present 09:00 AM to 11:00 AM 01:00 PM to 04:00 PM
ACHDR Present2 11:30 AM to 01:30 PM 03:00 PM to 05:00 PM
As majority of the transactions are with pre-determined frequency sponsor banks should try
to upload the transactions in the first session.. Only the residual transactions should be
uploaded in the second session.
All the member Banks are advised to take a note of the additional session and changes in
timings and make necessary arrangements for processing the inward files.
