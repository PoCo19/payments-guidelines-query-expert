Legacytransaction migrationprocess
Reference may be taken from Circular No.006 on“"Legacy mandate amendment"dated May
28, 2018.NPCl is working with the member banks on verification of legacy transaction data
with the NACH Debit (156format)transactions data processed by the banks in the last one
year with routing code, utility code and account number as matching fields.
Post this verification,NPC will share both matched and non-matched data to the sponsor
and destination banks concerned. In case of matched records (i.e.transactions processed
against those mandates during the last one year), destination banks must make necessary
arrangements to process the inward transactions received in ACH 306 format. In-case of
non-matched records (i.e.there was no transaction processed during the last one year), the
destination banks have the option to cancel those mandates at their discretion or make
arrangements to process the inward transactions received against those mandates.
Sponsor banks, may please note, that the transactions, pertaining to those mandates
identified and marked for cancellation, will not be allowed for presentation in ACH Debit.
Sponsor banks should communicate cancelled mandate data to the respective corporates
for necessary updation at their end. In case valid mandate copy is submitted as evidence by
any Corporate through their sponsor banks (for the cancelled mandates), NPCl, in co-
ordination with the destination banks will arrange for re-upload of the mandates after
confirming with the destination bank, this is will be done on case to case basis.
The migration will be carried out bank wise i.e. the destination bank that will be ready to
process in 306 format will be migrated to the 306 format and disabled from 156 format.
Corporates and Sponsor banks should present the data in 306 format only on the migrated
banks and in 156 format on the banks that are live on 156 format.This parallel processing
will continue till such time all the banks are migrated to 306 format (this is expected to be
completed by September, 2018). NPCl will provide notice of 10 days before migrating the
bank to 306 format to help the Corporates and Sponsor banks to do necessary changes for
submitting the presentation files in 306 format.
