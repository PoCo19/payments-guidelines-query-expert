NPCL
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOF INDIA
NPCI/2016-17/NACH/CircularNo.199 November29,2016
To
All member banks participating in NACH
Change in session linkage for NACH salary & pension files
We refer to our circular no.179 dated Aug 01, 2016 on NACH - Corporate salary/pension
product. It has been decided to allow the transactions pertaining salary product as of part
of all credit sessions. This will allow banks and their corporates an option of providing files
in batches.
Statistics and Information Management - Division of Household Surveys, all the salary
transactions to be uploaded using the salary product only (refer to our circular NPCl/2016-
17/NACH Circular no.188 dated September, 2016 on salary product)
The member banks are advised to take a note of the following points while processing the
salary and pension files,
Sponsorbank
1. Upload files pertaining to salary and pension related files with product identifier
"SAL"&“PEN"
Destinationbank
1.Separate Inward files will be received with the product identifier.
2. Provide response to all the transactions received.
If you need any further clarification, please write to ach@npci.org.in
