NPCL

NPCI/2016-17/NACH/CircularNo.157 May 02, 2016
To
AllNACHMemberbanks
Extensionforreceivingthe ECSmandateform
We refer to our circular number 151 dated February 25, 2016 extending the date of
accepting the EcS mandate till April 30, 2016.
that even though majority of the destination banks who are processing the mandates in the
NACH system within the TAT of 5 days, there are few banks which are still not processing
the mandates with the stipulated TAT. Hence it has been decided to further extend the
window for acceptance of EcSdebit mandate till May 31,2016.Member banks are advised
to use ACH mandate only on all the banks that are processing the mandates within TAT and
EcS forms only as an alternative in case of Non-live banks or banks that are not processing
the mandates on time.
Member banks are advised to review their internal mandate processing and provide
sufficient man power to adhere to the stipulated timeline of T+5 for processing the
mandates presented on them.
Member banks are advised to take note of the same and disseminate the instructions
contained herein to all theconcerned.
