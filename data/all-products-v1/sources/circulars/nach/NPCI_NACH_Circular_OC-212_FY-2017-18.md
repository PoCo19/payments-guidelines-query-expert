# Circular No 212 - Capturing of beneficiary name and account mandatory in APB as per CBS

Circular/reference number: OC-212

NPCI
NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATION OF INDIA
NPC1/2016-17/NACH/Circular No. 212 February 02, 2017
To
All 'NACH member banks
Capturing of beneficiary name & account mandatory in APB as per CBS
Refer to NPCI Circular Number 204 on “Beneficiary name and account Number mandatory
in APB Return File" the beneficiary name & account should be populated by the
destination bank from the Core Banking Solution (CBS). As the Aadhaar based payments
are made purely on the basis of Aadhaar numbers it has been decided to discontinue the
practise of providing the beneficiary name in the inward file generated from NACH system.
The destination banks should make necessary provisions to populate the beneficiary name
for all the successful transactions at the time of submitting the return file. You may also
refer to our Circular Number 101 dated April 24, 2015.
Note that there is no change in the file format or content submitted by the sponsor banks.
Sponsor banks should continue to provide the beneficiary name in the input file as is being
done currently.
In the response file the beneficiary name and the account number value given by the
destination bank will be provided to sponsor bank for their consumption and onward
transmission to the government departments or corporates as the case may be.
This change will be made effective from February 13, 2017. Member banks are advised to
take note and ensure compliance.
