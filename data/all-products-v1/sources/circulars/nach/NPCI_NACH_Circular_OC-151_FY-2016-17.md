ExtensionforreceivingtheECSmandateform
We refer to paragraph 1 under Mandates in Circular number 148 dated February 05, 2016 -
reproduced belowfor ready reference under Note- in connection with the updates of Steering
CommitteeMeetingheld onJanuary 19,2016.It was advised therein that NACHmemberbanks
shouldaccept onlythe new AcH mandate formforthe fresh debit instructions witheffect from
February 16, 2016.
Considering the requests from user institutions/member banks and due to the ensuing financial
year closing, it is now decided to extend the date to accept the EcS mandate form till April 3o,
2016. The instructions to accept ACH mandate form for fresh debit instructions will be effective
from1st May 2016.
Memberbanks arerequested to take note of the same and disseminate the instructions contained
herein to all concerned.
