# Circular No.211 - Penalty for mandates beyond TAT

To
AllNACHmemberbanks
Penaltyfor mandates beyondTAT
TheNACHsteeringcommittee inthemeetingheld onDecember 16,2016hasapproved the
proposal for levyingpenalty on the mandates pending beyond the agreed TAT.Penalty of
Rs. 10/- per mandate will be levied as per the guidelines provided below.
Mandatesbelow3lakhs:
1. Oto7days-Nopenalty-IncentiveofRs.5.0o/-permandate
8to 10days-Nopenalty-Noincentive
Beyond10days-Noincentive-PenaltyofRs.10.00/-permandate
Mandates above3 lakhs:
0 to 10 days - No penalty - Incentive of Rs. 5.00/- per mandate
Beyond10days-Noincentive-PenaltyofRs.10.00/-permandate
Member banks are advised to take note and make necessary arrangements to process the
mandates within the agreed TAT. The Instructions contained herein may be disseminated
to all the concerned.
ThiswillbeeffectivefromApril01,2017.
For any clarifications please writeback to ach@npci.org.in
