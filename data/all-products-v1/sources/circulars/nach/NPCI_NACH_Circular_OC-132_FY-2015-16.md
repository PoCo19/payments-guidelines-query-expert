AllNACHMemberBanks
Implementation of new Mandate format
Refer to our circular no. 131 dated September 19 2015, on revised new Mandate format,
wherein we advised the member Banks to implement new mandate format effective from Oct
01 2015. This was done to enable destination Banks to process the mandates within the agreed
date. On analyzing the sample data on the mandates presented during the month of Sep 2015
it had been observed that, member banks are continuing to present the mandates in old format.
We advise the member banks to present the mandates only in the new format with effect from
Nov012015.
