To

Reductionoffiles availabilityinSFGfrom07daysto03workingdays
Currently the files uploaded / downloaded by the banks from NACH platform are made
availabletothe banks for o7days, now it has been decidedto reduce the files andreports
availability to 03 working days in SFG (Upload/Download) i.e.files beyond 3 working day will
bearchived and will not beavailable in SFG GUI (front end).
All the member banks should ensurethatthe files are downloaded ondailybasis and the
copies of all the files downloaded and uploaded are maintained at their end for their internal
use.
Memberbanks areadvised to takenote.This changewill be effective from September21,
2018.
