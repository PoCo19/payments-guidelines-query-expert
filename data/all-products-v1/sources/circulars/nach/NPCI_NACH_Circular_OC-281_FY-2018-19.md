# Circular No.281 - Aadhaar encryption and masking of customer information

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2017-18/NACH/CircularNo.281

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCL
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2017-18/NACH/CircularNo.281 March 28, 2018
To
AllNACHMemberbanks
Aadhaarencryptionandmaskingofcustomerinformation
NPCI will be carrying out certain activities on Aadhaar mapper for compliance to the
statutory requirements,theactivitywillbecommenced from09:00PMonMarch29,2018
and likely to be completed before midnight of April 02, 2018. During this period the banks
will not be able to do fresh inserts or other Aadhaar movement related updates to the
mapper.Further the facilities like look up, APl services also will not be available to any of
the stakeholders.
There will not be any impact on Aadhaar based transaction processing on March 31st, 2018
because of the mapper related activity, banks can continue topresent the Aadhaar related
transactions on that day as usual.
Member banks and other stakeholders are advised to take note of this and plan their
activitiesaccordingly.
Foranyclarificationpleasewritebanktoach@npci.org.in
