Revised SessionTiming forACHDEBITPresentation 1st session
Referto NPCI Circular No.160Aon“Additional Session for ACHDebit"member banks has
been provided with three session for the ACH debit transactions. In order to make the
inward files available to the destination bank early it has been decided that ACH session 1
be closed at 07:30 am instead of 09:00 am.
The sponsor banks can upload the presentation files 7 days in advance to the value date of
the transactions. NPCl will initiate the closure of the session by 07:30 am and release the
inward to the destination bank by 08:15 am after posting the settlement.
The proposed change are effective from August 10, 2017.
