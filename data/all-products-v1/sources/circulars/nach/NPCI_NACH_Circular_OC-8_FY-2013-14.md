Participation in NACH-CECS credit product
We are pleased to inform that CECS Credit product has been launched by NPCl.This is a
robust system and it is designed to handle large volume to cater to the growing number
of transactions. Some of the salient features of the system are
1.On line validation with response for reject with proper reasons
2. Facility for partial acceptance of the files
3. Capability to do settlement on T + 0 basis
4. Highly secure system with dual authentication for logging in to application and
transaction files signed digitally
5. Capability to handle high volume i.e. 10 million transactions per day
6. Various Mis formats to cater to the different needs of the member banks
7. Simple to use as no changes are required to be done by the banks in their existing
format
2. Further, as on date 131 member banks i.e. 116 banks that are already participating in
NECS clearing and 15 new banks are on boarded to participate in CECS clearing.The list
of participants in provided in the Annexure I.
We request all the member banks to start processing transactions through the CECs
Credit system. Further as we are anticipating the volume to go up soon, the
adhere to the following session timings:

Returns 3:00PMto5:00PM 2:00PMto3:00PM
