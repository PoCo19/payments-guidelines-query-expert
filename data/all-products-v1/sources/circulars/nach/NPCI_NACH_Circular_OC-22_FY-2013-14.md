# Circular No.22 - Increasing Mandate Approval Cycle to 5 Business Days

NPCI

NPCI/2013-14/NACH/Circular22 18thNovember,2013
To,
AllNACHDebitParticipatingBanks
Increasing Mandate approval cycle to 5business days.
Madam/Dear Sir,
As part of theNational AutomatedClearingHouse(NACH)-Debit,MandateManagement System
(MS), it is the responsibility of the Receiving Bank to accept/ reject the mandate raised on
thembySponsorBank.
2.NPCl's Mandate Management System currently follows a 3 business days cycle from the date
of initiation of the mandate,for the Receiving banks to accept or reject a mandate.
3.Based on the feedback from the banks, it is proposed to increase the cycle to 5 business
days from 22nd November 2013.
4.This will givea larger processing window to thereceiving banks to act on the mandate.NPCi
will conduct periodic review on the mandate approval cycle in-order to assess the possibility of
reducing thetimelinein future.
5. All ACH Debit participating banks are requested to make note of the same and ensure
compliance.
