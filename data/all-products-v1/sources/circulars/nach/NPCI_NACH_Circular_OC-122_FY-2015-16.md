Changes in bank Mis
Changes have been made in banks Mis module. The reports available in each tab are given in
annexure l.
The reports in recon current day tabs will have the data of that particular business day. For
the reports before a particular business day, banks have to view the recon history tab.
DMS, MMS, Mapper & Audit reports tab will have the list of reports as earlier.
Memberbanks arerequested to be guided accordingly.
