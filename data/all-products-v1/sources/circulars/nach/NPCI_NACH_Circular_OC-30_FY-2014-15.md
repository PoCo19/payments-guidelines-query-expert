To
All NACH APB Banks
Processingof FilesreceivedonRegional Holidays
NpCl is thankful to you for the support and encouragement for making the National
Automated Clearing House (NACH) a successful platform for processing of payments which are
bulk and repetitive in nature. It is our endeavor to provide the best of services to the member
banks and ensure that the improvement in quality at all times.
2. We draw your attention to clause13 (Clearing Timings) of Procedural Guidelines of NACH
system where in it is decided that NACH system will work on all days except Sundays and
Holidays as defined by RBI for RTGS.
3. It has been observed that a few member banks are not making necessary arrangements for
processing the inward transactions on local holidays though they don't fall under the RTGS
holidays (extract of the clause in Annexure I). Transactions processed through NACH system
pertaining to various products need to be settled and returns should be submitted on the
same day. Any laxity on this part will lead to delayed credit and late returns causing
inconvenience to the customers and government departments for subsidy related payments.
4. All member banks are requested to ensure that sufficient staff is available on all the
working days of NACH (non-RTGS holidays) so that the inward transactions are processed on
time. We are attaching the list of RTGs holidays 2014 for your kind reference.
5. Request all member banks to take a note and ensure compliance.
For any queries/further help required, please feel free to email at nachsupport@npci.org.in
