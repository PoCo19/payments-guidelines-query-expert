To,
AllNACH Member Banks
Subject - Simplified Mandates- Extension of Limits
Overview Type:
Circular
Reference may be taken from circular- NPCi/2023-
simplification and harmonization of limit for all variants of
mandates. The limit for e-mandates registered through Product / Brand
simplified authentication is now being revised. NACH
Scope and Applicability
This circular shall be applicable to all NACH Members
processing mandate registration via simplified authentication Category
mechanism (Simplified Aadhaar, PAN and Customer ID) E-Mandates
Operational Directions
1. Extension of Limit Member
With effect from the mentioned effective date, the limit for All NACH Member Banks
mandates registration via simplified authentication mechanism
(Simplified Aadhaar, PAN and Customer ID) shal be revised
from  15,000/- to  50,000/- Published Date
May 22, 2026
2. Adoption
a) All Members shall ensure that the revised limit is
incorporated for simplified authentication mechanism. Effective Date
b) The limit is advised to be kept configurable to Juty 01, 2026
incorporate future changes.
Member banks to ensure adoption by all their
associated NACH participants (including merchants,
corporates and payment aggregators).
