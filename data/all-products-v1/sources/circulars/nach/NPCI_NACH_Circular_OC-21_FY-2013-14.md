A kind reference is invited to NPCI circular ref. nos. NPCI:2013-14:NACH:Circular 7 dated 31st 
May, 2013 on “Transactions Processed on National Automated Clearing House (NACH) System” 
and NPCI:2013-14:NACH:Circular 13 dated 20th August, 2013 on “Processing of Transactions on 
NACH APB and EBT System”. Full version of Circular 7 and 13 are available on NPCI website 
under 
the 
link 
http://npci.org.in/documents/Circular_No_7.pdf 
and 
http://npci.org.in/documents/Circular_No_13.pdf respectively. 
 
2. Vide these circulars, among other things, all NACH System participating banks were advised 
to put in place necessary infrastructure to process atleast 5 lakh transactions per session 
(within 90 minutes) on NACH System. 
 
3. Above stated processing capability of NACH System is imperative for the banks keeping in 
view the continuous increase in number of transactions being processed through NACH System. 
 
4. We request member banks to benchmark their NACH System processing capabilities as well.
