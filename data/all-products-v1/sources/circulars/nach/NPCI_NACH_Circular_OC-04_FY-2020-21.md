Aggregator portability  
Reference may be taken from circular no. 230 dated June 15, 2017 on “Corporate portability 
for corporates under aggregators”. It has been decided to extend the facility for legacy 
mandates as well. The process and documents as detailed in our circular no. 230 will remain 
the same.  Considering that the process has been in place for a considerable time and the 
banks have set up the systems and process in place for updating the new utility code in 
mandates, the cooling period has been revised to 7 working days. 
Member banks are advised to take note and disseminate the information to all the concerned.
