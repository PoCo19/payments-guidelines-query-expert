Waiver of APB (Aadhaar Payment Bridge) Transaction Charges 
 
 
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) implemented and operationalised Aadhaar 
Payment Bridge (APB) and subsequently migrated APB system onto NACH (National 
Automated Clearing House) platform. 
 
2. APB system was implemented to promote financial inclusion and thereby ensuring that 
customers receive benefits & subsidies of various state/central government schemes 
directly into their bank accounts using the Aadhaar numbers under the Direct Benefit 
Transfer (DBT).  
 
3. Keeping in view the national importance of the project, NPCI Board has decided to offer 
APB transaction processing at no charge to the banks (including interchange fee) for the 
period 01st July, 2013 to 31st March, 2014.  
 
I am sure this step will help Government in using the Aadhaar Payment Bridge more for the 
DBT.
