AddendumtoCircularNo.100-NACHDebit(156)
Refer to Circular No 100dated April 15,2015 on NACH Debit (156)is operational with effect
fromJuly13,2015forthevaluedateJuly142015.
In the first phase migration of RECS Mumbai (MICR starting 400) has been commenced, we shall
communicate the plan of migration for the remaining centers shortly. One of the key areas for
successful migration is having a consolidated data base of mapping of old account number to
new account numbers. Currently this data is with branches handling RECS/ECS transactions, as
NACH inward has to be handled at a central place member banks are advised to collect the data
from various centers and consolidate.
All the member banks are advised to treat the inward received through NACH debit platform at
par with the inward received through RECs/ECS with regard to processing transactions with old
account numbers.
