Revised new mandate format
With reference to our circular no: 130 dated September 11 on the new mandate
format and implementation date, we have received representations from various
stake holder seeking changes in the format to allow printing the clauses on the front
side of the mandate.
Considering the cost impact and changes in the format it has been decided to accede
to the request of allowing printing of the following clauses on the face of the
mandate.The sample mandate format is provided in AnnexureI.
Above signature
authorizing to debit my account as per latest schedule of charges of the bank.
Below signature
This is to confirm that the declaration has been carefully read, understood &
made by me/ us. I am authorizing the User entityl Corporate to debit my
account.
I have understood that I am authorized to cancel/ amend this mandate by
appropriately communicating the cancellation/ amendment request to the User
entity/ corporate or the bank whereI have authorized the debit.
The new mandate format implementation date is October 01, 2015.All the member
banks are advised to take note and implement.
