AllNACHmemberbanks
Re-presentation of returned transactions
It has been observed thatthememberbanks are representing the transactions in spiteof
the original transactions being returned withthe reasons like‘Account closed',‘Mandate
not received', etc.
All the sponsor banks and corporates should ensure that enough care is taken to remove the
transactions pertaining to returns with the reason Account closed', no transactions should
berepresentedinthiscase.Incaseof'Mandatenotreceived'casesthereprocessingshould
happenonlyaftergettingthemandates registered.
The Instructions contained herein may be disseminated to all the concerned. For any
clarifications please write back to ach@npci.org.in
