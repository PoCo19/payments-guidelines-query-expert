To,
AllNACHMemberBanks
New MapperFileFormat
Reference to our circular numbers 67 & 69 dated December 05,2014 and December 12,2014
respectively regarding the implementation of new mapper file format for seeding the Aadhaar
numbers
Now that sufficient time has been provided to the member banks for migration to the new format
we shall be discontinuing the old mapper file format with effect from November 15,2015.
All member banks are advised to make necessary changes and upload mapper files in the new
format only.From November 16, 2015 the files in old format will be rejected by the system.
In caseof anyfurther clarifications please writeto ach@npci.org.in ornachsupport@npci.org.in
