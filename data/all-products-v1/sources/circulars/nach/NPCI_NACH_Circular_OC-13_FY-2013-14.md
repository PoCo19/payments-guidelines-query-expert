Madam / Dear Sir,  
 
 
A kind reference is invited to NPCI circular ref. no. NPCI:2013-14:NACH:Circular 7 dated 31st 
May, 2013 on “Transactions Processed on National Automated Clearing House (NACH) 
System”. 
 
2. Vide this circular, all NACH participating banks were advised to ensure compliance and 
submit the compliance certificate on the following points in the given format latest by 07th 
June, 2013. 
a) Capturing transaction narration in customer account statement for APB / EBT 
transactions. 
b) Capturing correct return reason code, Beneficiary Account number, Beneficiary Name 
as updated in your core banking system and providing return/response file for all the 
inward files/transactions to the sponsor bank. 
c) Building adequate transaction processing capacity for APB / EBT transactions.

4. It may be highlighted that NPCI reserves the right to take appropriate measures in case of 
failure by any of the participating banks to comply with the provisions of the NACH Procedural 
Guidelines or Circulars issued from time to time in the interest of all the participants.
