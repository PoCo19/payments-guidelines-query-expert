Frequency Tag in MMs Inward Files
Refer to our Circular no: 65 dated December 01, 2014 clarifying MMs file format, It is
observed that a few banks are initiating MMs files without the frequencytag, this in turn is
causing issues at the destination banks leading to unnecessary delay in processing the
mandates.
It is further clarified that frequency tag is mandatory field and all the member banks should
strictly follow the MMs file format as specified by NPCi and provide the tag in the
presentation file.
We are in the process of incorporating controls to.check for the frequency tag in the file,
after implementation the system. witl reject the records without frequency tag. All the
member banks are advised to comply with.the specifications.
