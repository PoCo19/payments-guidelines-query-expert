# Circular no.010 - Increasing the mandate limit for TReDs

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2021-22/NACH/Circular

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2021-22/NACH/Circular No.010 February 11th,2022
To
All NACH Member Banks
Increasing the mandate limit forTReDS
February 10, 2022, wherein Reserve Bank of India (RBl) has announced increase in
NACH mandate limit to  3 crore for TReDS settlements.
In line with the directions of RBI the current mandate limit of Rs.1 Crore for TReDS
category is revised to  3 Crore. The limit for other categories remain unchanged. All the
member banks are advised to take note and carryout necessary modification for
implementing the changes. This will be with effect from February 15, 2022.
The information herein may be disseminated to all the concerned.
