# Circular No.143 - RTGS Holiday 2016

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2015-16/CircularNo.143

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2015-16/CircularNo.143
December 29,2015
To
AllNACHmemberbanks
Dear Sir/Madam,
NACH:RTGSHoliday-2016
Please find the RTGS holiday list for calendar year 2016 issued by Reserve Bank of India for
your reference in the Annexure-l.
NACH works on all days except for RTGS Holidays.
Ensure that sufficient staffs are available on all the NACH working days. In case of the
centre which manages NACH is on holiday, then request banks to have an alternate
arrangement in place for processing the files received if any from NACH.
Member banks are requested to take a note and ensure 100% compliance.
