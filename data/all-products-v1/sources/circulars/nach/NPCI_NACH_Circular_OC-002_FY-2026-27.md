Subject - Introduction of ACH Debit settlement on realization under NACH
Overview
NPCl offers ACH debit transactions under NACH as a payment Type
product for purpose of making electronic fund transfer. Currently, Circular
settlement is carried out separately i) at presentation stage, and i)
at return stage. In view of mitigating settlement risk, settlement only Product / Brand
upon realization is being introduced for ACH Debit transactions. NACH
This measure is being implemented to mitigate settlement risk and
improve liquidity management across the NACH ecosystem by Category
ensuring that financial settlement is executed inly for transactions ACH Debit Transactions
that are duly confirmed or deemed accepted within prescribed
timelines.

Operational Directions
1. Discontinuation of Settlement at Presentation Stage Effective Date
01 July 2026
Financial settlement at the presentation stage shall be discontinued.
Hence, at presentation, member banks must continue exchange
ACH Debit transaction data between member banks and shall not
trigger any financial settlement.
2. Settlement on Realization
Financial settlement shall be effected only after either:
a) Receipt of positive confirmation from the destination bank; or
b) Deemed acceptance of the transaction where no response is
received from the destination bank within prescribed timelines,
of the existing return session cycles.

3. Handling of Negatively Confirmed Transactions
For ACH Debit transactions that are negatively confirmed:
a) No financial settlement shall be executed; and
b) Details of such negative confirmations shall be provided to
presenting banks through the existing channel of response files.

4. Use of Presentation Session Settlement Reports
As presentation sessions shall no longer result in financial settlement, settlement reports
generated for such sessions shall be used only for reference and reconciliation purposes.
Members shall rely on file-level acknowledgements and revised reconciliation arrangements for
monitoring, validation, and control.
5. Reconciliation and Acknowledgement Controls
For reconciliation purposes:
a) Acknowledgements received for each presentation file shall continue to be treated as the
primary basis for validation.
b) In cases of record-level rejections within a file, member banks shall take appropriate
corrective action and re-present such records, wherever applicable.
Member banks are advised to further strengthen acknowledgement-based reconciliation
processes and related operational controls to align with the on-realization settlement framework.
There shall be no change to existing file formats or acknowledgement structures.
6. Risk Mitigation and Liquidity Management
The ACH Debit settlement on realization framework ensures that financial settlement is processed
only for transactions that are confirmed or deemed accepted. This is expected to significantly
reduce settlement risk, enhance reconciliation accuracy, and improve liquidity predictability and
management across the NACH ecosystem.
Action Required by Member Banks
Member banks shall review and suitably align their systems, internal processes, reconciliation
mechanisms, settlement posting logic, Mls, and liquidity management practices to ensure
complete readiness for implementation of the revised settlement mechanism on or before the
Effective Date.
