Transport layer security (TLS) update
In order to secure all communications between NACH server and bank servers, it has been
implementationare provided in Annexurel.
Member banks are advised to take note and make necessary changes in their internal system
with immediate effect. The access restriction will be implemented from August 31, 2018
and with effect from that date NACH access will be available only to banks that have
implemented TLS1.2 protocol.
If any further clarifications are required, the same may be raise the query through CRM tool
provided in NACH system. (Path: NACH portal → ACH →> Utilities →> CRM Tracker).
