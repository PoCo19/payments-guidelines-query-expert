To,
AllNACHMemberBanks
MigrationofNACHdebit(156)formattoACHdebit(306)Legacyformat-PhaseV
Reference may be taken from Circular No.031 on“Migration of NACH debit (156)format to
ACH debit (306) Legacy format dated November 09, 2018 wherein we had migrated 631
banks.After furtheranalysis of thedata we are migratinganother setof 35banks toACHDebit
306 from November25, 2018.List of 35banks isannexed inAnnexure-Iand the total list of
666banksisannexedinAnnexure-ll.
Sponsor banks are advised not to raise the transactions in NACH Debit (156) format on these
destination banks and intimate their respective corporates. Going forward all the transactions
need to be raised in ACH debit Legacy (306) format for these banks. Accordingly, we will
disable these destination banks from NACHDebit (156)formatfromNovember25,2018.
Memberbanks are advised totake note and make necessary arrangements accordingly.
WithWarmRegards
Giridhar.G.M
