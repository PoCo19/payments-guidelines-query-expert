All member banks participating in NACH
Making user bank account number mandatory in ACH for Salary files
Refer to circular no. 188 dated September 23, 2016 on “NACH Salary credit identifier", the
user bank account number field (header record) in the ACH credit input file for salary
("SAL") product will be made mandatory effective from July 02, 2018. If not provided, NACH
system will reject the entire file. The details of the field is given below for your reference:
Product : ACH Credit
Product Identifier : SAL
Field : User bank account number
Field length : 204 to 239
In addition, it is mandatory for banks to provide the PAN & TAN of the corporates for whom
the salary credits are processed through NACH. The details have to be submitted as per the
format in Annexure I on or before July 13, 2018. In-case of new corporate the same can be
submitted as part of the corporate creation form.
Member banks may note that this is a requirement of Reserve Bank of India for
assessment of employment index therefore all the sponsor banks should implement this
on priority.
Please write to ach@npci.org.in for any clarifications.
