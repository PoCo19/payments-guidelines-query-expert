All member banks are advised to expedite the implementation of APl based E-Mandate
process through internet banking as well as debit cards based authentication of the same. As
acceptance of mandates by various participating banks will be essential for successful
implementation, it is decided that a bank will be on boarded on to APl based mandate (both
variants) as sponsor bank only if that bank offers the service as a destination for both the
variants, i.e. if a bank implements either internet banking based variant or debit card based
variant only as a destination bank then such bank will be on boarded as sponsor bank only for
that variant.
As on date 8 banks and 15 corporates have completecl or undergoing certification, the list of
banks is published on NACH landing page as well as NPCl website.
