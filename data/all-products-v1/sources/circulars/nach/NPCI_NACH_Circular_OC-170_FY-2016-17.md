Miscellaneous others return reason (code -04)
It has been observed that banks are using miscellaneous others (reason code:04 ) return
reason leading to lot of inconvenience to the sponsor banks and the government
departments in case of direct benefit transfer transactions.It has been decided to do
awaywiththisreasonwitheffectfromSeptember01,2016forAadhaarbasedtransactions
(APBS). Member banks are advised to review their return mapping and come up with
additional reasons to be included even for other products of NACH as well. The list may
be provided separately for the following products.
1. APB
2. ACH credit
3. ACH debit
Note that the reason will be removed only for APB transactions however for other products
as of now this will be continued. Member banks are advised to take immediate measures
to review the mapping and take corrective action and also provide the list of additional
reasons before July 31, 2016.
With effect from September 01, 2016 APB returns with the reason code: 04 (miscellaneous
others) will be rejected at NACH.
