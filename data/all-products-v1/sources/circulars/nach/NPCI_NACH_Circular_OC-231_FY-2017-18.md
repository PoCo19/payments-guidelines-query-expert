eMandates- Amount cap& charges
In the 26th NACH steering committee meeting held on May 23, 2017, the following
proposalspertainingtoeMandateswereapproved
1. Maximum amount:
The cap on the eMandate amount is Rs.1 lakh per mandate.This is to be
reviewed after 1 year.
2. Incentive:
Processedbetween0to2workingdays-IncentiveofRs.5.0o/-permandate.
No incentive beyond 2 working days.
3.Penalty:
penalty and the periodicity will be decided at a later stage.
4. NPCI charges:
Refer to our circular no. 153 on the “Waiver of mandate processing charges"
for mandates, charges are waived till December 31, 2017. Charges from
January01,2018will beasperthedirectionsof theboardofNPCl.Thisis
applicabletoeMandates as well.
Thisiswithimmediateeffect.Memberbanksmaypleasenoteanddisseminatethe
information to all the concerned. For any clarifications please write back to
ach@npci.org.in
