Issuing returnmemoforrejected/returnedmandates
The mandates initiated, might get rejected by NPCl at the time of upload or returned by
the destination bank for various reasons as defined by NPCl in the MMs system.The list of
reject reasons for mandates is provided in Annexure I and the list of return reasons is
provided in Annexure ll.
As the reasons for rejection/returns are not clearly communicated to the corporate who in
turn is expected to intimate the customer, the end customer is not in a position to rectify
and re-submit the mandates.
return memo. The return memo should be pre-printed with all the return and reject
reasons listed, the relevant reject/return reason should be highlighted based on the
reject/return flag received in the NACK file from the MMS system.
Member banks are advised to take note and ensure adherence.
