To,
All NACH member banks
Dear Sir/Madam,
RTGS Holiday - June 16, 2018
RBl has declared June 16, 2018 as RTGS holiday on account of Ramzan ID(ld-Ul-Fitr).As per the
procedural guidelines, NACH will also remain closed on RTGs holiday therefore there will not
be any NACH sessions on June 16,2018.
Revised holiday list issued by RBl is enclosed. All Member Bank are advised to take a note of
the same and plan accordingly.
For any clarifications please write back to ach@npci.org.in;
