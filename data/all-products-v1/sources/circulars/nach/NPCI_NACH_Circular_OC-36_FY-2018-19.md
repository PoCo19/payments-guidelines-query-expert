MigrationofNACHdebit(156)formattoACHdebit(306)Legacyformat
Reference may be taken from Circular No. 33 on "Migration of NACH debit (156) format to
ACH debit (306) Legacy format dated November 20, 2018 wherein we had migrated 666
banks. After further analysing the data we are migrating another set of 27 banks to ACH Debit
306fromDecember3,2018.Listof27banks isannexed inAnnexure-Iandthetotal listof
693banksisannexed inAnnexure-ll.
Sponsor banks are advised not to raise the transactions in NACH Debit (156) format on these
destination banks and intimate their respective corporates. Going forward all the transactions
need to be raised in ACH debit Legacy (306) format for these banks. Accordingly, we will
disable these destination banks from NACH Debit (156) format from December 3, 2018.
Member banks are advised to take note and make necessary arrangements accordingly.
