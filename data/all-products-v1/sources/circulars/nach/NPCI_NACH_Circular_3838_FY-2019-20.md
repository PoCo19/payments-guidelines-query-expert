Additional toproducttypeLON'inNACH
This is in continuation to our Circular No.016 on Introduction of new product type 'LON' in
order to facilitate processing of credits to loan accounts under various otherDBT schemes,
LON category as a product identifier is extended to all the DBT schemes. There is no change
intheexistingfieldlength(262-264)andfileformats.
Sponsor banks may please take note of the change and accordingly initiate the transactions
with producttype‘LON'.Destination banks are advised to implement necessarychanges in
their CBS and other relevant systems to process transactions received under'LON'category
tothe creditof loanaccounts.
