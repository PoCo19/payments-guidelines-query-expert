DBTL Commission-Payout
This is to inform that, the first lot of the DBTL commission payments for the period
16.01.2015 to 31.03.2015 has been released by the Union Government of India.State Bank of
India being the sponsor bank with the help of NACH system will credit the same to the
respective destination banks. The date of DBTL commission payout will be communicated
later.
Thedetailed process of the DBTLcommission payout is givenbelow.
1. The DBTL commission will be processed through ACH Credit special session (Session 3)
in NACH.
2. The banks should inform their treasury about the credits being passed in their account
with RBl through NACH settlement so that the necessary process for booking income
will be carried out.
3.As is in the case of settlement, the sub-member banks credits also will be given to the
settlement account of the sponsor banks.
4. The credit entries related to sub-member banks will be posted individually in the
account of sponsor banks i.e. the number of credit entries equal to the number of sub-
member banks as on March 31,2015.
5. The sub member banks details will be made available in the ACH Credit inward file
received by their sponsor banks (inward file format indicating the fields providing is
details is provided inAnnexurel)
6. The sponsor banks have to pass on the credit to their respective sub-member banks.
7. For this purpose a new sub-product is created under ACH credit product with product
code"COM"
8. Special ACH credit session will be opened on the day of the payout.
Calculation of remuneration per destination bank is done by State Bank India who is the
sponsor bank for the Oil Marketing Companies. The transaction counts were confirmed by
NPCI.
