NACHCreditadditional session
NPCIhaslaunched NACHcredit product onJuly07,2013 and asondate175banks are
participating on daily basis.It has been decided to launch a new variant of NACH Credit
transactions (156 characters)with effect from April 02,2014.This will be in additionto the
existing NACH credit product for which the sessions are alreadybeing run on daily basis.
2.This new variant of NACH Credit product will have below transaction codes which are
different fromtheexistingtype.
a. Header level code is“13"
b.Transactionlevel codeis“24"
(fileformat with changeshighlighted providedin AnnexureI)
3.This new NACH Credit session timings are asbelow:
Session type Session open Session close
Presentation 9:00 AM 10:00AM
Returns 11:00AM 4:00PM
4.Memberbanks are requested to note that as a result of the additional session there will be
separate credit and inward files for each of the sessions.Please keep your treasury
department informed, arrange to process the inward to credit the customers and submit the
returns as per the timeline stipulated above.
5.All the member banks are requested to ensure the following,
Makenecessary modifications in their systems to process the transaction files with
thenewheaderand transaction level codesas specified above.
Toprocess the transactions as perthe value date and credit the customer.
All the transactions received should be reconciled against the settlement received
fortheday sothat therewill notbeanyunprocessedtransactions.
Strictly adhere to the process of submission of returns file with in the stipulated
timelines.
