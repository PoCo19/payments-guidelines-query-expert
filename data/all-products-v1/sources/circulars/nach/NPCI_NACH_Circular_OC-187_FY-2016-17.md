PurposeTaginMMS
Refer to Circular No: 58 dated November 05, 2014 regarding NACH Debit - Mandate
Processing Guidelines, a few of the member banks are not processing the mandates drawn
on CC accounts as the utilization of funds is not known at the time of presentation of
mandates.
2. In the view of the above, we advise member banks to use the reference 2 field
(Debtor/Scheme Plan Reference Number) for providing the purpose of the mandate drawn
on CC accounts. Note that there is no change in existing the MMs file format for Sponsor
andDestination banks.Referto AnnexureIfor details.
3. Destination banks may use the purpose provided in the MMs file while processing the
mandates drawn on CC accounts. This will be an optional field.
4. Banks and corporates are advised to provide the purpose in the reference 2 field
the time or presenting the mandates.
The processing units may be advised accordingly.
