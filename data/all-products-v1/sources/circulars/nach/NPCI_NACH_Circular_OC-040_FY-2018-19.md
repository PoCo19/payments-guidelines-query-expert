MigrationofNACHdebit(156)formattoACHdebit(306)Legacyformat
Reference may be taken from Circular No. 39 on“Migration of NACH debit (156) format to
ACH debit (306) Legacy format datedDecember 6, 2018 wherein we had migrated 700 banks.
After further analysing the data we are migrating another set of 8 banks to ACH Debit 306
fromDecember26,2018.Listof8banksisannexed inAnnexure-Iandthetotal listof708
banks is annexed in Annexure -ll.
Sponsorbanksareadvised nottoraisethetransactionsinNACHDebit (156)formatonthese
destinationbanks and intimatetheirrespectivecorporates.Goingforward all thetransactions
need to be raised in ACH debit Legacy (306) format for these banks. Accordingly, we will
disablethesedestinationbanksfromNACHDebit(156)formatfromDecember26,2018.
Memberbanks are advisedtotakenoteandmakenecessaryarrangements accordingly.
