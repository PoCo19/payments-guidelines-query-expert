Participation in NACH ECS Credit (CECS) System 
 
 
NPCI is thankful to the member banks for extending their support towards participation in 
NACH System. 
 
2. All the existing member banks of NECS (operated by RBI at Mumbai) are now part of 
NACH ECS Credit (CECS) system except GPO. Banks are sending & receiving transactions on 
NACH ECS Credit (CECS) system on a daily basis.  
 
3. We seek support of participating member banks to increase transaction volumes on 
NACH ECS Credit (CECS) system. 
 
4. NPCI is working to ensure that member banks in other RECS centres also join NACH ECS 
Credit (CECS) system in due course of time. 
 
5. The list of member banks participating in NACH ECS Credit (CECS) along with their IFS 
Code and MICR Code is given in Annexure - I. 
 
6. We look forward for your continuous support to make NACH system a great success. For 
any queries/further help, please email us at ach@npci.org.in 
 
 
With Warm Regards, 
 
Vipin Surelia 
SVP & Head – NACH & Cheque Clearing
