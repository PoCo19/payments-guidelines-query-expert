NACH.CorporateSalary/pensionproduct
We are pleased to inform that we are introducing two new products called "CORP SAL"
and “"pENSION" under ACH Credit exclusively for corporate salary and pension credits. This
new product enables the destination banks to identify the salaryl pension credits received
and will help them to prioritize the same for crediting their corporate salary account-
holders.
Benefits:
Sponsor banks
1. Debit through CC or CA resulting in better customer service
Elimination of PO, DD, NEFT file etc leading to less effort
3. Straight through process
4. Better turnaround time
Dedicated support from NPCi
6. Warehousing of files up to 7 days
Destination banks
1. Selling point for better service for corporate salary account
2. Increase in CASA
3. Dedicated support from NPC!
Roles and responsibilities:
Sponsor banks:
1. Banks to upload the salary files with “SAL"and pension file with “PEN” product
identifier.
6:30 PM.
Destination banks:
1.Prioritise the“"SAL" &“PEN"product files received for processing.
2. SMS alert to be sent once the credit effected to the account.
3. Ensuring zero deemed acceptance as there is no extension allowed.
