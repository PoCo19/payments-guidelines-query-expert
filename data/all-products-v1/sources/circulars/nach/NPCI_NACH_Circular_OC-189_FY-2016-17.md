NPCI
NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATION OF INDIA
NPC1/2016-17/NACH/Circular No. 189 September 26, 2016
To
All NACH member banks
Processing of ACH Credit files (ACHCR present3) on T+O settlement
Refer to the circular no 119 dated August 25, 2016 on migration of ECS 156 to ACH 306
format. Now that the process has been streamlined it has been decided that the
transaction processing will be carried out as per the regular ACH credit transaction
processing cycle i.e. on the same day.
In order to reduce the returns with the reason ‘No such account' NPCi has facilitated the
process of old account conversion (OAC): Out of 78.79 lakh records submitted by the
corporates and the member banks 76.85 lakh transactions are already cleared by the
respective bank. The sponsor bank and their corporates are advised to take immediate
measures to verify and update the records.
The current process of providing the inward files on T-1 basis before the settlement is
posted will be discontinued w.e.f January 01, 2017. The inward files will be delivered to
the bank in the morning and returns are expected to be uploaded by evening as per the
session timings communicated by NPCl.
All the member banks are advised to take immediate measures to ensure the change over
to the new process seamlessly.
