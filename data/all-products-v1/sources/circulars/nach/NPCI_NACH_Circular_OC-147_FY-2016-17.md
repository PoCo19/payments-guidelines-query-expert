# Circular No.147 - Mandate Management System

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2015-16/NACH/CircularNo.147

<!-- Page 1 -->

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2015-16/NACH/CircularNo.147 February01,2016
To
Member banks participating in NACH
MandateManagement System
Madam / Dear Sir,
The new / revised NACH mandate form was released vide circular No.131 dated September 19,
2015 which were to be used with effect from October 01, 2015 for all new mandates.However,
it has been brought to our notice that member banks are advising their customers to submit
the new NACH mandate format even for existing debit instructions. Similarly, it has also been
indicated that there is lack of clarity in the instructions given by member banks to their
customers as well as user institutions regarding the manner and content in which the mandate
form has to be filled up. The branch staff are also not able to guide the customers as they are
themselves not well-equipped with the details to be filled in the form and its applicability.
This is causing a lot of confusion and anxiety among general public leading to increase in
customer complaints/queries. All the member banks are, therefore, advised to take the
following steps on priority basis:
a. The front desk and field staff should be well trained on all the aspects of NACH
debit format and its applicability so that they can handle the customer queries
and guide them appropriately.
b. Educate customers/corporates about the ACH debit mandate format with clear
instructions on the values/data to be filled in the mandates before it is
executed bythecustomer.
C. In all customer communications, on new mandate format, it should be explicitly
made clear that the new format is applicable only for new debit instructions,
and that new mandate forms need not be submitted for already existing debit
instruction/s.
d. To clearly indicate that the mandate/s in old format that is/are already
submitted by the customer for existing Ecs debit instructions continue to be
validevenaftermigrationtoNACH system.
All member banks are advised to accord top priority to this matter and act accordingly.
