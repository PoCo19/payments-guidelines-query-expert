Embossingsignaturesonmardateimage
It has been noticed that fewpresenting banks/corporates are indulging in the following practices
1.Embossing the signature of the customer (cropped from some other document) on the
image of the physical mandate.
2. Getting the customer sign on the electronic gadge:t (on the surface of the tablet, IPAD)
that is usedforinputting themandate details.
Such practices are not in compliance with the procedural guidelines. The banks and corporates
areadvisedtodesistfromsuchpracticesimmediately
NPCl will analysethemandates submitted by various corporatesandbanks onan ongoingbasis,
if anybank/corporate isfoundtobeindulging insuchpracrices,wemaytakepunitive actionthat
may include imposition of penalty and/or suspending the corporate from participating in Mandate
ManagementSystem.
Sponsor banks are advised to communicate to the corporates participating through them
accordingly.
