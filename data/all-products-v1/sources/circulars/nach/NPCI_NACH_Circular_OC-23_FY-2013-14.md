Deactivation of Moved out AadhaarNumbers in Bank's Core Banking System
Madam/DearSir,
As part of the APB System,all participating banks should seed the Aadhaar numbers
received from the customers,first in the core banking system of the bank and
subsequentlyin the NPCI mapperona dailybasis.
2. APB System facilitates the participating banks to download the“Moved Out”report
from SFG whichprovides the list of Aadhaarnumbers (earlier seeded against the bank's lIN
in NPCI mapper)thathavebeenmoved from thebank's mapping inNPCImapper toother
APBparticipatingbank.
3.Further to the above moved out report, on weekly basis every Monday, NPCl provides
download of list of Aadhaar numbers that are active in NPCl mapper and mapped to a
specific bank.
4. In order to ensure consistency of the Aadhaar numbers seeded in the bank's core
banking system and Aadhaar numbers seeded in the NPCl mapper, all APB participating
banks are advised to ensure that they deactivate all such Aadhaar numbers from their core
banking system that are highlighted in the“"Moved Out" report.On weekly basis banks
should reconcile the Aadhaar numbers mapped in their CBS with the list of active Aadhaar
numbersprovidedbyNPCl.
5.This will ensure consistency between the Aadhaar numbers seeded by the bank in the
corebanking system and Aadhaar numbers seeded by the bank in theNpCl mapper thereby
helpto eliminate returns on account of Aadhaar number notbeing mapped.
6. All APB participating banks are advised to make a note of the same and ensure
compliance on daily basis.
