Declaration on bank details for remittance of DBT - charges and incentives by NPCl
using e-payment module of PFMS platform
The Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, and Government of India vide its Office
Memorandum F.No. 32(07)/PF-I1/2011(Vol.1l) dated February 26, 2016 has issued direction
that all DBT transactions are to be routed through NPCl. The settlement of charges and
incentives for applicable schemes will be done by the Implementing agencies based on the
vouchers submitted by NPCI for onward distribution to the different entities
PFMs platform while remitting the charges and incentives to different entities after the
same is settled by the implementing agencies.
Implementation of the above directive involves creation of entities in the PFMs system
along-with details of account where the credit will be afforded.
Member banks are advised to share the required details for creation of entities in the form
of a declaration with all the details filled (Annexure I) duly signed by authorised signatory
of the rank of DGM/GM before June 15, 2018.
Note that DBT incentive and charges will not be paid to the banks that does not comply
with the requirement before June 15, 2018. Please ensure submission of details before
that.
For any further clarification please write to ach@npci.org.in
