Waiverof Mandate Processing Charges onNACHDebit.
Madam/DearSir,
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCl)implemented and operationalised
NACH (National Automated Clearing House).NACHDebitis oneof the products in
the platform.Transactions which are processed through NACH Debit should have
the valid customer mandates and the same is processed through the module
calledMandateManagement System(MMS)intheNACHplatform.
2.NpCl also taken various initiatives to encourage the banks to participate on the
NACHDebit platform,including the launch of Mandate Validation Service on
October7,2013.
3.In orderto encourage wider participation on NACH Debit,NPCI has decided to
offer the mandate processing service at no charge to the banks from O1st January
2014to31stDecember2015.
4.All NACH Debit participating banks are requested to make a note of the same.
