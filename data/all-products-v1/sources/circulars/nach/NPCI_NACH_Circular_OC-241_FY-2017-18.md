NACHDebit(156format)toACHDebit(306format)migration-Final Cutover
We draw your reference to the circular # 232 dated June 19, 2017 on the migration road
map from NACHDebit (156format) toACHDebit (306 format), As per the circular the final
cut over was proposed on July 25, 2017. The target date was already extended multiple
times however at the request of a few of the member banks it has been decided to extend
thetimelineone last time.Therevised cut-over will be fromOctober12,2017.Wehave
given clear 2 months from the last deadline. It is expected that the banks should complete
their internal processes and be ready for processing the transactions without any issues.
Member banks are advised to note that requests for further extensions, beyond stipulated
date of October 12, 2017 will not be entertained. With effect from October 13, 2017 the
transactions will beprocessed in the new format only.All member banks are advised to take
a note of this final cut-over date and pass on this communication to all the respective
departments including the senior officials in the bank about this development.
