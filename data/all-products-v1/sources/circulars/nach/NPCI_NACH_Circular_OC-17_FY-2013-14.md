Making Beneficiary Account Holder's Name and Destination Bank Account Number
MandatoryinAPBReturnFile
Madam/ Dear Sir,
In the APB system used for direct benefit transfer of Government benefits and subsidies, the
return file sent by the destination bank has Beneficiary Account Holder's Name (40 digit alpha
numeric) and Destination Bank Account Number (20 digit alpha numeric) as optional fields.
Currently, 70% of the destination banks are providing the return file with Beneficiary Account
Holder's Nameand Destination Bank Account Number.
2. The Ministries, Government Departments and Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) wanted all
the banks to mandatorily provide these two 2 parameters in the APB return file.
3. Keeping in view the above, it has been decided to make Beneficiary Account Holder's Name
and Destination Bank Account Number fields mandatory in the APB return file sent by the
destination bank w.e.f. 11th November, 2013.
4. APB destination banks are advised to make necessary arrangements at their end to capture
both the Beneficiary Account Holder's Name and Destination Bank Account Number in APB
return file.
5.All APB participating banks to ensure compliance by the given timeline.
