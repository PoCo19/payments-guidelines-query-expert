DearSir/ Madam,
NACH:RTGSHoliday-2017
Please find the RTGS holiday list for calendar year2017 issued by Reserve Bank of India for
your reference in the Annexure - I.
NACH works onall days except forRTGS holidays.
Ensure that sufficient staffs are available on all the NACH working days. In case of the
centre which manages the NACH is on holiday, then request bank to have an alternate
arrangement in place for processing the files received if any from NACH.
