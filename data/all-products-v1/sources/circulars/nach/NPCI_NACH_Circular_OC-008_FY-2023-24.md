Mandate duration and mandatory final collection date
The mandate management system facilitates the acceptance, digitization, processing, and transmission of
mandates across banks, allowing customer requests for debits based on amounts and periodicity through
NACH. In order bring in more robustness in the process it has been decided to implement the following
measures
1. Maximum period for which a mandate can be issued: Mandate can be issued for a maximum
duration of 30 years from the date of issuance. Validation will be built in the central system to accept
only the mandates that are for a duration of 30 years or below.
2. Final collection date (end date) of mandate to be mandatory: It has been decided to remove the
option of "until cancelled" for all categories of mandates, it will be mandatory to provide the Final
coilection date (end date) for all the categories of mandates. Refer to point 1 above the end date
cannot be more than 30 years from the date of the mandate.
Apart from the above it has been decided to discontinue Aoo1 (APl Mandate) category code. This is a generic
code and hence not serving any purpose. All the stakeholders should present the mandate with relevant
category codes only.
This will be effective October 01, 2023
This is applicable for all forms (physical as wel as electronic) of mandates. The information herein may be
disseminated to all the concerned.
