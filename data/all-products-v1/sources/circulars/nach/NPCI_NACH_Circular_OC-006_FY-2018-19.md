Legacymandateamendment
Refer to circular no.267dated January 03,2018 on“NACHDebit (156)to ACH Debit(306
format) migration - Commencement of Sessions". To address the issue of old account (pre-CBS)
account number issue, we will be providing an option for member banks to amend legacy
mandates.This facilitywill be provided only to the destination banks.
The amendment to legacy mandates will be auto accepted, so member banks who are executing
the legacy amendment must ensure correctness of the data before executing amendment. The
sponsor bank will receive the records of amended mandate, they should in turn provide the
records to corporate for updation at their end. All the stakeholder may note that the
transactions should bepresented with the new account number as per the amended record, if
old account numbers are used then the transaction will be rejected by NACH due to validation
failure.
Thelegacyamendment request hastobesubmittedbythememberbanks intheformatdefined
for this purpose (Refer Annexure I). Once the request is received, NPCl will intimate the bank
on the period during whichtheamendment canbe executedbythebank.All the amendments
should be completed withinthe stipulated timeline.
ThisfunctionalitywillbemadeavailableeffectivefromJune01,2018.
