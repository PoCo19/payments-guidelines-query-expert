AllNACHMemberbanks
Migrationto306fileformatfrom156format
NPCI is in the process of migrating ECS debit to NACH platform.As per the RBI mandate, in
the second phase, all the transactions will be migrated to 306 file (ACH credit) format. One
of the critical factors that will have a bearing on this migration is physical mandates that are
in the possession of destination banks.
The matter was deliberated in 19th NACH steering committee meeting held on July 23, 2015.
The committee has recommended that confirmation should be sought from the member banks
on availability of physical mandates with them.
Member banks are advised to check at their and send their confirmations on availability of
physical mandates, for the all the transactions received through Ecs debit platform, to
ach@npci.org.inonorbeforeAugust25,2015.
