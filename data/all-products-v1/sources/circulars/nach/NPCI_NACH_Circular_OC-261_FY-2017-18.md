# Circular No.261 - Mandate processing charges of NACH

Circular/reference number: NPCI/2017-18/NACH/Circular

<!-- Page 1 -->

NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATION OF INDIA
NPCI/2017-18/NACH/Circular No. 261 November 28, 2017.
To
All NACH member banks
Mandates processing charges of NACH
Refer to our circular no. 153 dated March 29, 2016 on “Waiver of mandate processing
charges" of NPCI till December 31, 2017. Please note all the ACH mandates (based on the
scanned copy of the physical mandates) presented with effect from January 01, 2018 will
attract charges as per the table given below.
Type of Service NPCI Charges Remarks
Mandate Initiation /  1.00 on initiating bank
 1.50 per mandate
modification / cancelation  0.50 on receiving bank
NPCi has introduced E-Mandate to bring in more efficiency and accuracy in mandate
processing. As per the procedural guidelines the following charges are applicable for E-
Mandate.
Type of Service NPCI Charges Remarks
Mandate Initiation /  0.50 on initiating bank
modification / cancelation  1.00 per mandate  0.50 on receiving bank
To encourage the usage of E-Mandates the charges (detailed above) for E-Mandate are
waived till December 31, 2018.
Member banks are advised to take note and disseminate the information to all the
concerned.
For clarifications please write to ach@npci.org.in
