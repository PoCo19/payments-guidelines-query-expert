Introductionofnewproducttype‘LON'inNACH
The Department of Higher Education under the Ministry of Human Resource Development,
Government of India is implementing the Central Sector Interest Subsidy scheme for
educationloanstotheeligiblestudentsunderDBT.Aspertheschemethesubsidyamount
releasedbythedepartment shouldbecrediteddirectlytotheloanaccountof thestudents
(beneficiaries) using NACH (based on IFSC and Loan Account No).
To facilitate this scheme we are introducing a new product type “Credit to loan account"
intheACHcreditinputfileforLoan("LON")asanidentifierforcreditingtheamounttothe
Loan account of the beneficiary.The details of the field are provided below:
Product : ACH Credit
ProductIdentifier : LON
Field length : 262 to 264
The inward filesgenerated will be intheformat asgiven below.
ACH-CR-ABCD-DDMMYYYY-LONO00001-INW.tXt
Memberbanks are advised to take note of the change and put in place necessary system
arrangements to process the transactions to the credit of loan accounts.The scheme will
be commenced from August 01, 2018.
In case of any further clarifications, the same may be raised through CRM tool
provided in NACH system. (Path:NACH portal→ACH→ Utilities→>CRMTracker)
