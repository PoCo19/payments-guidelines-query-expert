Reference may be taken from Circular No.040 on"Migration of NACH debit (156) format to
ACH debit (306) Legacy format dated December 21, 2018 wherein we had migrated 708
banks. After further analysing the data we are migrating ancther set of 6 banks to ACH Debit
306fromJanuary02,2019.Listof6banksis annexed inAnnexure-land thetotal listof714
banks is annexed in Annexure-ll.
Sponsor banks are advised not to raise the transactions in NACH Debit (156) format on these
destination banks and intimate their respective corporates. Going forward all the transactions
need to be raised in ACH debit Legacy (306) format for these banks. Accordingly, we will
disable these destination banks from NACH Debit (156) forimat from January 02, 2019.
Memberbanks areadvisedtotakenoteandmakenecessaryarrangementsaccordingly.
