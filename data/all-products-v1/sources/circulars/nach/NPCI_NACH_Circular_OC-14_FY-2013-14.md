Change in SettlementMechanismof NACHECS Credit(CECS)System
NPCl is thankful to all the member banks for extending their support towards participation in
NACHECSCredit(CECS)system.
2.Currently,the settlement of NACH ECS Credit (CECS)takes place inthe settlement account
of the participating banks maintained with Deposit Accounts Department (DAD)of RBI and
involves submission of settlement file through RTGS gateway of NPCl. This settlement
mechanism makes IFS Codemandatoryfor theNACHECSCredit (CECS)participating banks.
3. Banks that do not have their own IFs Code were required to participate as indirect / sub-
memberbanks onNACHECSCredit(CECS)system eventhough theyhavetheir ownMICRCode
& current account withDAD.Banks willingtoparticipateinNACHECS Credit(CECS)systemas
directmemberbanks were not able to do so inview of thisrequirement.
4.Keeping the above in view,NPCI has decided to change the settlement mechanism of NACH
ECS Credit (CECS)from IFS Codebased settlement to MiCR Code based settlement.This
mechanism will allow settlement to take place in the current account of the member banks
maintainedwithDADand will also enable direct participation of thosememberbanks on NACH
ECS Credit(CECS)system that donot havetheirown IFS CodebuthaveMiCRCode&current
account.
5.Wewish to inform that w.e.f.2oth August,2013, the settlement mechanism forNACHECS
Credit(CECS)will bebased onMICRCodes of theparticipatingbanksand willtakeplaceinthe
current account of the member banks maintained with DAD.
6.With theimplementation of new settlement mechanism,sponsor banks will beable to send
transactions drawnon133 memberbanks (listenclosed)on NACHECSCredit(CECS)system.
These include117member banksof existing NECS (operated by RBl).
7.We look forward for your continuous support to make NACH ECS Credit (CECS)system a
great success.Foranyqueries/furtherhelp,pleaseemailusatach@npci.org.in
With.WarmRegards,
GiridharGM
