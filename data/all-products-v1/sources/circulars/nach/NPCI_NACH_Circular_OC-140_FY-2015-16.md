AdditionalAPBPresentationfornon DBTLtransaction
Union Government of India has directed all major departments to push the Direct Benefit
Transfers (DBT) via NACH and it is expected that the volume of DBT transactions will be
multifold.To have a largertime windowfor processing these DBTtransactions,it hasbeen
decided to open additional APB and ACH Credit (306 file format) Presentation sessions with
effect from December 28, 2015.
Sponsor Banks may note that the input files (presentation files) of APB (non DBTL) & ACH Credit
(306) presentation files can be uploaded in the following sessions. The consolidated Session
timelines for APB product is given below for the reference of all member banks.
