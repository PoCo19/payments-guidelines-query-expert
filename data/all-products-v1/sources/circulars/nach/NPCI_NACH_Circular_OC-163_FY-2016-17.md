# Circular No.163 Presentation of ECS mandates

NPCI
NATIONALPAYMENTSCORPORATIONOFINDIA
NPCI/2015-16/NACH/CircularNo.163 June07,2016
To
Memberbanksparticipating inNACH
Presentationof ECSmandates
We refer to our circular no. 157 dated May 02, 2016 on extension for receiving the
ECS mandates forms. The sponsor banks may continue to use EcS mandates only in
case of banks that are not clearing the mandates within the stipulated TAT. All
efforts should be made to present only ACH mandate forms on the banks that are
adhering to the timelines.
The member banks are advised to review the volume received and the staff
compliment and ensure that the department is sufficiently staffed to process the
mandates within the time line. The 22nd steering committee of NACH has
deliberated the proposal of levying penalty on the mandates not processed within
TAT. It was decided to convene a special Steering Committee meeting during the
month of July 2016 to finalize the modalities and the amount of penalty to be levied.
All the member banks are advised to take note and do the needful.
