Advisory -Processing of Credit to Beneficiary Account
1. Background:
NPCl offers NACH as a payments product for purpose of making electronic funds transfer. In order
transactions to accounts held with beneficiary bank based on the following logic:
1. Transactions are routed to the beneficiary bank once the 11-character IFSC Code is available and
matches the NPCI IFSC Code Master (Master).
2. Where the full 11-character IFsC code is not present in the Master, transactions are routed to the
sponsor bank corresponding to first four characters.
Purpose:
NPCl has observed instances where sub-member banks have not updated or maintained their
IFSC Codes in the NACH system. In such cases, transactions are routed to sponsor banks based
solely on the first four characters of the IFSC code which may lead to i) inadvertent credits to
unintended accounts, ii) customer disputes and i) operational, financial and reputational risk of the
ecosystem. Hence, this advisory is being issued to avert the aforementioned risks.
I. Operative advisory:
Member banks are advised to take the folfowing actions:
1. All banks shall ensure that newly allotted IFsC codes are promptly updated in the NACH system.
2. Banks must periodically verify the accuracy and completeness of their IFSC details in the NACH
system and notify NPCl of changes, if any. NPCI has previously shared through designated
email addresses of member banks a master list of iFsC codes mapped to each member bank.
Member banks shall carefuily verify this list and ensure there are no discrepancies. Any
discrepancy identified shall be immediately reported to NACH operations through CRM system
for necessary correction and confirmation.
3. All member banks shall implement appropriate system controls to validate the IFSC code
received in inward transactions while processing inward credit transactions. Credits to
beneficiary accounts shall be processed only if the IFsC code belongs to the beneficiary's bank.
However, if the IFsC code pertains to another bank (including sub-member banks), such
transactions shall be returned with Reason Code "72"- IFsc Code not updated by Sub-
member Bank.
The contents of this advisory may be disseminated to all concerned officials and departments for
necessary action.
