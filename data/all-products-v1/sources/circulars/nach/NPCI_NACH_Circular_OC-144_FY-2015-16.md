EcsDebitPlanof migration-Additional Centers
This has reference to the circular no. 111 dated July 21, 2015 and circular no. 137 dated
November 23, 2015 on migration of ECS debit transactions on to the NACH platform. As part of
the initial phase the following centers are made live in NACH platform.
1. Mumbai
2. E Bangalore
3.  Trivandrum
4. Chennai
5. Kolkata
6.   Hyderabad
7. Jaipur
8. Bhubaneshwar
9. Guwahati
10. Pune
11. Chandigarh
12.Ahmedabad
13. Delhi
Banks have commenced processing the ECS Debit (156) volumes in NACH platform under NACH
Debit (156) product and it is expected that the volume migration of the above centers will be
completed by January 2016. It is now decided to commence migration of the remaining Ecs
centers across the country on to NACH platform with effect from January 05, 2016. The list of
those centers is enclosed in the Annexure l.
Now that migration has reached the critical phase, it is expected that more volume will be
processed through NACH platform under NACH Debit (156) product. Member banks are advised
to take a note of the same and make necessary arrangements to handle the high volumes.
Member banks should process the inward transactions received throughNACHplatform at par
with the processing of transactions received through EcS system of RBl.
