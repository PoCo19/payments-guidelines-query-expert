2. Usage of the above mentioned return reason is causing inconvenience to the customers and
fortheGovernment departments.
3. Various circulars pertaining to dormant and inoperative accounts mainly deal with the
subject of precautions to be taken at the time of debiting such accounts.There are no
specific restrictions on crediting such accounts.
4.NACH application has got common list of return reason codes therefore the abovereasons
appearin the list onlytobeused fordebit products. Thesameare not applicablefor credits.
5. Member banks are advised to take necessary steps not to return the transactions on
account of the above reasons.
