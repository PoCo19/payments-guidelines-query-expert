MigrationofNACHdebit(156)formattoACHdebit(306)Legacyformat
Reference may be taken from Circular No. 36 on "Migration of NACH debit (156) format to
ACH debit (306) Legacy format dated November 29, 2018 wherein we had migrated 693
banks. After further analysing the data we are migrating another set of 7 banks to ACH Debit
306fromDecember12,2018.Listof7banksisannexedinAnnexure-Iandthetotal listof
700 banks is annexed in Annexure-ll.
Sponsorbanksareadvised nottoraisethetransactions inNACHDebit (156)formatonthese
destination banks and intimate their respective corporates.Goingforwardall the transactions
need to be raised in ACH debit Legacy (306) format for these banks. Accordingly, we will
disablethesedestinationbanksfromNACHDebit(156)fcrmatfromDecember12,2018
Member banks are advised to take note and make necessary arrangements accordingly.
