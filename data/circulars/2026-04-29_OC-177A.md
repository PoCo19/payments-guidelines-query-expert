NPCI/UPI/OC-177A/2026-27 — 29th April 2026

To, All UPI Members

Dear Sir/Madam,

**Subject: Addendum to UPI Global Acceptance Limits and Country Code Enablement**

With reference to NPCI/UPI/OC-177/2023-24, dated 31st October 2023, UPI Global transaction limits along with the list of Country/Currency code to be enabled for UPI Global Acceptance were introduced. To expand the reach of UPI Global Acceptance, the Limits and List of Countries for Global Acceptance are being revised as below. All members are requested to note the following revisions and implement them as needed.

### 1. UPI Global Acceptance Limits

The per transaction and per day limit for UPI Global Acceptance in Europe (P2M) are being increased to ₹2 lakh. Each member bank may set their own limits, subject to the overall applicable limits, specified herein. For regions other than Europe, UPI Global Acceptance transaction limits will continue to be governed by NPCI/UPI/OC-177/2023-24.

### 2. Addition of New Countries for UPI Global Acceptance

The list of countries enabled for UPI Global Acceptance is also being updated. Members may enable the codes of the newly added countries and currencies as needed. Please refer Annexure I for consolidated list of country and currency codes and find below the new additions,

| Sr. No. | Country | Country Code | Transaction Currency Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bahrain | BH | BHD |
| 2 | Cambodia | KH | KHR |
| 3 | Canada | CA | CAD |
| 4 | Kuwait | KW | KWD |
| 5 | New Zealand | NZ | NZD |
| 6 | Oman | OM | OMR |
| 7 | South Africa | ZA | ZAR |
| 8 | Switzerland | CH | CHF |

Issuer Banks, PSP Banks and UPI Applications are requested to take note of the above revisions, which shall come into effect from 31st May 2026.

Further, it was also incumbent upon the banks to abide by the extant and future RBI, FEMA and other relevant guidelines.

Yours Sincerely,

SD/-

Sourabh Tomar

Deputy Chief UPI Product
