NPCI/UPI/2023-24/0C/ 76A 12‘ March 2024

To

All Members of UPI, Dear Madan/Sir,

Subject: Revision in transaction limits based on merchant & transaction types - Addendum to OC76

NPCI vide circular NPCI/UPI/2019-20/0C76 dated 31st October 2019, had released guidelines regarding transaction limits based on merchant segment and transaction types.

Considering the constantly expanding UPI merchant ecosystem and the impending risks which can arise, it is advised that the following guidelines to be taken note of and implemented by respective entities.

Cae Circular Reference & as Payer PSP shall ensure P2P Intent based NPCI/UPI/2019-20/OC/76A 1 transactions (Initiation mode ‘04’ and ‘05’) shall be : (New) disallowed.

Payee PSP shall ensure Intent based transactions NPCI/UPI/2019-20/0C/76 2 (Initiation mode ‘04’) shall be disallowed for all (Modification) ‘Offline’ non-verified merchants.

QR share & Pay shall have a limit of INR 2000/- for NPCI/UPI/2019-20/OC/76 3 all P2P transactions (Payer PSP to ensure that UPI (Existing — No change) app to identify the same). g 9 QR share & Pay shall have a limit of INR 2000/- for NPCI/UPI/2019-20/0C/76 4 all P2M (non-verified offline merchants) and Payer (Existing - No change) PSP to ensure that Payer UPI app identify the same.

Wallet load, Gift/Prepaid cards (MCC 6540) shall NPCI/UPI/2019-20/0C/76 continue to be disallowed on ‘collect’ and QR mode (Existing - No change) payments as per the extant guidelines. 9 9 All stakeholders to take cognizance of the above guidelines and ensure compliance effective 1% April 2024.

Yours sincerely,

Sd/-

Viswanath Krishnamurthy Chief Risk Officer
