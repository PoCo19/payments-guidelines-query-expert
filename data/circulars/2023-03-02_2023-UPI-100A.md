NPCI/UPI/OC-100A/2022-2023 (Addendum) Daie: 02/03/2023

To,

All Member Banks, PSP, TPAPs, Unified Payment Interface (UPI)

Dear Sir/Madam,

Sub: Addendum to “Mandatory Brand Guidelines for BHIM UPI” As the UPI ecosystem is growing towards 300 million transactions per day, it is essential to educate users in order to maintain their faith in UPI. As new users are added to UPI on a daily basis, an awareness video will assist users, particularly those with limited knowledge of smartphone usage, to start using the app safely.

Ali UPi Apps (Bank Apps & TPAPs) should ensure to have the following:

1. When user installs and opens a UPI App, video on “How to use UPI safely” should be availabie on the first screen of the App in a prominent place. In case playing the video in ihe App is not feasible, link to the video may be provided.

2. There should be a provision io change the language of the Video.

3. These videos should be dispteyed to both existing and new users. Member Banks, PSPs, TPAPs are hereby requested to take note of the above and ensure compliance before 315 March 2023.

Your Faithfully,

SD/-

Kunal Kalawatia Chief of Products
