Subject: Enhancements in UPI Single Block Multiple Debits (UPI Reserve Pay)

With reference to UPI operating circular NPCI/UPI/OC. No 200/2024-25 dated July 31, 2024, NPCI has introduced “UPI Single Block and Multiple Debits (henceforth to be referred as UP! Reserve Pay)”. UPI Reserve Pay feature facilitates the customer to block the funds in the account for multiple debits which can be initiated by the customer on the merchant's platform, till the reserved funds gets exhausted or the block has been revoked or expired.

To further enrich the offerings to customers and merchants, this addendum aims to include applicability of UPI Reserve Pay to all UPI-permitted source of funds (including SA, CA, OD, RuPay Credit Card, pre-sanctioned Credit lines, etc.) for merchant segments. All prior released NPCI guidelines for UP! Reserve Pay shall continue to be applicable.

In addition to existing roles, responsibilities, and regulatory guidelines, members shall ensure adherence to the following requirements:

General guidelines applicable to all participating members:

1. Members shall adhere to RBI guidelines on ‘Harmonization of Turn Around Time (TAT) and customer compensation for failed transactions using authorized Payment Systems’ dated September 20, 2019, as amended from time to time.

2. Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) functionality shall be available for all such transactions.

3. Reconciliation process shall be as per the existing UP! guidelines. Purpose code 77 in the existing UPI raw file and a line item in the Net Settlement Report shall be available to identify these transactions and settlements respectively.

4. The customer shall be clearly advised the terms and conditions for UPI Reserve Pay by the merchant/

PA/ Acquirer before reservation of the funds.

5. The extant pricing of RuPay Credit Cards on UPI and pre-sanctioned Credit Line on UPI shall apply to

successfully executed UPI! Reserve Pay financial transactions as applicable.

Issuer Banks shall ensure

1. The reserve amount details is shown to the customer in the statement and other channels as applicable in due course.

2. Mandatory notifications in the form or SMS or email or other channels for block creation, modification, debit, revoke and expiry.

3. Only utilized amount debited after actual purchase to be considered for bill generation as applicable for credit accounts on UPI.

4. One mobile number (assumed as one customer) is allowed to create only one block at a time for the particular merchant.
