NPCI/UPI/OC-100B/2026-27 5" June 2026

To,

All Member Banks, PSPs, Unified Payment Interface (UPI)

Dear Sir / Madam,

Subject: Addendum to “Mandatory Brand Guidelines for BHIM UPI Merchant QR (Quick Response)”

With reference to NPCI circulars NPCI/UPI/OC-100/2020-21, NPCI/UPI/OC-100A/2022-23 and NPCI/UPI/OC-190/2023-24, this circular outlines revised BHIM UPI Merchant QR Brand Guidelines to standardise the customer experience while scanning merchant QR. The updated brand guidelines are enclosed in Annexure A of this circular and shall be applicable in accordance with the timelines specified below:

1. All new Merchant QR codes issued after 1% December 2026 shall mandatorily be compliant with the updated BHIM UPI Merchant QR Brand Guidelines.

2. All existing Merchant QR codes which are being replaced from 1* December 2026 shall mandatorily be compliant with the updated BHIM UPI Merchant QR Brand Guidelines.

3. QR code designs are required to be approved by NPCI before implementation (approval process is enclosed in Annexure B).

Acquiring Banks/ PSPs are hereby requested to take note of the above and communicate it to relevant stakeholders and their partners to ensure timely compliance.

Your Sincerely,

SD/-

Sourabh Tomar Deputy Chief UPI Product

### Annexure B

Design Approval Process:

1. Mandatory Approval Requirement: All UP! QR Code designs shall obtain prior approval from NPCI before implementation or deployment.

2. Submission Process: Approval requests, along with the proposed QR code design shall be submitted to: upi.marketing@npci.org.in | upi.compliance@npci.org.in

3. Turnaround Time : NPC] shall review and communicate its approval/ observations within 5 working days of receiving the submission.
