Members - Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Members — NETC FASTag Members — RuPay Auto-replenishment of NETC FASTag and RuPay NCMC with UPI Autopay Dear Madam / Sir, UPI Autopay covers recurring needs such as recurring bill payments, OTT subscriptions, loan EMI payments, and other scenarios where periodicity of payment is well-defined.

To further enhance customer experience in various use cases which are recurring in nature but without any fixed periodicity,

1. Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has announced to facilitate e-mandate framework for auto-replenishment of balances in NETC FASTag and RuPay National Common Mobility Card (NCMC) in “Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies” dated 07" June 2024.

2. Further, it was directed:

- a) To include auto-replenishment of NETC FASTag and RuPay NCMC, as and when the balance falls below a threshold set by the customer, under the e- mandate framework.

- b) Exemption from 24-Hours prior Pre-Debit Notification (PDN) for auto- replenishment of NETC FASTag and RuPay NCMC.
