NPCI/UPI/OC No. 193B/2024-25 Dec 04, 2024

To,

All Members of Unified Payments Interface (UPI)

Sub: Addendum to OC-193 & OC-193A Compliance to UPI technical specifications - TRAN ID

Reference may be taken from UPI operating circular vide reference NPCI/UPI/OC No.

193/2023-24 dated 28" Mar 2024 and NPCI/UPI/OC 193A/2024-25 dated 10 Sep 2024. It was advised that all the participating entities should ensure that duplicate TRAN IDs should be completely eliminated. It has been observed that while the volume of such duplicate TRAN IDs have come down, however, a few of the participating entities are continuing to send the duplicate TRAN IDs. In this regard the following shall be the process:

1. NPCI on best effort basis shall settle the duplicate transactions with TRAN IDs.

2. Settlement for such transactions will be done through adjustment entry.

3. Amount will reflect in NET ADJUSTED AMOUNT line item in NTSL.

4. The details of duplicate TRAN ID are made available in adjustment report, details are as follows, | Adjtype | ispute Flag | _——ReasonCode__| This shall be effective from Dec-05-2024.

Compliance and liability shift quidelines as mentioned in OC-193 & 193A will remain unchanged.

Participating entities must take all measures to immediately fix the issue of duplicate TRAN IDs. Member banks to take note of the above and align all the settlement & reconciliation process accordingly. Please disseminate the Information contained herein to the concerned officials.

With m regards 4 GM Chief — Customer Success
