NPCI/UPI/OC No. 187/2023-2024 26" December 2023 (Updated as on 23 February 2026*)

To,

All Members of UPI - Banks, PSP's & Third-Party Applications Dear Sir/ Madam

Subject: UPI Reporting requirements (Acquiring entities & UPI Apps) The impressive growth of the UP! platform in becoming the preferred digital payment choice for merchants is exemplified by its rise from contributing 45% to Person to Merchant (P2M) transactions in May 2021 to 59% in Nov 2023 (Source: NPCI data). This success can be attributed to UPI's simplicity, real-time transactions, interoperability, and cost-effectiveness. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) highlights the deployment of over 308 million UP! QR codes for P2M payments, underlining the importance of maintaining stability and efficiency to uphold strong success rates.

In the Steering Committee Meeting dated 3 August 2023 and subsequent working group discussion, a consensus was reached on the necessity for centralized monitoring of UPI success rates. Hence proposed reporting guidelines for merchant transaction data monthly submissions encompassing failure reasons, active merchants, payment modes, UDIR etc. are issued. By collecting the data, collaborative efforts can be fostered for root cause analysis and the development of solutions aimed at enhancing overall success rates.

The following guidelines are issued to UPI Members for the enhancement of customer experience that all Acquirers, shall submit the data as per the data submission framework outlined in Annexure-1 from January 2024 onwards.

UPI Members are advised to ensure that the contents of this Circular are promptly cascaded to their respective sub-members and other concerned entities for necessary compliance

Yours Sincerely,

SD/-

Sohini Rajola Executive Director - Growth *Obligations of non-UPI participants have been aligned to the corresponding UPI member for ensuring compliance
