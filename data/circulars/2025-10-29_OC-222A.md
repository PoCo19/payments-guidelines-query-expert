NPC1/2025-26/UPI/222A Oct 29, 2025

To,

All members of Unified Payments Interface (UPI)

Subject: Addendum to Segregation of UPI Settlement Cycles for Auth and Dispute transactions.

We refer to the UP! URCS Operational Circular (OC) issued vide reference NPCI/2025- 26/UPI/222 dated 12th September 2025, wherein it was communicated that the segregation of AUTH settlements and Dispute transactions shall be executed through the 11% (DC1) and 12% (DC2) settlement cycles, effective 3° November 2025.

We hereby inform all members that the implementation timelines have been rescheduled from 3°? November 2025 to 15" December 2025, considering ongoing rollouts, feedback from members and to ensure operational readiness across stakeholders.

Effective from 15" December 2025, adjustment reports will be shared only three times a day (refer Annexure — 1 for details).

Please note that starting 15" December 2025, adjustment reports for settlement cycles 1 to 10 will be discontinued, as adjustments will now be processed only in the 11% (DC1) and 12% (DC2) settlement cycles.

Member banks are advised to take a note of the above and disseminate the information contained herein to the officials concerned.

Yours Faithfully,

SD/-

Giridhar GM Chief - Customer Success
