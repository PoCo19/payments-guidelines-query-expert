NPCI/2026-27/PD/001 7" May 2026

To,

All Members participating in various NPCI Products

Sub: Initiation of Self-Attestation of Members/Participants for F.Y. 2026-27

Dear Sir/Madam,

We refer to circular no. NPCI/2022-23/OC/PD/001 dated 7 September 2022 and NPCI/2023- 24/PD/005 dated 28th December 2023 on adherence to NPCI guidelines by members and Sell- Attestation. We also refer to the advisory no. NPCI/2025-26/PD/002 dated 24th September 2025 on submission of Self-Attestation by Independent Control Functions of the members.

Self-Attestation is a process where members need to submit status of compliance with respect to NPCI guidelines. The Self-Attestation checklist is based on documents such as Procedural Guidelines, Operating Circulars, Technical Specification Documents issued by NPCI from time to time. Members need to submit Self Attestation through NPCI's PCOMP portal. This activity will be on annual basis / as and when required This is to inform you that the directives for FY. 2026-27 are updated on the PCOMP portal for Self-Attestation by members.

Action for members/participants:

1. All members need to ensure completion of the Self-Attestation process for F.Y. 2026-27 by 31% August 2026 for all directives assigned to them.

2. In case of the directives issued under ‘General’ tab, the members need to complete the Self-Attestation as per the date specified on the PCOMP portal for the respective directives.

Please note, any non-adherence to the timelines specified for completion of Self-Attestation process and non-compliance observed will be liable for appropriate actions and may further lead to penai actions by Surveillance and Enforcement Team (S&E) as per circulars referred above.

Please make a note of the above information and disseminate the same to the concerned officials.

Yours Sincerely,

SD/-

Amit Gawde In charge-Business Compliance
