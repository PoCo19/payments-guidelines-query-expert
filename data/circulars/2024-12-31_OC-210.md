NPCI/UPI/(OC210/2024-25 31% December 2024

To,

All Members of UPI Dear Madam/Sir, Guidelines for volume cap for Third Party App Providers (TPAPs) in UPI We invite reference to our earlier circulars NPCI/UPI/OC-97/2020-2021 dated 5" November 2020 and NPCI/UPI/OC159/2022-2023 dated 24 December 2022 on “Guidelines for volume cap for Third Party App Providers (TPAPs) in UPI”.

Considering various factors, the timeline for compliance of existing TPAPs who are exceeding the volume cap, is extended by two (2) years i.e. till December 31, 2026.

Yours sincerely,

SD/-

Kunal Kalawatia Chief of Products
