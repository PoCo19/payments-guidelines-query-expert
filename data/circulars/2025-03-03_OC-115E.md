NPCI/UPI/OC-115E/2024-25 3" March 2025

To,

UP! Member Banks, PSPs, and Third-Party App Providers

Subject: Addendum to circular NPCI/UPI/OC-115/2021-22 on the Numeric UPI ID resolution

This has reference to UPi OC NPCI/UPI/OC-115/2021-2022 dated 20th July 2021 on ‘Rollout of “Numeric UPI ID Mapper’ to enable “UPI Number’, UPI Steering Committee Meeting held on 16" July 2024 and multipie Working Group discussions for UP] Number aimed at enhancing interoperability and user convenience in UPI number-based UPI payments.

Following are the additional guidelines for UP] Number:

1. The Banks, PSP App shall use the Mobile Number Revocation List/Digital Intelligence Platform (MNRL/DIP) and update their database accordingly at regular intervals, at least on weekly basis. The activity of recycled or the churned mobile numbers reflecting correctly in the bank and PSP/TPAP databases will reduce the chances of errors due to churned mobile numbers.

2. UPIApp shall obtain explicit user consent with clear opt-out option (by default checked out, and user needs to click on opt-in) for seeding or porting UPI Number.

- a) UPI App shall ensure clear, non-intrusive communication without misleading/forceful messaging. Consent shail not be taken before or during a transaction in any condition.

- b) Seeding or porting communications of UPI number shall exclude miscommunication (For example - the user will stop receiving the money using UPI without taking an action on seeding the number in mapper)

in case of NPC! mapper response time is not as per requirements, then the PSP app can resolve the

number locally, subject to the PSP App report such occurrences on monthly basis to NPCI as listed in

### Annexure A

All members are advised to comply before 31% March 2025.

Yours sincerely,

SD/-

Kunal Kalawatia

Chief of Products
