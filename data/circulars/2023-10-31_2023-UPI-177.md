NPCI/UPI/OC - 177/2023-24 31 October 2023

To,

All Members of the Unified Payments Interface (UPI)

Dear Sir/Madam,

Subject:

1. Revision of UPI Global Acceptance Limits

2. Launch of International Mobile Application Payment Acceptance through UPI QR code in India

3. Currency and Country code enablement for UPI Global Acceptance Transactions

1. Revision for UPI Global Acceptance Limits The per-transaction limit for UPI Global to be the same as that of UPI, and UPI Global transactions to be included in the per-day limit of UPI. For existing limits refer to NPCI/UPI/OC No.120/2021 dated 24* September 2021 (this is subjected to change from time to time).

Member Banks (Payer PSP and Remitter) and UPI Application are requested to take note of the above and undertake required changes. Members are requested to make the necessary changes by 30" November 2023.

2. Launch of International Mobile Application Payment Acceptance through UPI QR code in India In order to promote UP! payment platform globally, International Mobile Application Payment Acceptance through UPI QR code in India has been introduced. This will enable Indian Merchanis to accept payments from international customers through their International Mobile Application by scanning UP! QR codes in India.

In online transaction request, purpose code of these transactions will be “60”.

Member Banks (Payee PSP) and Merchants are requested to take note of the above enhancement and undertake required changes. Members are requested to make the necessary changes by 31% December 2023.

3. Currency/Country code enablement for UP! Global Acceptance Transactions Members were informed that International Merchant Payments through UPI (UPI Global) is live for Singapore, UAE and Bhutan through below circulars: JOOIA, The Capitai, B Wing, 10" Floor, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra CE), Mumbai 400 057- T: +91 22 40009100 F: +91 22 40009101 contact@npci.org.in www.npci.org.in CIN: U74990MH2008NPL189067
