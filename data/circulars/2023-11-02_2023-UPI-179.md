NPCI/UPUOC No.179/2023-24 2"4 November 2023

To,

All Members, Unified Paymenis Interface (UPI) Dear Madan/Sir,

Subject: Customer awareness and engagement for UPI LITE Enablement and Top-up

This is in reference to NPCI circular NPC] /UPI/OC No.138/2021-22, dated 16 March 2022 with subject ‘Introduction of “On-Device wallet’ — UPI LITE for Small Value Transactions’ and NPCI/UPI/OC No.169/2023-24, dated 31% August 2023 with subject ‘Enhancement in UPI LITE transaction limit to 500’, whereby guidelines to operationalize UP! LITE small value transactions have been introduced.

The objective of introducing UPI LITE was to reduce the need for infrastructure scale up at bank end to cater the growing volumes of UPI small value transactions. UP] LITE is fast and seamless, as UPI PIN is not required to complete the transaction. In addition, UPI] LITE reduces the demand of availability of the issuer infrastructure for completing the transaction and hence provides better success rates. To grow UPI LITE and further enhance the customer experience, UPI Apps shall carry out the below actions:

1. For customers who have not enabled UPI LITE — UPI apps must create customer awareness by highlighting the faster checkout experience. UPI Apps shail send a pop-up/ notification with call to action to enable UP] LITE, when customer performs UP! transaction with PIN below 500. The notification shall be sent after the UPI transaction and there shall not be any change in the customer experience for the UPI transaction.

2. For new UPI customer onboarding, UPI apps shall define journey for enabling UPI LITE also during UPI onboarding with consent from the user.

3. Post UPI LITE enablement, UPI LITE shall be the default instrument for transactions value below 500.

4. Top-up notifications/ pop-up with call to action shall be given to the customer, if UPI LITE balance goes below 200. Pop-up/ notifications shall be given in below ways:

- a) Pop-up/ notification when user opens the App and the UPI LITE balance is below 2200.

- b) After UPI transaction, if UPI LITE wallet balance is below 200 then pop-up / notification shall be given to the customer.
