NPCI/UPI/OC-209/2024 October 25, 2024

To,

All UPI Members — Banks, PSPs and 123Pay Service Providers, Dear Madam / Sir,

Subject: Guidelines on UPI features for UPI 123Pay UPI 123Pay enables smart phone and feature phone users to digitally undertake a host of transactions based on four approaches, including voice payment based via IVR number, proximity sound-based payments, missed call-based payment approach, app-based scan and pay on feature phone. NPCI developed Server-Side Common Library (SSCL) which is hosted at NPCI end for enabling 123Pay transactions.

A reference is invited to press release issued by Reserve Bank of India (RBI), dated 9° October 2024 with Subject ‘Statement on Development and regulatory Policies’ whereby RBI has decided to increase per transaction limit from %5,000 to 10,000 for UPI 123Pay.

1. Members shall increase the per transaction limit to 10,000 from %5,000 with immediate effect.

2. Members shall implement Onboarding with Aadhar OTP in UPI 123Pay. Details as defined in circular NPCI/UPI/OC-116/2021-22 and NPCI/UPI/OC-116A/2021-22 to be followed.

3. Members shall collectively identify and tag UPI 123Pay transactions as mentioned in

### Annexure 1. :

4. Members shall implement UPI Number functionality through integration with UPI numeric ID Mapper. Details as defined in circular NPCI/UPI/OC-115/2021-22 to be followed.

5. New members shall comply to above mentioned guidelines for go live on UPI 123Pay services.

Compliance Action: All live 123Pay members are advised to comply before 1°t January 2025.

Yours sincerely,

SD/-

Kunal Kalawatia Chief of Products
