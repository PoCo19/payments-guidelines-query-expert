NPCI/UPI/OC No. 229/2025-2026 12° November 2025

To,

All Members of Unified Payment Interface (UPI)

Subject: Implementation of ISD-GSTIN (Input Service distributor) option in URCS back-office system.

Reference may be taken from the notification No. 16/2024-Central Tax dated 6' August 2024, regarding the Input Service Distributor (ISD) which is effective from 18 April 2025.

In alignment with this requirement, NPCI has invited few member banks to assess the scope and system enhancements required. Based on the feedback received, a solution has been designed and presented during a workshop on 24th July 2025, which was unanimously accepted by participating banks. The necessary developments have since been completed, and the back-office system is now ready for deployment.

An ISD/GSTIN update option is be made available in the URCS back-office system, allowing members to configure their preferred reporting structure for GSTIN or ISD GSTIN.

These updates will be reflected in the monthly GST reports accordingly.

Timeline for ISD-GSTIN Updates: ISD-GSTIN updates must be completed before 20th of each month to ensure the changes are reflected in the same month’s GST reports (Example: If the ISD is updated on 20 November 2025, the changes will be reflected in the November GST reports, which will be generated in the first week of December 2025).

Note: All banks’ GSTIN details are already configured in the URCS system at the time of onboarding the bank in URCS. Members are advised to use the new update option only if opting for ISD. Otherwise, the existing GSTIN-based reporting will continue unchanged Go-Live Date: The ISD-GSTIN update functionality will be available in URCS effective 17 November 2025.

### Annexure -A

Procedure for Updating ISD-GSTIN in the Back Office System

1. Login and Navigation To begin, the user must iog in to the back-office system. From the home page, select the "Update ISD-GSTIN" option in the main menu. The system will display the definition of ISD details and prompt for confirmation. Click “Click to Proceed” to continue.

2. Selecting Report Type and Updating ISD After proceeding, the system will present options with two types of report: Payable and Receivable. Each option includes two dropdowns: GSTIN and ISD. The user should select the appropriate values based on the requirement and submit the request.

Once submitted, the request moves from the Maker Tray to the Checker Tray.

The checker is responsible for reviewing and verifying the details entered by the maker. Upon approval, the system updates the ISD information and generates the monthly GST reports accordingly.

3. Timeline for ISD-GSTIN Updates A) |SD-GSTIN updates must be completed on or before the 20th of each month to ensure the changes are reflected in the same month's GST reports.

Example: If the ISD is updated on 20 November 2025, the changes will be reflected in the November GST reports, which will be generated in the first week of December 2025.

B) Updates made after the 20th will take effect from the following month.

Example: If the ISD is updated after 20‘ November 2025, the effect will be applied starting GST reports of Dec 2025 which will be provide in the first week of Jan 2026.

Note: ISD is applicable for all type of income and charges, i.e. Switching Fee, Interchange Fee, PSP Fee and any other Fee as applicable.
